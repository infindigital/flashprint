About mandatory safety signage

Mandatory signs tell people what they must do: wear eye protection, use the handrail, keep a fire door shut, wash hands before returning to work. They use a white pictogram on a blue circle and form their own class within safety signage. We produce mandatory safety signage in Dubai for factories, kitchens, warehouses, labs, offices and public buildings, alongside the statutory notices a client has been asked to display.

Statutory notices differ in origin but are handled the same way. Their wording, layout and sometimes size or position come from an authority, landlord, insurer or consultant, and our role is to reproduce what that document requires, exactly. We never paraphrase or 'improve' mandated wording, and we do not state that a sign meets a regulation; that judgement stays with your HSE officer, consultant or the authority.

Symbols follow the set you specify, typically ISO 7010-style pictograms or a set issued by your organisation. Where a colour reference is given for safety blue, we match it on the chosen material and show it on the proof. Orders are managed as schedules, so a new building can receive every mandatory sign and notice in one coordinated batch.

Mandatory safety signage FAQs

What makes a sign 'mandatory'?

In safety sign conventions, a mandatory sign instructs people to take a specific action, such as wearing a helmet or washing hands, and uses a white symbol on a blue circle. That sets it apart from prohibition (red), warning (yellow) and safe-condition (green) signs. Which mandatory signs your premises need is for your HSE officer, consultant or the authority to decide.

Will your signs pass an inspection?

We cannot promise that. Whether signage satisfies an inspector depends on the requirement, the location and how the signs are placed, which is the responsibility of your HSE officer, consultant or facility manager. What we do is reproduce the specification you supply accurately and give you proofs to check against it before anything is produced.

Can you print notices that an authority has given us?

Yes. Send the notice as issued, or the document that defines its wording and format, and we will reproduce it on the material and size you need. Where the source is a low-quality scan, we rebuild it as clean artwork and proof it so your team can compare it line by line with the original.

Do you supply mandatory signs for commercial kitchens?

Yes. Hand-washing, protective clothing, keep-door-closed and similar instructions are common in commercial kitchens. They are usually printed on PVC or vinyl with a wipe-clean laminate so they tolerate regular cleaning. Your food safety or HSE lead decides which signs are needed and where; we produce them to that list.

How should we order signs for a new building?

Build a sign schedule with one line per sign: reference, wording, symbol, size, material, quantity, floor and location. We quote against it, return proofs in the same order and pack signs by floor or area. That keeps installation straightforward and gives you a record for future replacements.
