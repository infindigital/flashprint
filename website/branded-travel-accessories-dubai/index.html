About branded travel accessories

Branded travel accessories in Dubai are well suited to a city that sits between continents. Sales teams on roadshows, consultants flying between Gulf offices, and clients who travel often all use a passport cover, a luggage tag or a travel adapter on every trip, which keeps your logo moving through airports and hotels.

Passport covers and travel wallets hold a passport, boarding pass and cards together, with RFID-blocking layers on selected models. Luggage tags range from PVC and printed plastic to PU leather with a privacy flap over the address card. Universal travel adapters handle UK, European, US and Australian sockets with USB charging ports built in. Cable kits pack USB-C and Lightning leads, adapters and a SIM tool in a slim case, and toiletry pouches cover the rest of the bag.

Branding is usually discreet. A deboss on a PU passport cover, a small pad print on an adapter or a woven label on a pouch looks like part of the product rather than a promotion, which is what makes people keep using it.

Branded travel accessories FAQs

Which countries do universal travel adapters cover?

Most universal adapters include pins for UK, European, US and Australian sockets, which covers the majority of business destinations. Some regions use other socket types, so tell us where your team travels and we will confirm coverage for the adapter you choose. Many models also include USB ports for charging devices directly.

Can luggage tags be personalised with names?

Yes. Tags can carry individual names printed on the tag face or on the address card, set from a spreadsheet. PU leather tags with a privacy flap keep the address hidden while showing your logo on the outside. We proof the name list before production so spellings can be checked.

Are power banks allowed in a travel kit?

Power banks are generally allowed in cabin baggage but not in checked luggage, and airlines apply limits by capacity. If your kit includes one, choose a capacity within common airline limits and advise recipients to carry it in hand luggage. We can confirm the capacity rating for the model you select.

Can travel items be packed as a set?

Yes. A typical set combines a passport cover, luggage tag and cable kit in a branded pouch, or adds a travel adapter and earbuds in a gift box. Items are branded with the method suited to each material and proofed together. Tell us how the kits will be delivered so they are packed accordingly.
