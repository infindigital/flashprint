Apparel printing in Dubai covers everything from 50 event t-shirts to a full staff uniform programme for a clinic, hotel or contractor. The garment, the fabric and the decoration method have to agree with each other, because a print that looks right on cotton can crack on stretch polyester or lift from a nylon vest.

We brand t-shirts, polo shirts, work shirts, hoodies, medical scrubs, doctor coats and hi-visibility safety vests in the sizes and colours you need. Tell us who will wear the garments, where and how often they will be washed, and we will recommend a method, place your logo on a proof and confirm garment stock before production starts.

Printing and embroidery methods explained

Each method has a sweet spot. The choice depends on the fabric, the number of colours, the quantity and the feel you want on the finished garment.

  • Screen printing: ink pushed through a mesh screen, one screen per colour. Strongest on larger runs of simple spot-colour designs on cotton.
  • DTF (direct to film): the design is printed onto film and heat-pressed onto the garment. Handles full colour and fine detail on cotton, polyester and blends without screen set-up.
  • Heat transfer vinyl: shapes cut from coloured vinyl and pressed on. Suits names, numbers and one-colour logos, including reflective and metallic vinyl.
  • Embroidery: the logo is stitched into the fabric for a textured, hard-wearing finish on polos, shirts, caps, jackets and coats.
  • Sublimation: dye bonds into white or light polyester fibres, so the print has no surface feel. Used for sports jerseys and all-over prints; not suitable for cotton.

Fabrics, fits and sizes

Cotton, typically 160–220 gsm for t-shirts, is soft and breathable and takes screen print and DTF well. Polyester and performance fabrics wick moisture and suit outdoor and sports use, where DTF or sublimation performs better than heavy plastisol ink. Poly-cotton blends balance comfort with shape retention and are common for polos and workwear.

Most ranges run from XS to 3XL, with 4XL and 5XL in selected styles, and many garments come in ladies', unisex and children's fits. For uniform orders, collect a size list per person and, where possible, try a size set before the full order. Stock colours and sizes are confirmed in your quote.

T-shirts, uniforms or safety vests: choosing the right service

T-shirt printing is for promotional and event shirts, merchandise and team tees, where the design, the head count and the event date drive the decisions. Uniform printing is about daily workwear such as polos, shirts, scrubs and coats, where consistent logo placement, staff sizing and matching reorders matter most.

Safety vest printing covers hi-vis vests for sites, warehouses and events. The print has to sit on thin polyester or mesh around the reflective tape without covering it, and role titles need to read clearly from a distance. Hoodies, polos and medical wear are available as part of any of these orders.

FAQs

Apparel & Fabric Printing FAQs

Which printing method is best for our design?

It depends on the design and the order. Simple one- to three-colour logos on cotton in larger quantities suit screen printing. Full-colour artwork, photos or small runs suit DTF. Names and numbers are usually heat transfer vinyl, and polos, coats and caps are normally embroidered. Send your artwork and quantities, and we will recommend a method and explain the trade-offs.

Can you print on garments we supply?

Often, yes. We check the fabric content, the care label and any existing decoration first, because some fabrics, coatings and waterproof finishes do not accept heat transfers or dense embroidery well. Supply a spare garment where possible so the method can be confirmed before your full batch is decorated.

How should printed and embroidered garments be washed?

Turn printed garments inside out, wash cool on a gentle cycle and avoid tumble drying on high heat. Do not iron directly over a print; iron on the reverse instead. Embroidered garments cope well with regular washing but should not be bleached. Always follow the garment's own care label, as fabric limits apply first.

Do you help with sizing for staff orders?

Yes. We share the size chart for the chosen garment, and for larger teams we recommend a size set so staff can try before ordering. Send the final list as a spreadsheet with name, garment, size and any personalisation, and we can pack the order per person or per department.
