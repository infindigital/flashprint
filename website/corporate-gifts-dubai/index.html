Main page in Apparel & Fabric Printing

Promotional gifts in Dubai tend to follow the calendar: year-end client thank-yous, Ramadan and Eid hampers, new-joiner welcome kits, conference giveaways and National Day packs. We brand the items people actually use, so your logo stays on a desk, a head or a keyring long after the event has ended.

Every item has its own decoration method. A ceramic mug takes a full-wrap sublimation print, a PU diary is debossed, a cap is embroidered and a metal USB drive is laser engraved. We match the method to the item, check your logo against it and send a proof before anything is produced. Branded t-shirts can be added through our apparel team if you want something wearable in the mix, and printed boxes turn loose items into a finished gift set.

The range now runs well past the classic desk gift. Insulated water bottles and tumblers, cotton and jute bags, magnetic power banks, portable speakers, lanyards with badge reels and bamboo, cork and RPET ranges all sit alongside the mugs and diaries, and any of them can be combined into one boxed set.

Branding methods matched to each item

The right method depends on the surface, the number of colours in the logo and how the item will be handled. Choosing well at the start avoids a fuzzy edge on a small logo or a print that looks stretched on a curved surface.

  • Sublimation: full-colour, edge-to-edge images on polymer-coated white or two-tone ceramic mugs
  • Pad printing: one or two spot colours on curved plastic items such as USB casings, pens and keychains
  • Laser engraving: permanent, tone-on-tone marking on metal, anodised aluminium, wood and bamboo
  • Debossing and hot foil: a pressed logo on PU leather or linen diary covers, plain or filled with gold, silver or colour foil
  • Embroidery: stitched logos on caps, polos and bags, sewn from a digitised version of your artwork

Drinkware, bags and technology gifts

Reusable drinkware is the category that gets used daily in this climate. Double-wall stainless flasks and tumblers keep a drink cold through a Dubai afternoon and take a laser-engraved logo that survives washing, while tritan sports bottles and glass bottles with bamboo collars cover gyms and meeting rooms.

Bags carry your logo past the person you gave it to. Cotton and canvas totes, jute shoppers, non-woven carriers and RPET backpacks all print or embroider well, and padded laptop bags and pouches suit staff kits where a discreet woven label works better than a large print. Technology gifts cover magnetic and cabled power banks, portable Bluetooth speakers, wireless chargers and cable sets, branded by UV print, engraving or a light-up logo.

  • Vacuum flasks, tumblers, sports bottles and glass bottles from around 350 ml to 1 litre
  • Cotton, canvas, jute, non-woven and RPET bags, plus backpacks and laptop bags
  • Power banks from 5,000 to 20,000 mAh, wireless chargers and cable sets
  • Portable Bluetooth speakers in bamboo, fabric-wrapped and retro styles
  • Lanyards with badge reels, card holders and safety breakaways for events and staff ID
  • Bamboo, cork, RPET and recycled-paper ranges for sustainability-led gifting

Gift sets, packaging and presentation

A single mug in a plain carton works for a staff kitchen. For clients, most buyers combine two or three items, such as a diary, a pen and a USB drive, in a printed or foil-stamped box with a fitted insert. We can also print sleeves, belly bands, swing tags and thank-you cards so the whole set carries the same colours.

Packaging is quoted alongside the items. Tell us whether the sets will be hand-delivered, couriered or handed out from an exhibition stand, because that decides how much protection each box needs and whether items should be pre-assembled or packed in bulk cartons.

Planning a bulk corporate order

Large orders run smoothly when the brief is complete from day one. Share the item list, quantities per item, logo files and colour references, plus the date the gifts must be in hand. Timelines depend on quantity, decoration method and stock of the colours you choose, so we confirm the schedule with your quote before production.

For mixed orders, such as 300 mugs, 150 diaries and 100 caps, we prepare one proof sheet showing every item with its logo size and position. Once you approve it, each batch is produced, checked and packed to your instructions.

  • Vector logo (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) and Pantone references where you have them
  • Item list with product colours and quantities per item
  • Personalisation, such as individual names on diaries or keychains, in a spreadsheet
  • Packing instructions: bulk cartons, individual boxes or assembled gift sets
  • Required date and delivery or collection details

FAQs

Corporate Gifts & Promotional Items FAQs

Which corporate gift items can you brand?

The core range covers mugs, caps, diaries, USB flash drives, keychains, insulated water bottles, cotton and jute bags, power banks, Bluetooth speakers, lanyards and boxed gift sets, with bamboo, cork and RPET versions across most of them. Branded t-shirts and polos come through our apparel service, and pens sit with our promotional print range. If you have another item in mind, send details and we will tell you whether it can be decorated and how.

Can you put individual names on each gift?

Yes, for most items. Names can be laser engraved on metal keychains and USB drives, foil-stamped on diary covers, embroidered on caps or printed on mugs. Send the names in a spreadsheet, checked for spelling and in the order you want them packed. We set one master layout, then proof the list before production so every name can be checked.

What logo file do you need for corporate gifts?

A vector file such as AI, EPS, SVG or PDF is best, because it scales cleanly to small branding areas. Engraving, debossing and pad printing all need a one-colour version, and embroidery needs a logo simple enough to be stitched. If you only have a JPG or PNG, our design team can redraw it as vector artwork before production.

How far ahead should we order Ramadan or year-end gifts?

As early as you can, because those seasons are busy for suppliers of blank stock as well as for decorators. Share a draft item list and quantities first, even before final numbers are known, so we can check stock and colours. Timelines depend on quantity, branding method and packaging, and we confirm the schedule with your quote.
