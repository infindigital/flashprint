LED Light Box Signage in Dubai for Shops and Offices

An LED light box is a fabricated enclosure with LEDs inside and a translucent face that carries your logo or message. Shapes range from slim rectangles to round projecting signs and cut-to-logo boxes, single or double-sided, for shopfronts, arcades, canopies and building entrances.

About LED light box signage

LED light box signage in Dubai is how many shops stay visible after dark, especially where fascias are small and pedestrians approach from the side. A projecting box, fixed at right angles to the wall, can be read from further along the pavement than a flat sign, while a wall-mounted box brightens a narrow fascia or entrance canopy.

Construction follows the size and look. Smaller boxes use an extruded aluminium profile or a folded ACP body with an opal acrylic face, printed directly or covered in translucent vinyl. Very slim boxes use edge-lit panels, where LEDs sit around the rim and a light guide plate spreads the glow. Larger boxes are framed in aluminium or GI with backlit flex or fabric faces, which are lighter and easier to replace than big acrylic sheets.

Exterior boxes need outdoor-rated LED modules and drivers, commonly IP65 or higher, sealed cable entries, drainage points and ventilation, because heat trapped in a closed box is hard on components. Drivers go where they can be reached for servicing. For printed graphics in existing indoor frames, such as menu boards and poster displays, our backlit displays page is the better starting point.

Materials

Opal acrylic face

Diffuses light evenly and holds a printed or vinyl graphic crisply; the usual face for small and medium boxes.

Backlit flex face

Used on large boxes where acrylic would be heavy or need joins, and simple to swap after a rebrand.

Aluminium profile body

A slim, powder-coated extrusion that gives a neat edge and lets the face be removed for servicing.

Folded ACP body

Tray-folded composite panel for custom shapes and deeper boxes, finished to match facade colours.

Finishing options

Translucent vinyl graphics

Colour-matched films on opal acrylic, cut or printed, glowing in brand colours at night.

Opaque face with lit logo

A dark or metallic face with only the lettering routed out and backed, so the logo alone lights up.

Powder-coated body

Box sides finished in a RAL colour to match the fascia, canopy or brand palette.

Double-sided faces

Two lit faces on projecting boxes, one for each direction of travel along the street.

Artwork checklist

  • Supply the logo in vector format with Pantone references for matching translucent vinyl
  • Light colours glow brightest; very dark faces may need a lit logo cut-out to read at night

LED light box signage FAQs

Are LED light boxes suitable for outdoor use in Dubai?

Yes, when they are built for it. Exterior boxes need outdoor-rated LED modules and drivers, sealed cable entries, drainage and ventilation to handle summer heat and humidity, and UV-stable faces. Indoor light boxes are simpler and should not be moved outside. Tell us where the box will be fixed, and we specify it accordingly.

How bright should my light box be?

Bright enough to read clearly after dark without glare. Some malls and landlords set limits on brightness, light colour or operating hours, and very bright signs can bother residents nearby. We plan module spacing for even light and can add a dimmer or timer, so the sign can be adjusted once it is in place.

What is the difference between a light box and a flex sign board?

Both glow from inside, but the scale and build differ. A light box is usually a compact, fully enclosed unit with an acrylic or fabric face, often projecting or shaped to a logo. A flex sign board is a larger frame with a tensioned flex face, used for wide fascias and rooftop signs where acrylic would be impractical.

Can the face be changed if we rebrand?

In most cases, yes. Acrylic faces can be re-covered with new vinyl or replaced, and flex or fabric faces can be reprinted to fit the same box. If the shape of the logo changes significantly, a cut-to-shape box may need a new body. We assess existing boxes before quoting a face change.

Do you need a power point near the sign?

Yes, an illuminated sign needs a suitable power supply close to the fixing position, connected by a qualified electrician where your building rules require it. We advise on driver location and cable routes during the site visit, so the supply can be ready before installation day.
