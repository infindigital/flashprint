School Printing in Dubai for Nurseries and Colleges

ID cards, certificates, report cards, exam papers, prospectuses, campus signage and event print for schools, nurseries, universities and training centres, planned around the academic year.

Get a Free QuoteWhatsApp Us

School printing in Dubai runs to the academic calendar. British, American and IB schools start in late August or September, while Indian and Pakistani curriculum schools begin their year in April, so peak demand arrives at different times across the city. Each new year brings student and staff ID cards, planners, welcome packs and updated signage. Later come exam papers, report cards, sports day banners, graduation invitations and certificates.

Admissions teams run their own cycle, with prospectuses, open-day roll-ups and outdoor banners needed well before registration opens. Universities and training centres add course guides, training manuals and certificates for every cohort. The groups below show what educational institutions typically order and how the pieces connect, so a school can plan one print schedule for the year instead of handling each request as it arrives.

What we produce

Printing for schools & education

ID cards, planners and welcome packs

Student and staff ID cards are usually printed on PVC at CR80 size (85.6 × 54 mm), with photos, names, classes and QR codes drawn from a data file. Student planners carrying the school calendar and branded folders for welcome packs follow close behind.

ID CardsDiariesFiles & Folders

Assessment and reporting

Exam papers and answer booklets need accurate collation, and packing and handling requirements can be agreed when you brief us. Report cards are often printed on heavier card with the school crest, with names and grades merged from a data file you supply.

Exam PapersReport Cards

Certificates and prize-giving

Certificates for graduation, sports day, attendance and course completion look more formal on heavyweight or textured stock with a foil crest. Engraved plaques and trophies recognise house winners and long-serving staff, and printed invitations bring families to the ceremony.

CertificatesPlaques & TrophiesInvitations

Admissions and open days

Prospectuses, admissions flyers and course brochures introduce the school to parents. Roll-up banners in reception and flex banners on the perimeter fence advertise registration dates and open days, and pop-up displays travel to education fairs.

BrochuresFlyers & LeafletsRoll-up BannersFlex BannersPop-Up Displays

Campus signage and learning walls

Wayfinding signs, classroom door plates and wall murals help younger pupils find their way and bring corridors to life. Printed wall graphics can carry house colours, school values, reading corners or science timelines, on vinyl that is replaced as displays change.

Wayfinding GraphicsAcrylic Sign BoardsWall MuralsWall Graphics

Sports day, trips and training materials

House T-shirts for sports day, staff uniforms and printed caps for trips are ordered in bulk by size. Universities, training institutes and corporate academies also need bound training manuals and workbooks for each intake.

T-Shirt PrintingUniform PrintingCapsTraining Manuals

Practical advice

Planning a school's print year

Most school print is predictable, so plan it like a timetable. List what you need each term, from ID cards in the first weeks to report cards and certificates at the end, and send files well ahead of each peak. ID card data is the most common cause of delay: agree a spreadsheet format for names, classes and photo filenames before term starts, and ask class teachers to check spellings before you approve proofs.

Durability matters where children are involved. Laminated posters, vinyl wall graphics and round-cornered signs handle knocks better than bare paper or sharp-edged panels. For nurseries, keep text short, graphics bold and displays low enough for small children to see. Banners on school fences face months of direct sun, so ask for UV-resistant inks. For exam papers, send clear written instructions on collation, packing and labelling, and keep one named contact responsible for the order.

FAQs

Schools & Education printing FAQs

Can you print student ID cards from our database?

Yes. Send a spreadsheet with names, classes, ID numbers and photo filenames, plus a folder of photos, and we merge them into your approved card design. We share proofs before printing so you can check spellings and photos. Common options include PVC cards punched for a lanyard or clip, and we confirm materials in your quote.

What paper works best for school certificates?

Certificates are usually printed on 250 to 350 gsm board or a textured stock that feels formal and resists creasing. Gold or silver foil on the crest adds a finishing touch. If names will be written by hand, choose an uncoated surface that takes ink well; if they are printed, we can merge them from your list.

Can you print exam papers in batches for different classes?

Yes. Exam papers can be printed, collated, stapled and packed by class or subject, following the instructions you send with the files. Tell us how many sets each class needs, whether answer booklets are separate and how you want packs labelled. We confirm the schedule with your quote, based on page counts and quantities.
