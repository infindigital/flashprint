Construction Printing in Dubai for Sites and Offices

Hoardings, project boards, site safety signage, hi-vis vests, vehicle branding and tender documents for contractors, subcontractors and developers, produced to the specification your consultant or client provides.

Get a Free QuoteWhatsApp Us

Construction printing in Dubai starts before the first pile goes in. As soon as a plot is fenced, the site needs hoarding graphics, a project sign board listing the developer, consultant and contractor, gate signs and safety boards at every entrance. As work progresses, directional signs for deliveries, PPE and hazard signage, reflective markings and hi-vis vests for the workforce follow, and many of them need replacing as phases change.

Office-side print matters just as much. Contractors bidding for work need a company profile that presents their capability clearly, along with letterheads, presentation folders and business cards for the tender team. Daily operations run on NCR delivery notes, material requisitions and inspection books. The groups below cover both sides, the site and the office, with links to the products contractors and developers order most often.

What we produce

Printing for construction

Hoardings and project boards

Printed vinyl on hoarding panels turns long runs of site fencing into branding for the developer and project. Project sign boards, commonly printed ACP mounted on a frame, display the permit details, parties and project name in the layout the authority or consultant requires.

Practical advice

Specifying signage for a live site

Dubai sites are hard on signage. Direct sun, heat, dust and wind all work against printed graphics, so outdoor items should use UV-resistant inks, laminated vinyl and rigid substrates such as ACP for boards. Hoarding graphics need to be sized to the panel system on site, with fixings that suit the structure and its wind exposure. Measure the actual run, including gates and openings, before artwork is finalised.

Content on project boards and safety signage is usually prescribed. The developer, consultant or relevant authority will specify what must appear, from permit numbers to emergency contacts, and we produce to that specification rather than inventing a layout. Keep editable master files so boards can be updated as parties or phases change. When a hoarding changes hands or a site closes, our installation team can remove the old graphics and fit the new branding.

FAQs

Construction printing FAQs

Can you install hoarding graphics on site?

Yes. We print hoarding graphics and install them at your site in Dubai, working within the site's access and safety rules. Send the hoarding elevations or a measured survey, the panel type and the artwork, and we confirm materials, quantities and the installation schedule with your quote. Let us know the main contractor's induction and PPE requirements in advance.

What should a construction project sign board include?

Content is normally set by the developer, the consultant or the relevant authority, and often includes the project name, plot number, permit details and the names of the developer, consultant and contractor. Supply the required wording and any template you have been given, and we produce the board to that layout on a suitable rigid material.

Can you print a company profile for a tender submission?

Yes. Company profiles for prequalification and tenders are commonly printed at A4 on silk or matt stock with a heavier laminated cover, then saddle-stitched or perfect-bound depending on page count. Our design team can help structure and lay out the content, and we share a proof so you can check every page before it is printed.
