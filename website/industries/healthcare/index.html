Healthcare Printing in Dubai for Clinics and Hospitals

Wayfinding, patient information, forms, prescription pads, privacy film, staff ID and uniforms for clinics, dental practices, pharmacies and hospitals, printed to your wording and your facility's requirements.

Get a Free QuoteWhatsApp Us

Healthcare printing in Dubai has to be clear before it is decorative. Patients arriving at a clinic are often anxious, unwell or visiting for the first time, and they rely on signs to find reception, the pharmacy or radiology. Forms, prescription pads and consent documents carry information that must be legible and correctly laid out. Everything also has to cope with frequent cleaning.

This page brings together what clinics, dental and dermatology practices, pharmacies, diagnostic centres and hospitals typically order across our whole range. The healthcare catalogue lists individual medical items; here we look at how they fit together in a facility, from the entrance sign to the leaflet a patient takes home. Regulated content such as licence displays, mandatory notices and medication labelling should follow the wording your licensing authority or compliance team specifies, and we produce to that specification.

What we produce

Printing for healthcare & clinics

Wayfinding patients can follow

Directory boards, department signs, room numbers and floor graphics guide patients from the car park to the consultation room. Large type, strong contrast, Arabic and English text and consistent pictograms make routes easier to follow for every age group.

Hospital SignageWayfinding GraphicsFloor GraphicsAcrylic Sign Boards

Patient information and awareness

Patient information boards in waiting areas explain services, patient rights and visiting hours, while posters and leaflets support campaigns such as breast cancer awareness month or diabetes screening. Treatment brochures are common in dental, cosmetic and wellness clinics.

Patient BoardsPostersBrochuresFlyers & Leaflets

Forms, pads and clinical stationery

Registration forms, consent forms, lab requests and referral letters are printed as pads, NCR sets for duplicate copies or single sheets for scanning. Prescription pads carry the clinic's details and doctor fields, and sequential numbering can be added where your process needs it.

Medical FormsPrescription PadsNCR / Carbonless FormsLetterheads

Privacy and wipe-clean surfaces

Frosted glass film on consultation rooms, treatment areas and pharmacy counters gives privacy while keeping spaces bright. Wall graphics and murals in paediatric and dental clinics can be printed on laminated vinyl that suits routine wiping.

Frosted Glass FilmWall GraphicsWall Murals

Labels and safety notices

Warning labels mark sharps bins, clinical waste, oxygen and hazardous stores, and safety signs cover fire exits and restricted areas. Pharmacy and laboratory teams also use barcode labels and regulatory stickers for stock, equipment and sample handling.

Warning LabelsSafety SignsBarcode LabelsRegulatory Stickers

Staff ID, uniforms and appointment cards

Photo ID cards showing the facility name and department, together with printed or embroidered uniforms, help patients recognise staff. Appointment cards at the standard 90 × 55 mm business-card size slip easily into a wallet.

ID CardsUniform PrintingBusiness Cards Basic

Practical advice

Getting clinic print right

Legibility comes first. Use generous type sizes on signs and forms, avoid low-contrast colour combinations, and keep Arabic and English equally prominent. For patient forms, fill in a printed sample before ordering in quantity: writing spaces are often too small, and a form that looks fine on screen can be awkward to complete on a clipboard.

Choose finishes that stand up to cleaning. Matt laminated and acrylic-faced signs are easier to wipe down with routine disinfectant than uncoated surfaces, so avoid textured papers on anything that sits on a reception counter. Content controlled by regulation, such as licence and complaint notices, price displays or medication warnings, should be supplied in its final approved wording; we set and print it exactly as provided. Multi-branch groups benefit from a signage template so each new site matches the rest without redesigning every sign.

FAQs

Healthcare & Clinics printing FAQs

Can you print medical forms in Arabic and English?

Yes. Bilingual forms are common in Dubai facilities, and we can lay out Arabic and English side by side or on alternate sides. Please supply the final approved text for both languages as editable copy. We check the layout and share a proof, but the clinical and legal wording remains your team's responsibility.

Can prescription pads include sequential numbering?

Numbering can be added to prescription pads and NCR books where your process requires it, along with the clinic name, address, licence details and doctor fields you specify. Tell us the starting number, sheets per pad and if you need duplicate copies, and we confirm the available options in your quote.

Can your signage follow our hospital's wayfinding plan?

Yes. Send us the signage schedule, floor plans and any design standards from your consultant or facility management team. We produce directories, department signs, room plates and floor graphics to that plan and can install them at your facility in Dubai, scheduled around clinical operating hours where needed.
