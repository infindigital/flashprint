Restaurant Printing in Dubai for Cafés and Kitchens

Menus, table print, delivery packaging, signage and uniforms for restaurants, cafés and cloud kitchens, produced as one consistent brand and specified for how hard your front of house works.

Get a Free QuoteWhatsApp Us

Restaurant printing in Dubai covers far more than the menu. A typical opening brief includes dine-in menus, a takeaway leaflet, tent cards for the day's specials, printed boxes and bags for delivery, seal stickers, a shopfront sign and printed staff uniforms. When these items come from different suppliers, colours drift and the brand starts to look patched together. Producing them together keeps the logo, typefaces and colour references consistent from the pass to the rider's bag.

The brief is similar for an independent café in Jumeirah, a multi-branch group or a delivery-only kitchen, and the questions are usually practical: which menu stock survives spilled karak and daily wiping, how to change prices without reprinting everything, and what packaging holds up in a delivery bag in August. The groups below set out what food and beverage businesses most often order, with links to each product.

What we produce

Printing for restaurants & cafés

Menus that survive service

Dine-in menus are handled at every cover, so popular choices are 350 gsm card with matt or soft-touch lamination, or synthetic paper that can be wiped down between guests. A6 tent cards carry specials, set lunches and new dishes without touching the main menu.

Restaurant MenusTent Cards

Table and counter print

Paper table mats double as a menu or promotion space in casual dining and cafés. Counter displays hold loyalty cards, QR codes for ordering or a featured dessert, and small acrylic signs work well for allergen notes and Wi-Fi details at the till.

Table MatsCounter DisplaysAcrylic Sign Boards

Delivery and takeaway packaging

Branded boxes, kraft bags and paper carriers are what customers actually see when they order through an app. Seal stickers across the bag opening act as a visible tamper seal, and printed labels identify dishes, sauces and allergens for the customer.

Custom BoxesKraft BagsPaper BagsDie-Cut StickersProduct Labels

Shopfront and dining-room signage

Fascia signs with 3D letters or an LED light box make the outlet visible from the road or mall walkway after dark. Inside, neon-style signs and wall murals set the mood, while glass stickers carry opening hours and delivery-app badges on the door.

3D Letter SignageLED Light BoxesNeon Sign BoardsWall MuralsGlass Stickers

Staff uniforms and caps

Printed T-shirts, uniforms and caps keep kitchen, floor and delivery staff recognisable. Logos can be printed or embroidered, and ordering a few spare sizes for new starters stops the team ending up in a mix of old and new designs.

Uniform PrintingT-Shirt PrintingCaps

Promotions and local marketing

Flyers announce a new branch to nearby towers, and posters or roll-up banners cover Ramadan iftar offers, Eid set menus and summer promotions. Loyalty and stamp cards printed at 90 × 55 mm, the standard business-card size, fit neatly into a wallet.

Practical advice

Specifying print for a busy restaurant

Plan for heat, grease and cleaning. Laminated card suits a café with a short menu, while high-volume venues often move to synthetic paper. Keep prices, seasonal dishes and set menus on a separate insert or tent card, so a price change means reprinting one sheet rather than the whole book. If your menu needs calorie or allergen information, build it into the layout from the start instead of adding stickers later.

For delivery, match the packaging to the food: a grease-resistant board or liner for fried items, kraft bags with sturdy handles for multi-item orders, and labels that stay put on chilled containers. We confirm board and adhesive options in your quote. Outlets inside malls and hotels usually have to follow the landlord's fit-out and signage guidelines, so send those with your artwork.

FAQs

Restaurants & Cafés printing FAQs

Which menu material works best for a busy restaurant?

For menus handled every service, synthetic paper or laminated 350 gsm card are the usual choices. Synthetic stock resists tearing and can be wiped with sanitiser, while laminated card has a more traditional feel and is simple to reprint when dishes change. For fine dining, printed inserts in a hard cover give you a substantial book with pages you can swap.

Can you print bilingual Arabic and English menus?

Yes. Bilingual menus are common in Dubai, and we can set Arabic and English side by side or on facing pages. Send the approved Arabic text as editable copy rather than an image so it can be typeset properly. Our design team can help with the layout, but the translation itself should come from you or your translator.

Can you produce packaging and signage for a new outlet together?

Yes. Many openings combine menus, packaging, stickers, uniforms and a fascia sign in one brief. We quote the items together, check the artwork for each and share proofs before production. Signage can be fabricated and installed at your premises in Dubai. Timelines depend on quantities, finishes and fit-out access, so we agree the schedule with your quote.
