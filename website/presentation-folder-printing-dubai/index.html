About presentation folder printing

Presentation folder printing in Dubai is mostly driven by documents that need to arrive together and look organised: proposals, tender submissions, property sale packs, onboarding kits and conference materials. A good folder protects the pages, keeps them in order and puts your brand on the outside of everything you hand over.

Our standard format is an A4 folder, closed at around 220 × 310 mm so A4 sheets sit inside without catching. One or two inside pockets are die-cut and glued, with slits to hold a business card on the pocket. For thicker document sets, a capacity spine of 5–10 mm stops the folder from bowing and lets it close flat.

Folders are printed flat on 300–350 gsm board, then laminated, die-cut, creased and folded. Because the pockets fold back over the inside panels, the artwork is built on a die-line template that shows glue areas, fold lines and which parts appear upside down on the flat sheet. We supply that template before you start designing.

Materials

350 gsm silk board

Stiff enough to protect A4 documents, with a smooth surface for sharp colour. The usual choice for corporate folders.

300 gsm board

Slightly lighter and more flexible, suited to conference packs and event quantities where many folders are handed out.

Uncoated board

A natural surface that can be written on, chosen by schools, clinics and law firms that prefer an understated look.

Kraft board

Brown board with a natural texture, pairing well with white or black printing for sustainability-focused brands.

Finishing options

Matt lamination

Protects folds and edges from cracking and scuffing, which matters for folders opened and closed repeatedly.

Soft-touch lamination

A velvety feel for client-facing folders, often paired with a foil or spot UV logo on the front.

Spot UV

Glossy highlights over a matt background, adding interest without extra print colours.

Hot foil and embossing

A foiled or raised logo on the cover for developers, hotels and professional firms presenting to key clients.

Artwork checklist

  • Design on our flat die-line template and keep the die-line on a separate layer
  • Add 3 mm bleed on all outer edges and around the pocket shapes
  • Remember the pockets fold inward, so their artwork sits upside down on the flat layout
  • Keep ink and lamination off glue flaps where the template marks them
  • Avoid fine text across fold or spine lines, where creasing can crack the print
  • Check that colour running across the spine continues cleanly from front to back

Presentation folder printing FAQs

How many pages will a presentation folder hold?

A flat folder without a spine is designed for a modest set of loose sheets, such as a short proposal or a few brochures. For thicker proposals or tender documents, a capacity spine of 5–10 mm lets the folder close flat with more pages inside. Tell us your typical page count and paper weight and we will recommend the right format.

Can you add a business card slit?

Yes. A die-cut slit on the pocket holds a business card at an angle so it stays visible when the folder opens. Slits can go on the left pocket, right pocket or both, and are marked on the template so your artwork does not run across them in a way that looks broken when the card is inserted.

What is the difference between a presentation folder and a file?

A presentation folder is a printed, die-cut board folder with pockets, used to hand over loose documents neatly. Files, such as ring binders, box files or document wallets, are storage items that can also carry your branding. If you need something for regular filing rather than a client handover, let us know and we will advise on suitable formats.

Can folders be printed on the inside?

Yes. The inside panels and pockets can carry colour, patterns, a map, a service list or contact details. Many clients use the inside flap for a short company overview or an index of what is included. Inside printing is set up on the same flat template, and we show both sides on the proof before production.

Are presentation folders suitable for tender submissions?

They are widely used for tenders because they keep documents in order and present a clean first impression. Check the tender instructions first, as some clients specify binding, labelling or document formats. For presentation folder printing tied to a tender, we produce to the size and specification you provide and can add the tender reference on the cover.
