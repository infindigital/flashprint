About prescription pad printing

A prescription pad is one of the most personal items a clinic prints. Each doctor usually has their own version, carrying the clinic's logo and contact details, the doctor's name and specialty, and the identifiers your team asks to include, with a clear area for writing and signing. We handle prescription pad printing in Dubai for single-doctor practices, polyclinics, dental clinics and hospital outpatient departments.

The usual format is A5 or A6 on 80 to 100 gsm bond, glued at the head into pads of 50 or 100 sheets with a board back. Many clinics add sequential numbering so every sheet can be traced, a light background tint or repeated pattern that helps distinguish originals from plain copies, and perforation. Where a copy is needed, two-part NCR prescription sets are available.

Content decisions, such as which identifiers, licence details or wording must appear, belong to the clinic and the doctor. We build a master template, then set each doctor's details from a list you provide and proof every version before printing. That keeps a multi-doctor order consistent and easy to repeat.

Materials

Bond paper

Uncoated sheets that take ballpoint and fountain pen well and accept clinic stamps cleanly.

Uncoated offset

A slightly smoother alternative that holds crisp logos and fine type.

2-part NCR

A top sheet for the patient and a copy retained by the clinic, with no carbon required.

Greyboard backing

A stiff back that keeps the pad flat when writing away from a desk.

Finishing options

Sequential numbering

Unique numbers on each sheet, useful for record keeping and reconciliation.

Background tint or pattern

A light printed tint or repeated clinic mark behind the writing area.

Perforation

A tear line near the glued edge so sheets detach cleanly.

Stub or counterfoil

A section that stays in the pad as a brief record of each sheet issued.

Prescription pad printing FAQs

What details must a prescription pad include?

That is for the clinic, the doctor and the requirements of your licensing body to determine, and it can differ by facility type. We do not advise on prescription requirements. Send the details your team has confirmed, and we will lay them out clearly and proof every doctor's version before printing.

Can each doctor have their own pad?

Yes. We create one master template for the clinic and personalise the doctor's name, specialty and other supplied details for each version. Every version is proofed, and you can order different quantities per doctor. Pads for new doctors use the same template, so stationery stays consistent across the clinic.

Can pads be numbered?

Yes. Each sheet can carry a unique sequential number, optionally with a prefix for the clinic or doctor. Numbering can continue from your last order if you give us the final number used. Many clinics keep a simple log of the number ranges issued to each doctor.

What is a background tint and why add one?

It is a light printed colour, pattern or repeated logo behind the writing area. It gives originals a distinctive look that differs from a plain photocopy and helps staff recognise the clinic's own pads. It is one of several optional features, and we confirm the available options in your quote.

Should we choose A5 or A6?

A6 is compact, fits a coat pocket or small desk and suits short prescriptions. A5 gives more room for longer lists, instructions and bilingual text. Many clinics use A5 for general practice and A6 for specialists who write shorter notes. We can print a sample of each so doctors can compare.
