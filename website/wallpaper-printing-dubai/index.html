About wallpaper printing

Custom wallpaper printing in Dubai gives interior designers and business owners full control over what goes on the wall. Instead of adapting a scheme to a catalogue pattern, the pattern is designed for the space: a subtle brand motif repeated along a hotel corridor, a botanical design matched to restaurant upholstery, or a textured print that ties an office floor together.

Printed wallpaper differs from a wall mural in how it is designed and hung. A mural is one image scaled to one wall. Wallpaper is designed as a repeat that tiles without visible breaks across any number of drops, so the same design can cover a single feature wall or a whole floor. Straight-match repeats line up horizontally from drop to drop; half-drop repeats offset every other drop for a less regimented look.

The substrate matters as much as the design. Non-woven wallpaper is breathable, stable and hung paste-the-wall, making it the usual choice for homes, boutique hotels and offices. Fabric-backed commercial vinyl is heavier, scrubbable and far more tolerant of knocks from luggage trolleys and chairs, which is why it is specified for hotel corridors and restaurants. Where a fit-out calls for a particular fire classification, we source to that specification.

Materials

Non-woven wallpaper

Dimensionally stable, so it does not stretch when pasted. It hangs paste-the-wall and dry-strips when you redecorate.

Vinyl-coated non-woven

Adds a wipeable surface for pantries, clinics and offices while keeping the easy hanging of non-woven.

Fabric-backed commercial vinyl

Heavy-duty wallcovering for hotel corridors, restaurants and busy areas where walls get knocked and cleaned.

Textured and metallic media

Embossed or pearlescent substrates that give depth to damask, geometric and brand patterns.

Finishing options

Matt print

Flat and calm, hiding minor wall imperfections and avoiding glare.

Embossed textures

Linen, sand and canvas textures add a tactile quality that flat paint cannot match.

Metallic effect

Printed on silver or pearl base media for a subtle shimmer in lounges and suites.

Wallpaper printing FAQs

How much wallpaper do I need to order?

Divide the width of each wall by the roll width to find the number of drops, then multiply by the wall height plus a trimming allowance. Patterns with a large repeat need extra length for matching. Send your measurements or drawings and we calculate the quantity, including a small allowance for wastage and future repairs.

Can custom wallpaper be removed later?

Non-woven wallpaper is designed to be dry-stripped: it peels away in full lengths without soaking, leaving the wall ready for paint or new paper. Fabric-backed vinyl can usually be peeled too, sometimes leaving a backing layer to be soaked off. A suitable primer before hanging makes any future removal cleaner.

Is printed wallpaper suitable for hotel corridors?

Yes, when the right substrate is chosen. Hotel corridors take knocks from luggage and trolleys and need regular cleaning, so heavier fabric-backed commercial vinyl is the usual choice. Your interior designer or fit-out consultant normally specifies the wallcovering type and any fire classification required, and we source and print to that specification.

Can you print wallpaper for a single feature wall?

Yes. Many projects are one feature wall behind a bed, a reception desk or a banquette. For a single wall, either a repeating pattern or one large image can work. We print only the drops that wall needs, plus a spare drop for trimming or later repair if you want one.

What is the difference between paste-the-wall and paste-the-paper?

With non-woven wallpaper, paste is rolled onto the wall and the dry paper is hung directly, which is cleaner and makes alignment easier. Traditional papers and some vinyls need paste applied to the back of each drop, left to soak, then hung. Our installers use whichever method the substrate requires.
