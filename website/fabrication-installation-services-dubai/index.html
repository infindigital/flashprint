Printed graphics and signs only do their job once they are fixed in the right place, at the right height, on a surface that will hold them. Our fabrication and installation services in Dubai cover that final stage: building sign components, preparing substrates, and fitting signage, vinyl, wraps and graphics at your premises, from a shopfront or office floor to a warehouse or a fleet parked at your depot.

When fabrication and fitting are planned together, measurements, fixings and access are worked out before production starts rather than on the day. Every installation begins with a survey or a clear set of site photos and dimensions, and that is also the point where we go through building rules, working hours and approvals with you.

What fabrication covers

Fabrication is the physical build that sits between print and installation. For signage it can mean cutting aluminium composite panel (ACP) to size and folding tray edges, routing acrylic or foam PVC letters, building frames for light boxes, mounting printed graphics onto rigid boards, or preparing brackets and backing plates for projecting signs.

For graphics, it is the preparation that makes fitting straightforward: vinyl lettering cut, weeded and covered with application tape, large graphics split into panels with overlaps planned around joints, and wraps printed with extra bleed for trimming in place.

  • ACP trays, flat panels and cladding for fascia signs
  • Acrylic and foam PVC letters with drilling templates
  • Frames and light box housings built to the measured opening
  • Vinyl lettering and graphics cut, weeded and pre-taped for application
  • Printed panels mounted on foam board, foam PVC or ACP

How an installation is planned

A good installation is mostly decided before anyone climbs a ladder. During the survey we measure the fixing area, check what the wall, glass or panel is made of, note where power is available for illuminated signs, and look at access: ceiling heights, space for a ladder, tower or lift, permitted working hours and whether the area needs to be cordoned off.

Access, permits and landlord or building approvals are confirmed with you during the site survey. Many malls and towers have their own contractor rules, induction requirements and working windows, and some fixings need the landlord's written consent. The work is then produced and fitted to the specification your consultant, landlord or authority provides.

  • Brief and site photos, or a site survey
  • Quote setting out scope, materials and fixing method
  • Artwork check and proof approval
  • Fabrication and print production
  • Installation, clean-down and handover

Surfaces and settings

Most jobs involve one of four surface groups: masonry and blockwork for fascia signs, painted plasterboard and glass inside offices, vehicle paintwork and glass, and temporary structures such as hoardings and exhibition stands. Each needs different fixings and preparation. An anchor that suits concrete will not hold in plasterboard, and a vinyl that bonds well to glass may not grip a textured wall.

Dubai's climate is part of every plan. Heat and sun affect adhesive choice, direct sunlight on glass changes how film behaves during application, and outdoor fixings have to allow for wind load. We raise these points during the survey so the specification suits the location, not just the drawing.

FAQs

Fabrication & Installation Services FAQs

What is the difference between fabrication and installation?

Fabrication is the build: cutting, routing, bending, mounting and assembling the sign or graphic so it is ready to fit. Installation is the work on site, from marking positions and fixing to levelling, trimming and cleaning down. Many projects need both, and planning them together means brackets, templates and panel sizes suit the actual location.

Who arranges permits and building approvals?

Requirements depend on the building, the landlord and the type of work. We go through them with you during the site survey, including contractor registration, work permits, inductions and permitted hours. Where formal consent is needed, it is usually requested by the tenant or owner, and we provide the drawings and details that support the application.

Do you work outside normal business hours?

Many malls, towers and shops only allow installation outside trading hours. Tell us the permitted working window during the survey and we plan the job around it. Out-of-hours timing is set out in the quote along with the scope, access equipment and number of visits needed.

What do you need to quote for installation?

Send the location, photos of the fixing area with a tape measure or known object for scale, approximate dimensions, the surface material if known, and what is being installed. For larger or higher jobs we arrange a site survey. Details of building rules, parking and access help us plan the visit accurately.
