About floor graphics

Floor graphics in Dubai are used in two very different ways. Short-term graphics promote a launch at the end of a supermarket aisle, mark a queue at a pharmacy counter or brand the floor of an exhibition stand for a few days. Longer-term graphics mark walkways in warehouses, show seating zones in schools or put a logo at a showroom entrance. The film, adhesive and laminate change with each use.

Every floor graphic we produce is a sandwich of printed vinyl and a protective laminate. The base film is a floor-grade vinyl with an adhesive suited to the surface and to how long it needs to stay down. The laminate on top is textured to add grip underfoot and to take the abrasion of shoes, trolleys and cleaning machines. A plain print without it would soon scuff and become slippery when wet.

Different floors need different approaches. Smooth tile, vinyl flooring and sealed concrete take standard floor film. Exhibition carpet needs a dedicated carpet film, and outdoor paving or rough concrete needs a conformable film heated into the texture. Tell us the floor finish and the campaign length, and we specify from there.

Materials

Removable floor vinyl

Made for campaigns and promotions, peeling away from sealed floors when the activity ends.

Permanent floor vinyl

A stronger bond for longer-term markings such as walkways, logos and zones that face regular cleaning.

Carpet graphic film

Designed to sit on low-pile exhibition carpet for the length of a show and lift off afterwards.

Rough-surface film

A conformable film heated into concrete, paving or asphalt for outdoor and car park graphics.

Finishing options

Textured anti-slip laminate

A fine sand-like surface that adds grip underfoot and resists scuffing from shoes and trolleys.

Contour cutting

Graphics cut to the outline of a logo, arrow or footprint for a painted-on look.

Full-bleed floor wraps

Large areas printed in panels with matched joins and laid edge to edge.

White or clear base

White film for vivid colour, or clear film where the floor finish should show through.

    We match the laminate to footfall and cleaning, from light retail use to trolley routes.

  • Adhesive matched to duration

    Removable film for campaigns and permanent film for markings, so the bond suits how long the graphic stays.

Artwork checklist

  • Design at actual size; a 600 mm circle is a common size for queue and promotional decals
  • Keep text large and bold, because people read floor graphics from standing height a metre or more away
  • Allow 3–5 mm bleed on contour-cut shapes and 10 mm on panels that butt-join
  • Avoid placing faces or key text across tile joints or thresholds
  • Set the reading direction so the graphic is upright for people approaching it

Floor graphics FAQs

How long can floor graphics stay down?

It depends on the film, the floor and the traffic. Removable graphics are intended for short campaigns and events, while permanent floor film under a heavier laminate suits longer-term markings. Wet cleaning machines and trolleys wear any graphic sooner, so we ask about both before recommending a material, and can plan replacement visits for long campaigns.

Are floor graphics slippery?

Not when laminated correctly. Every floor graphic we produce carries a textured anti-slip laminate, which gives more grip than a smooth print. Where your landlord, mall or safety consultant specifies a slip-resistance rating, share it and we specify a laminate to that requirement. Avoid permanently wet zones such as pool surrounds unless the film has been chosen for them.

Can floor graphics go on carpet?

Yes, with a dedicated carpet film. It is made for short-pile exhibition and event carpet, holding for the length of a show and lifting cleanly afterwards. It is not suited to deep-pile carpet or long-term use. For exhibition stands, we often print the carpet graphic and the stand walls together so the colours match.

Will the vinyl damage tiles or leave residue?

Removable floor vinyl is designed to come away from sealed tile, vinyl flooring and concrete with little or no residue, particularly when lifted within its intended period. Unsealed, porous surfaces can absorb adhesive, so we check the floor first. Any light residue is cleaned off with a suitable remover during our removal visit.

Can floor stickers be used outdoors?

We can, using a rough-surface film heated into the texture of paving, concrete or asphalt. Outdoor graphics in Dubai face strong sun and heat, so they suit campaigns and events better than permanent markings. Send a photo of the surface and its location, and we will confirm whether it is suitable before quoting.
