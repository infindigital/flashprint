Shelf Wobblers for In-Store Promotions

Wobblers are small die-cut signs on a flexible plastic arm that sticks to the shelf edge and sways with air currents and passing shoppers. That movement draws attention in a crowded aisle, which is why brands use wobblers for offers, launches and 'new' messages.

About wobbler printing

Wobbler printing in Dubai is a small job with a specific purpose: movement. Shelves are full of static printed messages, from price tickets to packaging, and a sign that sways when a chiller door opens or a trolley passes is simply more noticeable. Brands use wobblers for promotions, new product flags and short benefit messages right beside the product.

A wobbler has two parts. The printed face, usually 60 to 120 mm across, is die-cut into a circle, starburst, arrow or product shape from laminated card or thin PVC. The arm is a strip of clear flexible PVC or PET, typically 100 to 150 mm long, fixed to the face and attached to the shelf with an adhesive pad. The arm length controls how far the face stands out and how much it moves.

Because they sit on the shelf edge, wobblers must not block price tickets or the product itself. Retailers may limit how many are allowed per bay, and some prefer clip fixings to adhesive. We print both sides when the wobbler will be seen from two directions along the aisle.

Materials

Laminated card

The standard face material for short campaigns in dry aisles, with crisp colour and a firm feel.

PVC face

Resists moisture in chillers and fresh produce sections and holds its shape longer than card.

Clear PVC arm

Flexible and nearly invisible, so the face appears to float and sway at the shelf edge.

PET arm

A springier clear strip that returns to position after being knocked by baskets or hands.

Finishing options

Gloss lamination

A bright, reflective surface that catches store lighting as the wobbler moves.

Die-cut shapes

Circles, starbursts, arrows, speech bubbles or product outlines.

Double-sided print

Matching or different messages on each face, visible from both directions along the aisle.

Where it’s used

  • Supermarket price promotions
  • New product introductions
  • Pharmacy and beauty offers
  • Chiller and freezer door promotions
  • Impulse lines in convenience stores

Artwork checklist

  • Supply a die-cut shape, or ask us to create one around your design
  • Keep text large, as the face is small and read while moving
  • Leave a clear area where the arm attaches to the back of the face
  • Add 2–3 mm bleed around the die-cut outline
  • Provide separate artwork for each side if printing double-sided

Wobbler printing FAQs

What is a wobbler used for?

A wobbler flags a product on the shelf using movement. It is a printed shape on a flexible arm that sways slightly with air from chillers, air conditioning and passing shoppers. Retailers and brands use wobblers for offers, launches and short messages because movement gets noticed among static tickets and packaging.

Which wobbler size should we choose?

Most shelf wobblers are between 60 and 120 mm across. Smaller sizes suit pharmacy and cosmetics shelves where space is tight, while larger faces work on supermarket shelves and chiller doors. The face should be noticeable without hiding the product or its price ticket, so check the shelf spacing before choosing.

Can wobblers go in chillers and freezers?

Yes, with the right materials. PVC faces cope with condensation better than card, and the adhesive pad needs to suit cold surfaces, since standard adhesives can lose grip at low temperatures. Tell us where the wobblers will be placed and we select a face material and fixing to match.

Do wobblers come assembled?

They can be supplied fully assembled, with the arm fixed to the printed face and the adhesive pad ready to peel, or as separate parts for merchandisers to put together in store. Assembled wobblers save time on the shop floor; separate parts pack flatter for large multi-store runs.
