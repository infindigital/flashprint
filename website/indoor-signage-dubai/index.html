Foam Board Signs

Foam board signs are lightweight printed panels, usually 5 mm or 10 mm thick, cut to shape and printed full colour on a smooth white face. They suit event directional signs, welcome boards, open-house notices and short-term office notices, and can stand on an easel, hang from ceiling hooks or fix to walls with removable pads.

Good indoor signage in Dubai does two jobs at once. It tells visitors where they are and where to go next, and it carries your brand into every corridor, meeting room and waiting area. The demands differ from outdoor work: there is no sun or sand to fight, but signs are read at close range, under bright ceiling lights, beside glass, stone and timber joinery.

We produce the full interior range, from a single engraved door plate to a complete sign family for a new fit-out: reception logo walls, acrylic sign boards, office name boards, printed and cut vinyl wall graphics, and foam board signs for short-term use. Based in Business Bay, we work with fit-out contractors, facility managers and business owners across Dubai, and can survey, produce and install as one job.

Materials that suit interior signs

Interior signs are judged from arm's length, so edge quality, colour accuracy and alignment matter more than weather resistance. Most sign families combine two or three of the materials below, chosen to match the interior design rather than the sign type. We confirm stock and options in your quote.

  • Cast acrylic, 3–10 mm: clear, frosted, opal or coloured sheet, laser-cut with polished edges and printed on the face or reverse
  • Brushed aluminium, stainless steel and brass-finish composite: engraved or printed door plates and directory boards
  • Printed and cut vinyl: logos, lettering and graphics applied directly to painted walls, glass and joinery
  • Foam PVC and foam board, 3–10 mm: lightweight printed panels for temporary, event and changeable signs
  • Stand-offs, VHB tape and concealed rails: the fixing changes how a sign reads, so we specify it alongside the material

Planning a sign family room by room

An office typically needs a reception or logo wall, a unit entrance name board, door signs for meeting rooms and private offices, glass manifestation, directional signs and statutory notices. Planned together, these share fonts, colours, heights and fixings, and the whole job usually needs fewer site visits.

Clinics add room identification, patient flow signs and doctor name plates, where changeable inserts save reprinting when rotas change. Retail interiors lean towards promotional wall graphics, category panels and fitting-room signs that are refreshed with each season. Hotels and serviced apartments need room numbers, amenity signs and back-of-house notices in a finish that matches the lobby.

For anything beyond a handful of signs we prepare a sign schedule: a numbered list of every sign with its location, size, material, wording and fixing method. Once you sign off the schedule and the proofs, production runs against it and the installation team works from the same document.

From survey to installation

Fit-out projects start with a site visit or drawings. We note wall finishes, glass thickness, cable routes for illuminated logos and any building rules on corridor-facing signs. Many Dubai towers issue tenant signage guidelines for entrance boards and lobby directories; share them early and we produce to that specification.

Timelines depend on quantity, finish and artwork readiness, and we confirm the schedule with your quote before production. Installation happens at your premises in Dubai, with drilling and fixing outside office hours available on request.

  • Brief, site survey or drawing review
  • Sign schedule and quote
  • Artwork check and proofs drawn to scale
  • Production, finishing and quality check
  • Installation and a final walk-round with you

FAQs

Indoor Signage FAQs

What is the difference between office signage, office name boards and reception signage?

Office signage is the whole family of signs inside a workplace, planned together. Office name boards are the specific plates and panels that identify a company at its entrance, a person at their door or tenants in a lobby directory. Reception signage is the logo feature behind your front desk, usually the largest and most visible piece. Each has its own page, and most fit-outs order all three together.

Can indoor signs be fitted without drilling into the wall?

Often, yes. Lightweight acrylic and aluminium panels can be bonded with VHB tape to smooth, sound surfaces such as painted plaster, glass or laminate. Heavier pieces, illuminated logos and signs in busy corridors are better mechanically fixed with stand-offs or rails. We check the wall finish during the survey and recommend the method that suits the weight and the surface.

Do you produce bilingual English and Arabic signs?

Yes. Many Dubai sign families carry both scripts, and we set layouts so the Arabic and English lines balance in size and weight rather than one being squeezed in. Supply the Arabic wording as typed text and our design team will set it. The translation itself should be checked by someone on your side before you sign off the proof.

Can you match our brand colours on acrylic and vinyl?

Send Pantone or RAL references and we select the closest coloured acrylic or vinyl from stock ranges, or print the colour onto white or clear material. Solid coloured sheet gives the most consistent edge colour, while printing offers a wider range of matches. We show options on the proof and can produce a small sample where a colour is critical.
