Stationery is the paperwork your clients actually handle: the card passed across a meeting table, the quotation on letterhead, the folder a proposal arrives in, the receipt a customer takes home. Our business stationery printing in Dubai covers each of those pieces, from 90 × 50 mm business cards to A4 letterheads, DL envelopes and triplicate invoice books.

Based in Business Bay, we work with offices, clinics, contractors, agencies and retailers across Dubai that need stationery to look consistent and work properly in daily use. That means letterheads that feed through an office laser printer, envelopes sized to the documents they carry and NCR sets that transfer cleanly to the last copy. We check artwork, send a proof for approval and confirm stock, finish and schedule in your quote before anything is printed.

Choosing papers and finishes for stationery

Stationery works best when the stock is chosen for the job, not only for the look. Cards need rigidity, letterheads need to accept toner and ink from office printers, and forms need to carry handwriting through several copies without fading.

Finishes follow the same logic. Matt or soft-touch lamination protects cards and folders that are handled often, while spot UV and hot foil add emphasis to a logo. Letterheads and forms are usually left uncoated, because lamination and heavy varnish get in the way of printers and pens.

  • Business cards: 350–400 gsm silk or uncoated board, with thicker duplexed boards for foil and embossed designs
  • Letterheads and compliment slips: 100–120 gsm uncoated bond or offset paper, left unlaminated so they print and sign easily
  • Envelopes: 90–120 gsm white wove, laid or kraft, plain or with a window
  • Presentation folders: 300–350 gsm board, usually laminated to protect the folds and pocket edges
  • Invoice and NCR books: CB, CFB and CF carbonless papers in white, yellow, pink and blue parts

Which pieces a Dubai office really needs

A new company in Dubai typically starts with business cards for the founders and sales team, an A4 letterhead carrying trade licence and TRN details, and DL envelopes for outgoing letters. Firms that send proposals or tender documents add presentation folders, often with a business card slit on the pocket.

Businesses that invoice or collect payment on the spot need numbered books. Retail counters, car workshops, maintenance contractors and delivery teams rely on duplicate or triplicate sets so the customer, the office and the accounts team each keep a copy. Where a single form is used for job cards, delivery notes or site inspections, custom NCR forms are laid out around the information your staff actually record.

Client-facing roles often call for a step up: gold foil business cards for directors, brokers and hospitality managers, printed on heavier board that feels noticeably different in the hand.

Ordering a matching stationery set

Ordering stationery together keeps colours, logo placement and typography consistent from item to item. We set up each piece from one master artwork, match your brand colours as closely as the chosen stock allows and show every item on a proof before production.

If you do not yet have finished artwork, our design team can build the set from your logo and brand guidelines, or refresh an existing layout that has drifted over years of reprints.

  • Send your logo as a vector file (AI, EPS or PDF) with your brand colour references
  • List the details each item needs: names, titles, mobile numbers, TRN, P.O. Box and trade licence number
  • Give quantities per item and say whether any cards need Arabic on the reverse
  • Mention the office printer you use if letterheads will be overprinted, so we can advise on stock and print method

FAQs

Business Stationery FAQs

Can you print a full stationery set in one order?

Yes. Business cards, letterheads, envelopes, folders and invoice books can all be quoted and produced together from the same master artwork. Ordering as a set helps keep colours and logo placement consistent across items. We list every item, stock and finish in one quote, then send proofs for each piece so you can approve them together before production starts.

What is the difference between an invoice book and an NCR form?

Both use carbonless paper. An invoice or receipt book is a numbered, bound book with your header, built for sales, tax invoices and payment receipts. NCR forms cover any other multi-part document, such as job cards, delivery notes, work permits or inspection sheets, and can be supplied as pads, books or loose sets. If you are unsure, send us a sample and we will suggest the right format.

Do you need my artwork in a specific format?

A print-ready PDF with 3 mm bleed and fonts embedded or outlined is ideal, with logos supplied as vectors. If you only have a Word file, a JPEG or an old printed sample, our designers can rebuild the layout for print. We check every file before proofing and flag anything that could affect the result, such as low-resolution images or text too close to the trim.

How long does stationery printing take?

Timelines depend on quantity, finish and artwork readiness. Digitally printed cards and letterheads are generally simpler to schedule than foil-stamped cards, numbered NCR books or die-cut folders, which involve extra finishing steps. We confirm the schedule with your quote before production, so you can plan around launches, office moves or new staff joining.
