Wall, Floor and Glass Branding

Walls, floors and glass are the largest surfaces in any interior. We print and install murals, anti-slip floor graphics, frosted film, glass stickers and wayfinding graphics that turn them into branding and navigation.

Wall and glass branding in Dubai works hardest where visitors already look: the long corridor wall, the glass meeting room, the shopfront door, the floor in front of a counter. Applied films change a space without joinery or repainting, and they can be updated for a new campaign or tenant without rebuilding anything.

This category covers the graphics we produce and install directly on building surfaces: full-wall murals, floor graphics with anti-slip laminate, frosted and decorative glass film, printed and cut glass stickers, and wayfinding graphics that guide people through offices, clinics, malls and car parks. Each surface needs its own film, adhesive and preparation, and choosing correctly matters as much as the print itself.

Choosing by surface, not by product

Start with where the graphic will go. Each surface has its own challenge: walls have paint that can lift, floors face foot traffic and cleaning machines, and glass deals with sunlight, condensation and constant touching. The right film is the one made for that surface, and we confirm stock and options in your quote.

  • Walls: printed wall vinyl or non-woven mural paper, matt laminated, applied to smooth cured paint
  • Floors: printed vinyl under a textured anti-slip laminate, chosen for the traffic and cleaning routine
  • Glass for privacy: frosted, dusted or gradient films that let light in and keep eyes out
  • Glass for messages: clear or white vinyl stickers, cut lettering and reverse-printed decals
  • Navigation: wall, floor and glass graphics combined with signs into one wayfinding system

What happens before anything is printed

Most application problems start before installation. Walls painted a week ago release solvents that make vinyl bubble; polished or waxed floors stop adhesive from gripping; glass cleaned with silicone-based products repels film. We ask about the surface, inspect it on site for larger jobs, and specify cleaning or priming where needed. On glass we also note whether panes are toughened, laminated or double-glazed, because darker or heavier films can cause heat stress on some sealed units in direct sun.

Measuring comes next. Glass is measured pane by pane, including mullions and door furniture, because panes that look identical often differ by a few millimetres. Walls are measured around sockets and switches, and floors are checked for joints, thresholds and slopes. Artwork is then built to those measurements with a small allowance for trimming in place.

Installation, change-over and removal

Our installers apply films at your premises in Dubai, dry or wet depending on the material. Glass films and large clear decals usually go on wet with a slip solution, which allows fine positioning and pushes out trapped air. Wall and floor vinyl go on dry, using air-release adhesives. Retail and mall work is normally booked before opening or after closing.

Many graphics in this category are temporary by design: a floor campaign for a product launch, window graphics for a sale, wayfinding for a one-off event. Removable adhesives and a planned removal visit leave surfaces clean for whatever comes next. The schedule depends on quantities, finishes and how ready the artwork is, and we confirm it with your quote before production starts.

FAQs

Wall, Floor & Glass Branding FAQs

For office privacy, should I choose frosted film or printed glass stickers?

Frosted film is the answer when privacy is the aim. It gives an even, etched-glass look across the pane or in a band at eye level, and logos can be cut into it. Printed glass stickers carry messages and branding such as opening hours, logos and offers. Many offices use both, with frosted bands on meeting rooms and printed decals on the entrance door.

How do floor graphics cope with foot traffic?

Floor graphics are sealed under a textured laminate made for walking on, which adds grip and protects the print from shoes and cleaning. If your venue or landlord specifies a slip-resistance rating, share it and we specify the laminate to that requirement. Graphics go on clean, dry, level floors, and in busy areas the edges should be checked regularly.

Can these graphics be removed without leaving damage?

Most films in this range come in removable-adhesive grades designed to peel away from glass, sealed floors and well-cured paint. Glass is the most forgiving surface; painted walls depend on the paint quality and age. We recommend removable grades for short campaigns and leased premises, and can return to take graphics down when the campaign or tenancy ends.

Do you install in malls and occupied buildings?

Yes. Mall and tower installations are usually scheduled outside trading or office hours, and many venues ask for a permit to work from their facility management team. Tell us the venue and we prepare the method statement and details they typically request, then work within the access times and site rules they set.
