About 3D letter signs

3D letter signs in Dubai range from cut acrylic letters on a clinic wall to large illuminated channel letters on a showroom parapet. What they share is construction: each letter is made individually, then fixed so the wall or cladding shows between them. That separation is why built-up lettering reads crisply from the road and looks more considered than a printed panel.

The main variables are face, return and light. Faces are usually opal or coloured acrylic on lit letters, and metal on non-lit or halo-lit letters. Returns, the sides of each letter, are formed from aluminium or stainless steel strip, or come from the thickness of solid acrylic. On front-lit letters the LED modules shine through the face; on halo-lit letters they wash onto the wall behind, with the letter held off the surface on stand-offs.

Size follows viewing distance. A common signmaker's guide allows around 25 mm of letter height for every 3 m at which the sign should be comfortable to read, so a name seen from 60 m across a car park needs letters of roughly 500 mm. We check proportions against your facade, the sign band and the landlord's guidelines before fabrication starts.

Materials

Acrylic face, aluminium returns

The classic lit channel letter: an opal or coloured acrylic face on a painted aluminium body, evenly lit and easy to match to brand colours.

Stainless steel

Brushed, mirror or gold-tone finishes for non-lit and halo-lit letters. Grade 316 is preferred near the coast, where 304 can tea-stain.

Solid acrylic

Letters cut from 10 to 30 mm acrylic, painted or vinyl-faced, for non-lit signs with clean edges and hidden fixings.

Powder-coated aluminium

Lightweight fabricated letters in any RAL colour, suited to very large signs where weight on the facade matters.

Finishing options

Brushed stainless

A fine grain that hides fingerprints and dust better than mirror, and looks consistent in shade or sun.

Mirror and gold-tone

Reflective finishes for hotels and luxury retail. They pick up their surroundings, so they read differently through the day.

Vinyl-faced acrylic

Translucent film over an opal face to hit brand colours closely, with the same colour by day and when lit.

Contrast returns

Returns painted a different colour from the face, often black, to give letters sharper definition in daylight.

3D letter signs FAQs

What is the difference between front-lit and halo-lit letters?

Front-lit letters have a translucent acrylic face, so the whole face glows and the name reads clearly from a distance at night. Halo-lit letters have an opaque face, usually metal, with light directed backwards onto the wall, creating a soft outline. Halo lighting looks refined but needs a light-coloured, even wall behind it to work well.

Stainless steel or acrylic: which holds up better outdoors?

Both are used outdoors. Stainless steel suits premium non-lit or halo-lit letters and weathers well when the right grade is chosen, with 316 preferred near the sea. Acrylic is needed wherever light must pass through the face, and UV-stable grades resist yellowing. The choice usually comes down to the look and lighting you want.

Can letters be fixed to glass or cladding?

Yes, with the method adjusted to the surface. On ACP cladding and render we use studs or stand-offs with fixings suited to the substrate. On glass, letters are usually mounted on a clear acrylic panel or bonded with suitable tape and silicone. Stone may need resin anchors. We check the surface during the site visit.

Do you work to landlord or authority requirements?

Yes. Many malls and buildings set rules on letter height, sign band position, colour and lighting, and authorities may add conditions for exterior signs. Send us the guidelines or permit drawings with your brief and we will produce to that specification. We can also supply scaled visuals and dimensions for your submission.
