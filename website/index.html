Why Flash Print

Gifts people keep, branded so the logo still looks right in a year

A corporate gift only works if the item is worth keeping and the branding survives being used. Most of the decisions that settle both are made before anything is decorated: the item, the surface, the method and how the logo is redrawn to suit it.

  1. 01

    The right method for each surface

    A curved plastic pen, a brushed metal USB casing, a PU diary cover and a ceramic mug each take a different process. We match them rather than forcing one method across the order.

  2. 02

    Your logo redrawn where it needs to be

    Engraving, debossing and embroidery need a simplified one-colour or stitchable version. If you only have a JPG, our design team redraws it as vector artwork first.

  3. 03

    Mixed orders kept straight

    For an order of several items we prepare one proof sheet showing every product with its logo size and position, so the whole set is approved in one go.

  4. 04

    Packed the way you will hand them out

    Bulk cartons for a warehouse, assembled sets for couriering, or individually boxed items for a stand. We pack to how the gifts actually leave your hands.

How it works

Four steps from gift list to delivered boxes

See the full process
  1. 1

    Share your list

    Send the items you have in mind, quantities per item, your logo file and the date the gifts are needed. A rough list is enough to start.

  2. 2

    We recommend & quote

    We confirm which items suit your logo, propose the branding method for each, check stock and colours, then send a written quote and schedule.

  3. 3

    You approve the proof

    A proof sheet shows your logo on every item at the correct size and position. Nothing is branded until you confirm it.

  4. 4

    We brand, pack & deliver

    Items are decorated, checked against the proof, packed to your instructions in bulk or as assembled sets, then delivered or collected.

FAQs

Questions about corporate gifts in Dubai

What corporate gifts can you brand in Dubai?

The core range covers ceramic and steel mugs, tumblers and bottles, dated and undated diaries and notebooks, metal and plastic pens, USB flash drives, power banks, keychains, caps, polos and T-shirts, and plaques and trophies. Printed gift boxes, sleeves, swing tags and cards bring several items together as one set. If you have another item in mind, send the details and we will confirm whether it can be decorated and how.

How is my logo applied to each gift item?

The method follows the surface. Ceramic mugs take full-colour sublimation, metal and wood are laser engraved, PU diary covers are debossed or foil stamped, caps and polos are embroidered, and curved plastic items such as pens and USB casings are pad printed. We recommend the method per item and show it on a proof before production.

What logo file do you need?

A vector file such as AI, EPS, SVG or PDF works best, because it scales cleanly down to small branding areas. Engraving, debossing and pad printing also need a one-colour version, and embroidery needs a logo simple enough to be stitched. If you only have a JPG or PNG, our design team can redraw it as vector artwork before production.

Can you add individual names to each gift?

Yes, for most items. Names can be laser engraved on metal keychains, USB drives and trophies, foil stamped on diary covers, embroidered on caps or printed on mugs. Send the names in a spreadsheet, checked for spelling and in the order you want them packed. We set one master layout and proof the list before production so every name can be checked.

How far ahead should we order Ramadan or year-end gifts?

As early as you can, because those seasons are busy for suppliers of blank stock as well as for decorators. Share a draft item list and quantities before final numbers are known so we can check stock and colours. Timelines depend on quantity, branding method and packaging, and we confirm the schedule with your quote before production starts.

Can you supply gift sets already boxed?

Yes. Items can be assembled into rigid or folding boxes with a fitted insert, finished with a printed sleeve, belly band or thank-you card, and packed either as ready-to-hand-out sets or in bulk cartons for your own distribution. Tell us whether the sets will be hand-delivered, couriered or given out from a stand, because that decides how much protection each box needs.

Do you only do corporate gifts?

No. Corporate gifts are one of our 26 service categories. The same team produces business stationery, marketing print, packaging, stickers and labels, indoor and outdoor signage, wall and vehicle graphics, exhibition booth building and apparel, along with design, fabrication and installation, so a whole campaign can come from one brief.
