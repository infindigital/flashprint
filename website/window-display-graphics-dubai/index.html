About window display graphics

Window display graphics in Dubai do much of the work a window dresser once did with props alone. In malls and on busy streets, the shopfront is the one piece of advertising a retailer fully controls, and every passer-by sees it. A strong window scheme tells people what is new, what is on offer and why they should step inside, and it changes with every campaign.

Most displays combine several layers. Graphics applied to the glass frame the window with headline messages, borders, partial coverage and decals. Behind the glass, suspended boards, standees and printed backdrops add depth and carry the product story. Where a stock room or back office needs screening, one-way vision film shows a full image outside while staff can still see out.

Unlike glass stickers or frosted film, which usually stay up for the long term, window displays are designed to be temporary. We use removable films and lightweight boards so the next campaign can go in without scraping or residue, and plan each change-over as a single visit: old graphics out, glass cleaned, new scheme in.

Materials

Removable printed window vinyl

Holds through a campaign and peels away from glass when the promotion ends.

One-way vision film

Perforated film printed on the outside face; from inside, its black backing lets staff and shoppers see out.

Foam board and PVC hanging boards

Lightweight panels printed on both sides, speaking to passers-by outside and shoppers inside.

Printed fabric backdrops

Tensioned fabric behind the glass gives a large, glare-free image with no adhesive on the window at all.

Finishing options

Reverse printing

Graphics applied inside the glass but reading correctly from the street, safe from cleaning and weather.

White underprint

Makes colours opaque on clear film so they hold their strength against bright daylight.

Contour cutting

Letters and shapes cut to their outline for an open, uncluttered window.

Double-sided printing

Hanging boards and decals that read from both the street and the shop floor.

Artwork checklist

  • Measure each pane separately, including only the visible glass inside the frame
  • Keep key messages between about 1,000 and 1,800 mm from the floor, at passing eye level
  • For one-way vision, avoid fine text and thin lines, which break up across the perforations
  • Supply separate artwork for each side of double-sided hanging boards
  • Share your mall's retail design guidelines so coverage and placement follow its rules

Window display graphics FAQs

How is a window display different from glass stickers?

Glass stickers are small, mostly long-term decals such as your logo, opening hours and push and pull signs. A window display is a temporary campaign built around the whole window, often combining large printed vinyl, hanging boards and backdrops. Window displays are planned for regular change-overs, while glass stickers usually stay until the brand or details change.

What is one-way vision film and when should I use it?

One-way vision is a perforated vinyl printed on the outside, with a black adhesive side facing inwards. From outside, people see the full graphic; from inside, the eye looks through the holes and sees out. It suits windows that need full coverage while keeping daylight and a view for staff, and it works best when it is brighter outside than in.

Can you change our window display without closing the store?

Yes. Change-overs are normally booked before opening or after closing, and in malls within the access times set by management. We remove the old graphics, clean the glass and install the new scheme in one visit, so the window is not left bare between campaigns and trading carries on as normal.

Do you produce window displays for several branches?

Yes. We create one master design and adapt it to each branch's window sizes from site measurements or drawings. Graphics are packed and labelled by store and pane, which keeps installation straightforward, and we can schedule installation across your Dubai branches as a planned sequence.

Will window graphics block too much light?

That depends on the coverage and the film. Solid printed vinyl blocks light completely, so we usually leave clear areas or use one-way vision where daylight matters. Many malls also limit how much of the glass can be covered, so a balanced design keeps the product visible while the graphics frame it.
