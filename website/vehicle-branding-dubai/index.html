A branded vehicle carries your name through every junction, car park and loading bay it visits. Our vehicle branding in Dubai covers the full range, from a single set of door logos to a complete colour-change wrap and one consistent livery across a mixed fleet of saloons, vans and light trucks.

Every job starts with the vehicle itself: make, model, body shape, paint condition and how it is used day to day. That tells us which film to specify, how much of the body to cover and where graphics must avoid door gaps, handles and parking sensors. We handle design adaptation, printing, lamination and installation, and we keep the master files so a replacement panel after a minor accident matches the rest of the vehicle.

Four ways to brand a vehicle

The right option depends on how much of the vehicle people actually see, how long the branding needs to stay on and how many vehicles are involved. Most companies combine two or more: a partial wrap on service vans, discreet door logos on management cars and reflective markings on anything that works after dark.

Coverage is the biggest decision. More film means more visual impact, but also more installation time and more material to remove when the vehicle is sold or the lease ends. We walk around the vehicle with you, on site or through photos, and suggest the areas that earn their place.

  • Full vehicle wrapping: every painted panel covered in printed or colour-change cast film, for maximum presence from one vehicle
  • Partial wraps: selected panels, often the rear half or the sides, with the design blending into the existing paint
  • Fleet branding: one livery adapted across different makes and models, with colour recorded and controlled between batches
  • Reflective stickers: chevrons, contour strips, fleet numbers and logos that return headlamp light at night
  • Car stickers: cut vinyl lettering, contact details and small logos for doors and rear glass

Films, laminates and working in Dubai heat

Cast vinyl is the standard film for wraps. It is thin, conforms to curves and recesses, and holds its shape when the panel heats up. Calendered film is thicker and stiffer: polymeric grades work well for lettering and flat van sides, while basic monomeric film is kept for short promotions on flat surfaces. Printed film is always overlaminated to protect the ink from UV, grit and car-wash brushes.

Heat changes how film behaves. Vinyl applied to a panel that has been standing in the sun traps air and over-stretches at the edges, so installation takes place in shade or under cover, and recessed areas are post-heated to set the film. Once the vehicle is back on the road, shaded parking and hand washing do more for a wrap's appearance than any product applied afterwards.

Preparation, permits and planning

Vehicles should arrive clean and dry, without wax, polish or a recent ceramic coating on the panels being covered, because all three reduce adhesion. We note chipped, rusted or resprayed areas before starting, since film will show surface defects and refinished paint can lift during later removal. Badges, mirror caps and lights may be removed and refitted so the film wraps behind edges.

Commercial advertising on vehicles in Dubai may need a permit. Arranging it is the vehicle owner's responsibility; we can supply proofs and dimensions for your application and then produce the graphics to the approved design. To prepare a quote we usually ask for:

  • Make, model, year and body type of each vehicle
  • Straight-on photos of both sides, the front, the rear and the roof
  • Brand guidelines and vector logo files
  • When and where the vehicles can be made available

FAQs

Vehicle Branding FAQs

Should my business choose a full wrap or a partial wrap?

A full wrap suits a single hero vehicle or a colour change, where the whole body becomes the advert. A partial wrap delivers large, readable branding on the panels people see most, such as van sides and rear doors, with less film and installation time. Many fleets use partial wraps as standard and keep full wraps for launch or showroom vehicles.

Can vehicle graphics be removed when we sell or return the vehicle?

Yes. Wrap films and quality vehicle vinyls are made to be removable from sound, factory-cured paint. Removal is cleanest when the film has not been left far beyond its intended service life and the paint has not been resprayed. We also offer sticker and wrap removal as a separate service, including adhesive residue cleaning.

Can the vehicle be wrapped at our premises?

Often, if you have a covered, clean area out of direct sun, wind and dust, with power and enough space to open every door. Wrapping outdoors in Dubai heat risks poor adhesion and trapped debris. If your site is not suitable, we agree an alternative covered location with you in the quote.

Do you design the vehicle graphics or do we need an agency?

We can do either. Our design team can adapt your existing brand to each vehicle using accurate model outlines, or build a new livery from your logo and colours. If your agency supplies the design, we check it against the vehicle template for door gaps, handles and bleed before anything is printed.
