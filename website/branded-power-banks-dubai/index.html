About branded power banks

Branded power banks in Dubai sit near the top of most technology gift lists because they get used daily rather than put in a drawer. The decision comes down to capacity, how the device connects and what the casing is made of, and those three choices also decide how your logo can be applied.

Capacity is quoted in milliamp hours. A 5,000 mAh bank gives roughly one phone charge and stays pocket-sized, 10,000 mAh is the usual middle ground for a working day, and 20,000 mAh suits travel and site teams who top up a laptop or several devices. Magnetic wireless models clip onto the back of a compatible phone and charge without a lead, while cabled models carry a short built-in USB-C or Lightning cable so nothing has to be carried separately.

Casings change the branding method. Aluminium and anodised bodies are laser engraved for a permanent tone-on-tone mark. ABS plastic takes UV print in full colour, and a light-up option backlights the logo through the casing while the unit is in use. Cork, bamboo and recycled plastic shells suit sustainability-led ranges and print or engrave well.

Materials

Anodised aluminium

Hard-wearing metal shell in silver, space grey or black. Engraves to a bright mark and resists scuffing in a bag.

ABS plastic

Moulded casing that takes full-colour UV print across the whole face, and is the base for light-up logo models.

Cork

Natural cork skin bonded to the shell. Warm to hold, prints in one colour and pairs with bamboo notebooks in a set.

Recycled plastic

Post-consumer plastic housings with the same output as standard models, used where the gift has to carry a recycled content figure.

Finishing options

UV print

Full-colour flat print straight onto the casing, including gradients and photographic detail.

Laser engraving

Permanent mark cut into metal or bamboo, showing as a lighter tone against the body colour.

Light-up logo

Your logo cut into the casing and backlit, so it glows softly while the power bank is charging a device.

Doming

Printed insert sealed under a clear resin dome, giving a raised, glossy badge on the face.

Artwork checklist

  • Send a vector logo (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) sized to the branding panel
  • Supply a one-colour version for engraving and for light-up casings
  • Full-colour UV print needs artwork at 300 dpi with a 2 mm clear margin
  • Avoid very fine hairlines on light-up logos; thin cuts do not carry the light evenly
  • Tell us the casing colour early, since a light engraving on a pale body has less contrast

Branded power banks FAQs

What capacity power bank should we order?

For delegate bags and general giveaways, 5,000 mAh keeps the unit pocket-sized and gives about one phone charge. For staff and client gifts, 10,000 mAh covers a full working day with something in reserve. Choose 20,000 mAh only for travel or site teams, because the unit is noticeably heavier and costs more per person.

Do magnetic wireless power banks work with every phone?

They attach magnetically to phones built for magnetic accessories, and to other phones fitted with a magnetic case or ring. On a phone without magnets they will still charge over the cable port, but the magnetic hold will not work. If your recipients use a mix of handsets, a cabled model is the safer choice.

How is a light-up logo produced?

Your logo is cut into the casing during production and an internal light shines through it, so the mark glows while the unit is charging. It works best with a solid, simple shape and a reasonable stroke weight. We review your artwork against the casing first and will suggest a simplified version if the detail is too fine.

Can power banks be shipped by air?

Lithium batteries are classed as dangerous goods and carriers apply specific rules on capacity, packing and labelling. It affects how stock is moved rather than anything you have to arrange. Tell us where the order is going at the brief stage so the route and packing can be confirmed in writing with your quote.
