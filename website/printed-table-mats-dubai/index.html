About printed table mats

Printed table mats in Dubai do two jobs at once: they protect the table or tray, and they give the outlet a large, captive advertising space. Burger and shawarma counters use tray liners to promote meal deals and apps; family restaurants print colouring activities for children; hotel breakfast rooms use mats to highlight the spa or other outlets in the property.

The main decision is disposable or reusable. Disposable mats are printed on uncoated or lightly coated paper, typically 80 to 120 gsm, and replaced after each guest. They can be supplied flat in packs or, on request, glued into tear-off pads that staff keep at the service station. Reusable mats are made from laminated board or synthetic sheet, wiped between services and replaced when worn.

If food will sit directly on the paper, as it often does on tray liners, tell us at the brief stage so we can discuss suitable papers and inks for that use. For long-running designs ordered in large volumes, offset printing is usually the sensible route; digital printing suits test runs, seasonal versions and short promotions.

Materials

Uncoated paper

Soft, absorbent and easy to write on, which makes it the natural choice for colouring mats and disposable placemats.

Lightly coated paper

Brighter colours and sharper photos than uncoated stock, while staying light enough to throw away after use.

Laminated board

Rigid, wipeable mats for cafés and breakfast rooms that are cleaned between guests.

Synthetic sheet

Flexible polypropylene that resists water and tearing, for terraces and reusable children's mats.

Finishing options

Matt lamination

Keeps reusable mats wipeable while avoiding glare from pendant lights over the table.

Rounded corners

A neater edge on reusable mats that are stacked, wiped and set out many times.

Tear-off padding

Mats glued along one edge into pads, so staff can tear off a fresh sheet at each setting.

Artwork checklist

  • Supply files at final size with 3 mm bleed, and keep text at least 10 mm from the trimmed edge
  • Plates usually cover the centre, so place key messages around the border
  • For colouring mats, use clean black outlines and leave large open areas to fill in
  • On uncoated paper colours print slightly softer, so avoid fine text reversed out of dark backgrounds
  • Size QR codes generously and test-scan them on the chosen paper before approving the proof

Printed table mats FAQs

What size should a table mat be?

A3 (297 × 420 mm) is a common placemat size and fits most restaurant tables with room for cutlery. Tray liners are sized to the tray, so measure the inside of your trays or send us one. Smaller tables or bistro settings may suit a narrower custom size. We can produce a sample to check against your table setting.

Can food sit directly on a paper table mat?

Most placemats are designed to sit under plates rather than carry food. Tray liners in burger and shawarma outlets often do touch food directly, so paper and ink choice matters more there. Tell us how the mats will be used and we will discuss suitable options with you before printing.

Can we have different designs in one order?

Yes. Many outlets run a children's activity mat alongside a standard adult mat, or rotate seasonal designs. Versions can be printed together in one order, with quantities split between them. Digital printing handles several versions in small quantities easily, while offset suits larger runs of each design.

How many disposable mats should we order?

Work from your covers per day. A restaurant serving 150 covers a day uses around 1,050 mats a week, plus extras for spillage and staff use. Add storage space and how often the design will change, then choose a quantity that lasts a sensible period without leaving you with old stock after a menu update.

Do reusable mats make sense for our restaurant?

They suit cafés, breakfast rooms and casual dining where mats are wiped between guests and the design stays the same for months. They cost more per mat than paper but are used many times. Disposable mats suit high-turnover outlets, frequent promotions and tray service, where a fresh sheet for every guest is expected.
