About promotional bags

Promotional bags in Dubai do more work than most giveaways because they are seen by people who never attended your event. A tote leaving an exhibition hall, a jute shopper going into a supermarket and a branded backpack on a commute all put the logo in front of a second audience, which is why the fabric and the print method are worth choosing carefully.

Cotton and canvas totes are the default for retail and events. Weight is quoted in gsm: a 140 gsm cotton bag is light and inexpensive for large runs, while 280 to 340 gsm canvas feels substantial and holds its shape on a shelf. Jute shoppers with a laminated lining sit at the gift end of the range. Non-woven polypropylene is the lightest option for high-volume handouts, and RPET fabrics made from recycled bottles give the same look with a recycled content story.

Beyond shoppers, the range runs into working bags. Laptop sleeves and padded briefcases, roll-top and commuter backpacks, gym duffels, toiletry pouches and document folders all take a discreet logo, usually a woven label or an embroidered mark rather than a large print. These are the bags that end up in staff welcome kits and long-service gifts.

Materials

Cotton canvas

Woven natural fibre with a matt surface that takes screen printing cleanly. Heavier weights stand up on their own and feel like a retail bag.

Jute

Coarse natural fibre with a visible weave and a laminated inner face. Prints well in one or two colours and reads as a gift rather than a handout.

Non-woven polypropylene

Bonded plastic fibre that is light, water-resistant and inexpensive at volume. The usual pick for exhibition and supermarket handouts.

RPET polyester

Fabric spun from recycled drinks bottles. Looks and behaves like standard polyester, with a recycled content figure you can quote in your campaign.

Finishing options

Screen printing

Ink pushed through a mesh, one screen per colour. The most economical method on cotton and jute for large runs of a flat-colour logo.

DTF transfer

Full-colour print released from a film under heat. Handles photographs and gradients that screen printing would need too many screens for.

Embroidery

Stitched logo on canvas, backpack panels and pockets. Adds texture and holds up on bags that are handled daily.

Woven label

Separate label stitched to the panel or strap, holding finer detail than direct stitching and giving a retail finish.

Where it’s used

  • Exhibition and conference delegate bags
  • Retail and supermarket reusable shoppers
  • Staff welcome kits with a laptop bag or backpack
  • Gym, sports club and wellness campaign giveaways
  • Hotel and spa guest bags and laundry pouches

Industries we support with promotional bags: Retail, Hotels & Hospitality, Schools & Education, Real Estate.

Why choose us for promotional bags

  • Fabric picked for the run

    We match weight and material to the quantity and the setting, so a delegate handout and a retail shopper are not quoted as the same bag.

  • Print method costed properly

    Screen, transfer and embroidery each suit different artwork and quantities, and we explain the trade-off before you commit.

  • Matched to the rest of the kit

    Bags can be produced with t-shirts, caps and lanyards so colours and logo placement line up across the whole set.

Artwork checklist

  • Send a vector logo (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) at the size it should print on the bag panel
  • Give a spot-colour breakdown with Pantone references for screen printing
  • Keep fine lines above 0.5 mm; coarse jute and canvas weaves lose thinner strokes
  • Supply full-colour or photographic artwork at 300 dpi if you want a DTF transfer
  • Confirm handle length and colour with the artwork, since it changes how the printed panel sits

Promotional bags FAQs

What cotton weight should we choose for a tote bag?

For a large handout run, 140 to 180 gsm keeps the cost and the bag weight down. For retail or a gift, 280 to 340 gsm canvas is worth the difference because it holds its shape, carries more and feels closer to a shop bag. If the bag is going into a delegate pack with heavy print material, choose the heavier weight.

Can you print a full-colour photograph on a bag?

Yes, using a DTF transfer rather than screen printing. The artwork is printed onto a film and pressed onto the fabric, so gradients and photographic detail come through. Screen printing is still better value for a flat one or two colour logo at volume, because each colour needs its own screen but then prints at speed.

Are recycled fabric bags available?

Yes. RPET fabric is spun from recycled plastic bottles and is used for shoppers, backpacks and pouches. Recycled cotton and jute are also options at the natural-fibre end of the range. We can confirm the recycled content figure for the specific fabric you choose so your campaign material quotes an accurate number.

Do you supply laptop bags and backpacks as well as totes?

Yes. The range includes padded laptop sleeves and briefcases, commuter and roll-top backpacks, gym duffels, crossbody bags and toiletry pouches. These usually take a discreet woven label or an embroidered logo instead of a large print. They are commonly ordered for staff welcome kits and long-service gifts rather than event handouts.
