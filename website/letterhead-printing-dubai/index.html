About letterhead printing

Letterhead printing in Dubai has a practical requirement that many buyers only discover afterwards: the sheets have to go back through an office printer. We print letterheads on uncoated papers, with inks and methods chosen to cope with laser fusers and inkjet printers, so your quotations and letters print cleanly on top of the pre-printed design.

A typical UAE letterhead carries the company logo, registered name, address, P.O. Box, phone and website, and often the trade licence number and Tax Registration Number (TRN). Many firms add an Arabic version of the company name. We lay these details out so the writing area stays generous and the footer does not clash with standard Word margins.

For larger quantities with a brand colour, offset printing with a Pantone match gives the most consistent result across reorders. Short runs and variations for different branches suit digital printing. Continuation sheets carrying only a small logo, and DL compliment slips, can be printed alongside so the set matches.

Materials

100 gsm uncoated bond

The everyday office weight. Feeds reliably through most laser and inkjet printers and folds neatly into DL envelopes.

120 gsm uncoated

Slightly heavier and more opaque, so printing on the reverse does not show through. A good choice for contracts and formal letters.

Laid paper

Fine parallel lines in the surface give a traditional, formal character favoured by legal and consultancy firms.

Linen-textured paper

A subtle woven surface with a crisp feel. Test a few sheets in your office printer before ordering a full run.

Finishing options

Uncoated natural finish

The standard choice. No lamination or varnish, so the sheet accepts toner, ink and pen signatures without smudging.

Blind embossing

A raised logo pressed into the paper without ink. Best kept small and away from the edge that enters the printer first.

Pantone spot colour

Printing your brand colour as a mixed ink rather than CMYK keeps it consistent across letterheads, envelopes and reorders.

Artwork checklist

  • Supply a PDF at A4 with 3 mm bleed if any colour runs to the edge
  • Leave a writing area that matches your Word margins, usually 20–25 mm at the sides
  • Specify Pantone references if you want an exact spot-colour match
  • Keep footer text at 7 pt or above so it survives photocopying and scanning
  • Send the Arabic company name as editable text in the correct font, not a screenshot
  • Mark which elements belong on the continuation sheet, usually just the logo

Letterhead printing FAQs

Will pre-printed letterheads work in my laser printer?

In most cases, yes. We use uncoated papers and print methods suited to overprinting, and avoid lamination or heavy varnish that can smear or cause feed problems. Very heavy textured papers and embossing can be less reliable in some printers, so we suggest testing a few sample sheets before you commit to a large run.

Should my letterhead include the TRN?

Many VAT-registered businesses in the UAE include their Tax Registration Number on letterheads and commercial documents for clarity. What you must show on specific documents, such as tax invoices, is a matter for your accountant or tax adviser. We will add whichever registration details you provide and lay them out neatly in the header or footer.

Do you print continuation sheets and compliment slips?

Yes. Continuation sheets carry a smaller version of your logo for the second and later pages of long letters and contracts. Compliment slips are usually DL size, 99 × 210 mm, and are handy for sending brochures or samples with a short note. Printing them in the same run as your letterheads keeps colour and paper matched.

Is offset or digital printing better for letterheads?

Digital suits smaller quantities, several branch addresses or frequently changing details. Offset suits larger runs, particularly when you need an exact Pantone brand colour that must match reorders and your envelopes. For letterhead printing we recommend a method in your quote based on quantity, colours and how often your details change.

Can you create a Word letterhead template as well?

Yes, as a design service. We can build a Word template that mirrors the printed letterhead, with the logo placed correctly and margins set for your documents. Staff can then produce PDF letters that look consistent with the printed stationery when sending by email. The template is quoted separately from printing.
