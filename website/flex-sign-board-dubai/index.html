Flex Sign Boards, Frontlit and Backlit

A flex sign board is a printed PVC flex face stretched over a fabricated metal frame. It covers large areas with one full-colour graphic, can be lit from inside with LED modules or floodlit from the front, and the face can be reprinted later without rebuilding the frame.

Construction
Flex face on GI or aluminium frame
Lighting
Non-lit, floodlit or backlit LED
Suited to
Large faces and changing messages

About flex sign board

A flex sign board in Dubai is the practical choice when a sign has to be large, colourful and full of imagery: supermarket and furniture showroom fronts, warehouse frontages, petrol station forecourt boards or rooftop signs seen from a highway. Unlike 3D letters or routed ACP, the whole message is printed, so photographs, gradients and multi-coloured logos are no harder to reproduce than a plain name.

The frame does the structural work. It is usually galvanised iron (GI) box section for large outdoor signs, or aluminium where weight matters, clad at the sides with ACP or painted sheet to form a neat box. The flex is then tensioned across the face, either laced through eyelets onto the frame or clamped in an aluminium profile that grips the edge and pulls it tight.

For a lit board, backlit flex is used with LED modules fixed to a back panel inside the box, spaced so the face lights evenly. Frontlit boards use standard flex with LED floodlights on arms. Because the face is replaceable, the same frame can carry a new campaign or a new tenant's name. If you only need a loose banner rather than a framed sign, see our flex banner printing page.

Materials

Backlit flex face

A translucent flex that spreads light from internal LEDs across the whole face, used for illuminated boxes.

Frontlit flex face

Opaque flex for non-lit or floodlit boards, giving denser colour in daylight.

Galvanised iron frame

Strong box-section framing for large and high-level signs, galvanised and painted to resist rust in humid conditions.

Aluminium profile frame

A lighter extruded frame with a clamping edge, neater for mid-sized shop signs and easier face changes.

Finishing options

Clamp profile edge

The flex is gripped in a slotted aluminium extrusion, so no eyelets or lacing show from the front.

Side cladding

Box sides finished in ACP or painted sheet, in a colour that matches the facade or brand.

LED floodlights

Slim floodlights on projecting arms, so a frontlit face stays visible after dark.

  • Rooftop and highway-facing signs
  • Furniture and car showroom fascias
  • Pole and pylon signs

Why choose us for flex sign board

  • Frames built to the site

    We size frame sections for the board's dimensions and exposure, and check the wall or roof it fixes to.

  • Even lighting layout

    LED module spacing is planned for the box depth, to avoid bright spots and dark bands on the face.

  • Face changes made simple

    Reprinted faces are made to fit your existing frame, avoiding a full rebuild when the message changes.

Artwork checklist

  • Design to the visible face size; we add tension allowance after measuring the frame
  • 30 to 75 ppi at full size is sufficient for boards read from the road
  • Choose bold sans-serif type; thin fonts break up at distance
  • Keep key text out of the outer 75 mm, which clamps or profiles may cover
  • On backlit boards, light areas glow and dark areas stay dark at night, so check the design reads both ways

Flex sign board FAQs

How does a flex sign board differ from an ACP sign board?

A flex board is a printed fabric face stretched on a frame, so the entire design is printed and the face can be lit evenly from inside. An ACP board is a rigid aluminium panel, usually carrying cut vinyl, routed or 3D letters, with a more permanent, architectural look. Flex suits very large faces and imagery; ACP suits crisp lettering.

Can a flex sign board be illuminated?

Yes, two ways. A backlit board has LED modules inside the box shining through translucent backlit flex, which gives an even glow across the whole face. A frontlit board uses opaque flex lit by LED floodlights mounted on arms above or below. Backlit boards look cleaner; floodlit boards can be simpler to build on very large faces.

Can you replace only the printed face?

Yes, and it is one of the main advantages of this type of sign. We measure the frame, check that it is sound and print a new face with the right edge finish for lacing or a clamp profile. Rusted sections, loose fixings or failed LEDs are worth repairing at the same visit.

How does the board cope with wind?

The frame and fixings carry the load, so section sizes, anchors and bracing are chosen for the board's size and height. Tension profiles keep the face tight so it does not flap. On very exposed rooftops we may recommend extra bracing or a structural check by a qualified engineer, especially where your landlord asks for one.

Is flex suitable for a long-term sign?

Flex is widely used for long-term outdoor signs in the UAE, but it is a printed surface and will fade gradually under strong sun, with reds and yellows usually going first. Many owners plan to reprint the face periodically, which is straightforward on this type of board. For fewer printed elements, consider ACP with 3D letters.
