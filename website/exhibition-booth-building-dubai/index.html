Exhibition Booth Building in Dubai and Stand Fabrication

Booth building covers the physical stand itself: the structure, walls, counters, flooring, lighting and storage that your graphics are fitted to. We design the booth, fabricate it in the workshop, build it on site during the organiser's build-up window and take it down after the show closes.

About exhibition booth building

Exhibition booth building in Dubai runs to the venue's calendar rather than yours. Halls at the World Trade Centre, Expo City and Dubai Festival Arena publish build-up and breakdown windows in the exhibitor manual, along with rules on height, rigging, hall floor loading and contractor passes. Everything we design and fabricate is planned backwards from those dates and those rules, so the structure that arrives on site can be installed in the hours available.

The first decision is how the booth is built. A custom stand is drawn for your space and made from timber, MDF and aluminium profile in the workshop, painted or laminated, then assembled in the hall. It gives you control over shape, height, storage and branding, and it suits an anchor stand you want people to remember. A modular stand uses a reusable aluminium frame system with interchangeable infill panels, which packs into crates, travels between shows and can be reconfigured when your stand size changes from one exhibition to the next.

A booth also has to work as a place to do business for several days. Visitors need a clear way in, staff need somewhere to store bags, brochures and stock, and demonstrations need power, data and a surface at the right height. We plan the layout around those working needs first, then apply the branding, so meeting tables, product plinths, screen positions and the lockable store room are settled before any panel is cut.

Materials

Timber and MDF joinery

The usual structure for a custom stand. Panels are cut, assembled, filled and spray-painted or laminated in the workshop, which gives clean corners, curved walls and built-in furniture.

Aluminium profile systems

Extruded aluminium frames bolted into walls, gantries and towers. They dismantle into crates, are reusable across shows and carry either printed infill or fabric.

Tension fabric on frames

Dye-sublimated fabric with a silicone edge stretched into an aluminium frame. Light to ship, quiet to install, and the graphic can be changed without touching the frame.

Acrylic and laminates

Used for counter tops, lit logo blocks, shelves and product risers. Acrylic can be edge-lit or back-lit, and laminates keep heavy-traffic surfaces looking clean through the show.

Platform decking and carpet

A raised floor hides cabling and defines the stand edge. Carpet, vinyl plank or printed floor vinyl is laid on top, with an access ramp where the platform is used.

Finishing options

Spray paint

Matte or satin paint mixed to your brand colour, applied to joinery before delivery so the stand arrives finished.

Cut and built-up lettering

Acrylic or foam letters fixed proud of the wall, with optional halo or face illumination for the main name.

Backlit panels

Translucent fabric or opal acrylic lit from behind, used on feature walls and fascias so the booth reads from across the hall.

Vinyl and fabric infill

Printed graphics applied to wall panels or slotted into frames, changed between shows while the structure stays.

Stand brief checklist

  • Send the exhibitor manual, the hall floor plan and your stand number and dimensions
  • Confirm which sides are open, since that decides the entrance and where walls can go
  • List what has to fit on the stand: products, screens, meeting seats, storage and stock
  • Note the maximum build height and any rigging or double-decker submission the organiser requires
  • Supply logos as vector files and brand colours as Pantone references for paint matching
  • Tell us the power, lighting and data points you have booked with the venue
  • Share the build-up and breakdown times printed in the manual so the plan works back from them

Exhibition booth building FAQs

What does exhibition booth building include?

It covers the stand as a physical structure: concept design and 3D visuals, technical drawings, workshop fabrication of walls, counters and furniture, flooring, lighting, delivery to the hall, build-up, on-site supervision and dismantle after the show. Printed graphics are produced alongside it. We confirm the exact scope in the quote so nothing is assumed.

What is the difference between a custom and a modular booth?

A custom booth is designed for one space and built from joinery, so shape, height and built-in furniture are open choices. A modular booth uses a reusable aluminium frame with interchangeable panels that crate up and travel between exhibitions. Custom suits a headline stand, while modular suits companies exhibiting several times a year in different stand sizes.

Do you handle build-up and dismantle at the venue?

Yes. We deliver to the hall, build the stand within the organiser's build-up window, and return at breakdown to dismantle and clear the space. Contractor passes, hall access times and working-at-height requirements are arranged with the organiser beforehand, and we build to the specification the organiser or venue provides.

Can we reuse the booth at the next exhibition?

Modular structures are designed for reuse, and much of a custom stand can be reused if the next space is a similar size and shape. Graphics are usually the part that changes. We can store crated stand elements between shows and reprint only the panels that need new artwork, which is worth planning at the design stage.

How far in advance should we start a booth build?

Start as soon as your space is confirmed and the exhibitor manual is issued. Design, client approval, organiser submissions and fabrication all have to fit before the build-up date, and organisers often need stand drawings submitted well ahead. Timelines depend on stand size, build type and approvals, and we confirm the schedule with your quote.

Do you design the booth, or do we supply a design?

Either works. Send a brief with your stand size, open sides, budget guidance and what has to happen on the stand, and we design it and present 3D visuals. If your agency has already designed the booth, we can build from their drawings, checking dimensions, materials and fixings with them before fabrication starts.
