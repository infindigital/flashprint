A construction project needs printed material from the day the hoarding goes up until handover. Flash Print Solution handles construction site printing in Dubai for developers, main contractors, fit-out companies and project managers: hoarding that screens the works and promotes the development, project sign boards that identify the parties involved, and entrance safety boards and directional signs that move people and deliveries around safely.

Site graphics sit in sun, dust and wind for months, get moved as phases change and often need updating when contractors or messages change. We plan materials and fixings with that in mind, work from your site layout and coordinate installation with your site team. Content that must follow an authority, master developer or consultant requirement is produced exactly as set out in the documents you supply.

What each site item does

The four products in this range share materials but not purpose, and briefing them separately keeps drawings and sign-offs simpler.

  • Hoarding graphics: large printed runs on the perimeter hoarding for development branding, renders, safety messages and screening
  • Project sign boards: the framed identification board at the entrance naming the project, developer, contractor and consultants
  • Construction safety boards: entrance boards showing required PPE, site rules, emergency contacts and the assembly point
  • Directional site signage: gate, route, parking, welfare and delivery signs that guide people and vehicles through the site

Materials for sites that change every month

For long hoarding runs, PVC banner or mesh is tensioned onto the hoarding frame, or printed vinyl is applied directly to plywood or metal panels. Mesh lets wind pass through, which helps on tall or exposed hoardings. Where the hoarding is rigid and smooth, vinyl gives a cleaner finish and can be trimmed neatly around gates and service doors.

Framed boards are usually 3 mm ACP on a steel or aluminium frame, while temporary directional signs often use 4 to 5 mm corrugated polypropylene that can be cable-tied to fencing and moved when the layout changes. Outdoor-grade inks and UV-resistant laminates help reduce fading under strong sun. General workplace safety signs, reflective traffic signs and equipment warning labels come from our safety and industrial range and are often ordered alongside.

Coordinating with the site team

Most site jobs start from a site plan or hoarding elevation. We measure on site or work from your dimensions, prepare panel layouts that allow for gates, columns and joints, and send proofs showing where every panel falls. Installation is scheduled around your permit-to-work, access and safety induction requirements.

Packages are often phased. Hoarding and entrance boards go in before mobilisation, directional signs follow once the logistics layout settles, and replacements come as phases change. Splitting the work this way lets each part be checked by the right person, from the developer's marketing team to the project manager or HSE officer. Timelines depend on quantities, fabrication and artwork readiness, and we confirm the schedule with your quote before production.

  • Hoarding elevations or a site layout with dimensions
  • Vector logos for every party named on boards
  • Wording and symbols for safety boards from your HSE team
  • Access times, permit and induction requirements for installers

FAQs

Construction & Site Printing FAQs

Can you install hoarding graphics and boards on site?

Yes. Our installation team fits hoarding graphics, framed boards and signs at construction sites in Dubai. We need the site address, access arrangements, working hours, any permit-to-work and induction requirements, and the name of your site contact. Large framed boards may need lifting equipment or footings, which we discuss at the quote stage.

Do hoarding designs or project boards need approval?

Some developments, master communities and authorities set rules for hoarding content, colours or board layout. Obtaining any permission is the responsibility of the developer, contractor or consultant. We produce the design they have signed off and can supply proofs or scaled mock-ups for their submission if that helps.

Which material suits a short-term site?

For signs that move often, corrugated polypropylene is light and easy to cable-tie. For hoarding runs, mesh or PVC banner suits frame-mounted hoarding, and vinyl suits smooth rigid panels. Boards that stay for the whole project are better on ACP with a steel frame. We suggest options once we know the duration and location.

Can you follow the developer's brand guidelines?

Yes. Developers often issue guidelines for hoarding and site boards covering fonts, colours, logo placement and how renders may be used. Send the guidelines with your files and we set up artwork to follow them, flagging anything that will not work on a particular material or panel size before proofs go out.
