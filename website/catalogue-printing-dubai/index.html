About catalogue printing

Catalogue printing in Dubai supports the trading, retail and distribution businesses that sell from a range: building materials, spare parts, furniture, fashion, cosmetics, foodservice supplies and more. A catalogue is a working tool. Buyers flip back to it, mark pages and compare codes, so clear structure, accurate colour and a durable cover matter more than in a one-off brochure.

Binding depends on page count and use. Saddle stitching suits catalogues up to around 48–64 pages. Perfect binding gives a square spine that can carry the title and suits thicker catalogues, while wire-o binding lets spec books and trade guides lie flat on a desk or counter. Cover stocks of 250–350 gsm with matt or gloss lamination protect against wear.

Product photography has to reproduce consistently from page to page, so we check colour set-up, image resolution and black text before proofing. Bilingual catalogues need extra planning: Arabic editions open from the right, which reverses page order and spine placement. We confirm this at the start so the layout is built the right way round.

Artwork checklist

  • Supply single pages in reading order with 3 mm bleed, not printer spreads
  • Ask us for the spine width before finalising a perfect-bound cover
  • Keep text 10–12 mm from the spine on perfect-bound catalogues, as part of the gutter is lost to binding
  • Package InDesign files with fonts and links, or export a PDF/X-1a
  • Convert RGB product images to CMYK with one consistent profile
  • Confirm the page count is a multiple of 4 for stapled catalogues

Catalogue printing FAQs

Should my catalogue be stapled or perfect bound?

Stapled binding suits catalogues up to around 48–64 pages, depending on paper weight, and opens flat easily. Perfect binding gives a square spine with room for the title and suits thicker editions, though it opens less flat near the spine. If the catalogue will be used on a counter or desk, wire-o binding is worth considering too.

How many pages should a catalogue have?

That depends on your range and how buyers use it. Stapled catalogues need a multiple of four pages. Perfect-bound catalogues are more flexible, but page counts in multiples of four or eight impose most efficiently. We can review your product list and suggest a page plan that avoids blank pages at the end.

Can you print a catalogue in Arabic and English?

Yes. You can print two separate editions, a combined bilingual layout on each page, or a flip format where each language starts from its own cover. Arabic editions open from the right, so the spine and page order are reversed. Tell us your preference early, as it affects the page plan, spine and cover design.

Can we update prices without reprinting the whole catalogue?

A common approach is to keep prices out of the main catalogue and print a separate price list or insert, which can be updated as often as needed. Digital printing makes short runs of updated inserts practical. We can size inserts to sit in a cover pocket or loose inside the catalogue.

Do you print short runs of catalogues?

Yes. Digital catalogue printing makes small editions practical for sales teams, exhibitions or a trial range. Larger runs are usually produced offset for consistent colour across every copy. We recommend a method in your quote based on page count, binding and quantity, and explain where the switch point sits for your job.
