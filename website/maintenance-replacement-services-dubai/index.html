Signs and graphics in Dubai work hard. Strong sun fades inks and yellows acrylic, heat softens adhesives, sand and wind scuff surfaces, and busy doors and counters take knocks every day. Signage maintenance in Dubai is about keeping that branding looking the way it was intended, by replacing what has worn out rather than living with a tired shopfront or a peeling window.

This service line covers the work that comes after the original installation: reprinting and replacing damaged signage components, stripping old stickers and adhesive before new graphics go up, rolling out a new brand identity across every location and asset, and preparing files so replacements match what is already in place. Each job starts with an inspection or clear photos, so the scope is agreed before anything is removed.

Common signs of wear and what replacement involves

Most maintenance requests fall into a few patterns. Printed flex faces on light boxes fade and turn patchy, with reds and yellows usually going first. Vinyl lettering on glass shrinks and cracks at the edges. Acrylic faces yellow or craze, 3D letters lose returns or trim caps, and window graphics on south- and west-facing shopfronts bleach unevenly.

Replacement is often simpler than a new sign. A light box frame that is structurally sound can take a new printed face, individual letters can be remade to the original drawing, and a single faded window panel can be reprinted to sit alongside its neighbours. We inspect what is there, recommend what to replace and what to keep, and quote accordingly.

  • Faded or torn flex and fabric faces on light boxes and frames
  • Cracked, yellowed or broken acrylic panels and letters
  • Peeling, shrinking or bleached vinyl on glass and walls
  • Damaged ACP panels on fascias after impact or weather
  • Outdated details such as phone numbers, opening hours and addresses

Colour matching and files

Matching new parts to old ones is the hardest part of replacement work. Colours on a sign that has been outside for several years have shifted, so printing from the original file may not match its faded neighbours. Where only part of a set is replaced, we discuss whether to match the current weathered state, reprint the whole set, or accept a visible difference until the rest is due.

Good files make replacement accurate. If you still have the original artwork, we check it and prepare it to print specification. If not, our print-ready file setup service can rebuild the files from your logo and measurements, leaving you with a clean set for future replacements.

Planning a rebrand or refresh

When a company changes its name, logo or colours, every sign, vehicle, sticker and printed item has to change with it. Rebranding works best as a planned roll-out: an audit of every item carrying the old identity, a priority order, removal of old graphics, and installation of the new, location by location. Working in stages keeps shops and offices open while the change happens.

Sticker removal is usually the first practical step. Old vinyl is taken off with heat and tools suited to the surface, adhesive residue is cleaned away, and the surface is prepared so new graphics bond properly.

FAQs

Maintenance & Replacement Services FAQs

Can you replace just the face of an existing light box?

Often, yes. If the frame, lighting and fixings are in good condition, a new printed face can be produced to the measured size and fitted in place of the old one. We inspect the box first, since cracked frames, water ingress or lighting faults change the scope. Any additional work is identified and quoted before we proceed.

Will the replacement match our other signs?

We aim for a close visual match by working from your original files and comparing against the existing signs on site. Signs that have been outdoors for years will have faded, so a brand-new part can look brighter at first. We show you the options, such as replacing a full set, before production starts.

Do you maintain signs you did not install?

Yes. We can inspect signs produced or installed by other companies and quote for replacement faces, graphics or parts. Clear photos, measurements and any original drawings or files help. Where parts are non-standard, we measure on site and make replacements to fit the existing structure.

What is included in a rebranding service?

A rebrand typically covers an audit of every item carrying the old identity, a phased plan, removal of old signage and stickers, production of new signs, vehicle graphics, stationery and interior branding, and installation location by location. The exact scope depends on how many sites and assets you have, and we set it out in a written quote.
