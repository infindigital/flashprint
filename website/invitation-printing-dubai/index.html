About invitation printing

Invitation printing in Dubai spans corporate and personal occasions: annual dinners, VIP launches, grand openings, conferences, Ramadan iftars, Eid receptions, graduations and weddings. An invitation sets expectations for the event, so the paper, weight and finishing say as much as the wording.

Popular sizes include A5 (148 × 210 mm), A6, DL, 5 × 7 inch (127 × 178 mm) and 150 mm square. Cards are printed on 350–450 gsm uncoated, textured or pearlescent board, or duplexed for extra thickness. Foil stamping for names and borders, blind embossing for crests or logos, and laser-cut patterns inspired by mashrabiya screens are all common choices for Dubai events.

Many events need more than one card. RSVP cards, venue maps, dress code or programme inserts, belly bands and lined envelopes can be produced as a coordinated set. From a guest list, we can print each invitation with the recipient's name and add a QR code linking to an online RSVP or location pin.

Materials

Cotton-style uncoated board

Soft and tactile, with a subtle texture that takes foil and embossing well. A classic choice for weddings and formal dinners.

Pearlescent board

A shimmering surface that catches the light, popular for Eid, Ramadan and evening events.

Duplexed board

Two boards bonded into one thick card, with the option of a contrasting colour layer visible at the edge.

Translucent vellum

A frosted, see-through sheet used as an overlay or wrap, printed with names or patterns above the main card.

Finishing options

Hot foil stamping

Gold, silver, rose gold or copper foil for names, dates, borders and monograms.

Blind embossing

A raised crest, logo or pattern without ink, adding texture that guests notice by touch.

Laser cutting

Intricate patterns, lace effects or cut-out windows, suited to sleeves and gatefold invitations.

Painted edges

A coloured edge on thick board, visible whenever the card is held or displayed.

Wax seal and belly band

Finishing touches that hold inserts together and complete the presentation of the suite.

Artwork checklist

  • Supply foil and emboss elements as separate vector layers
  • Check that thin script and calligraphy strokes are not too fine for foil; enlarge them if needed
  • Send guest names in a spreadsheet with one column per line of text
  • Add 3 mm bleed and keep text 5 mm from the edge, more on laser-cut designs
  • Allow 3–5 mm clearance around the card and inserts when sizing envelopes
  • Test any QR code from the proof before approving

Invitation printing FAQs

Can you print each guest's name on the invitation?

Yes. Send your guest list as a spreadsheet and we print each name, and any title or table number, directly onto the card during production. Names are checked on a proof before printing. Personalised names in foil need digital foil or individual dies, so tell us early if you want names to be metallic rather than printed.

Which paper feels most substantial for invitations?

Heavier, uncoated and textured boards usually feel the most substantial. Cotton-style board of 400 gsm or more, or two sheets duplexed together, gives a thick card that takes foil and embossing well. Pearlescent boards suit evening and festive events. We can share samples of different boards and finishes before you decide.

Do you supply envelopes with invitations?

Yes. Envelopes are sized to your card with a little clearance, in white, cream, kraft, coloured or pearlescent papers. They can be plain, printed with a return address, lined with a pattern or printed with guest names. Square and non-standard sizes are available; if invitations are being posted, check requirements with your courier.

Can invitations include a QR code for RSVP?

Yes. A QR code can link to an online RSVP form, event page or map location. Keep it at least 20 mm square with a clear margin, place it on the reverse or an insert if you want a cleaner front, and test the link before approving the proof. The code can be the same for everyone or unique per guest.

Can you produce laser-cut or acrylic invitations?

Laser-cut paper sleeves and gatefold cards are part of our invitation printing range, including cut patterns inspired by traditional screens. Acrylic invitations can also be produced through our laser cutting and engraving service, which suits VIP guests or keepsake pieces. Share the look you want and we will suggest a format that suits your quantity.
