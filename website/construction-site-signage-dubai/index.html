About construction site signage

A busy site has delivery trucks, concrete pumps, cranes, subcontractors and visitors all trying to find the right place. Clear construction site signage in Dubai keeps them on the correct routes, stops drivers wandering into work zones and gets visitors to the site office and induction room first. This page covers directional and information signs; entrance PPE boards and the project identification board have their own pages.

Typical signs include gate and entrance numbers, delivery and one-way arrows, pedestrian-only and vehicle-only routes, internal speed limits, parking and laydown area names, welfare and canteen locations, first-aid and assembly point directions, and block or tower identification as the structure rises. We produce them to your site traffic management plan or logistics layout.

Because layouts change as phases progress, many contractors combine permanent ACP signs on posts at fixed points with lightweight corrugated signs that can be cable-tied to fencing and moved. Reflective sheeting is worth adding to vehicle-route signs used at night or in covered areas.

Materials

Corrugated polypropylene

Light fluted board that cuts and drills easily and can be cable-tied to fencing. Well suited to signs that will move several times during a project.

Aluminium composite panel (3 mm)

Flat, rigid and weather-resistant for signs at fixed points such as gates, site offices and main junctions.

Aluminium sheet

Takes post clamps and channel backing for signs mounted on steel posts along internal site roads.

Reflective sheeting

Engineering-grade or prismatic sheeting for vehicle-route signs that must be read in headlights.

Finishing options

UV-resistant laminate

A protective layer against sun and abrasion that also lets dust be wiped away.

Eyelets and slots

Reinforced holes for cable-tying corrugated or banner signs to mesh fencing.

Channel backing

Aluminium channel on the reverse so signs clamp onto posts without holes through the face.

Artwork checklist

  • Mark sign positions and arrow directions on a site plan
  • List wording in English and Arabic for each sign, with sign references
  • Use high-contrast colours and large, bold lettering readable from a moving vehicle
  • Keep arrows as vector shapes so they print crisply at every size
  • Add your company logo only where it does not crowd the direction message

Construction site signage FAQs

What is the difference between site signage and a project sign board?

Directional site signage guides people and vehicles once they are on or near the site: gates, routes, offices, parking and welfare. The project sign board is a single large framed board at the entrance that identifies the project and the companies involved. Most sites need one project board and many directional signs.

Can signs be moved when the site layout changes?

Yes. Corrugated signs cable-tied to fencing are easy for your own team to move, and post-mounted ACP signs can be unbolted and re-fixed. We can also schedule a visit to relocate, update or replace signs as phases change. Tell us at the start how often you expect the layout to shift.

Should vehicle route signs be reflective?

For routes used at night, in basements or in heavy dust, reflective sheeting makes signs much easier to read in headlights. For daytime pedestrian signs, standard printed signs are usually enough. Your site traffic management plan may already state which signs need reflective material, in which case we follow it.

Do you make speed limit and traffic signs for inside the site?

Yes. For internal site roads and compounds we produce speed limit, stop, give-way, no-entry and one-way signs to the layout in your traffic management plan. Signs affecting public roads outside the site follow the road authority's specification and your traffic consultant's drawings, which we can produce when they are supplied.
