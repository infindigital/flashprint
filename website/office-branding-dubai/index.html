About office branding

Office branding in Dubai usually starts with a floor plan and a fit-out date. Reception is the first priority, typically a logo in 3D acrylic or metal letters on a feature wall, followed by glass partitions that need manifestation for privacy and so people notice the glass, then corridors and meeting rooms where printed walls tell the company story.

Each surface needs a different approach. Freshly painted walls must be fully cured before vinyl goes on, so we coordinate with your fit-out contractor rather than arriving on the painter's last day. Textured walls may need a conformable film or a printed panel instead. Glass takes etched-look frosted film, plain or cut with patterns, logos and room names at eye height.

Working in a tower brings its own rules. Many buildings require contractor access permits, method statements or after-hours installation. Share the building management's requirements and we plan the installation around them, including protecting floors and furniture while we work.

Materials

Printed wall vinyl

Laminated vinyl for smooth painted walls, used for murals, values statements and company timelines.

Frosted glass film

An etched-glass look for partitions and doors that adds privacy and makes large glass panels visible.

Acrylic

Clear, frosted or coloured sheet for reception logos, room plaques and directory panels, often mounted on standoffs.

Printed wallpaper

Wide-format wallcovering for full walls, with textured options that suit some wall finishes better than vinyl.

Foamex and composite panels

Rigid printed panels for walls that cannot take adhesive film, or for graphics you may move to another office later.

Finishing options

Matte laminate

Removes glare on walls lit by downlights or windows, so graphics read and photograph well.

Cut vinyl lettering

Individual letters with no background, for quotes, values and room names on paint or glass.

Brushed metal effects

Metallic film and letters that echo steel, glass and stone interiors.

Standoff mounting

Acrylic panels held off the wall on metal standoffs for a floating, layered look.

Office branding FAQs

What does office branding usually include?

A typical project covers the reception logo, glass manifestation on doors and partitions, one or two feature walls and room identification signs. Larger offices add wayfinding, floor numbers, directories and graphics in pantries or breakout areas. We start with the spaces visitors see first and can phase the rest if the budget or programme requires it.

Can graphics go on newly painted walls?

Only once the paint has fully cured, which depends on the paint system and ventilation. Vinyl applied too early can trap solvents and fail to bond, or pull paint away if it is ever removed. Your fit-out contractor or paint supplier can confirm the curing time, and we schedule the installation to suit.

Will glass film leave marks when we move out?

Frosted and decorative window films are designed to be removed, and on glass they usually come away cleanly with the right technique, with any adhesive residue cleaned off afterwards. Many landlords expect films to be removed at the end of a lease, and we offer removal as a separate service.

Can you install outside office hours?

Yes, where the building allows it. Many Dubai towers restrict noisy or disruptive work to evenings or weekends, and operational offices often prefer it anyway. We plan installation times around your building's rules and your team's schedule, and confirm them with the quote.

Do you work with interior designers and fit-out contractors?

Yes. We can work from your designer's drawings and specifications, supply samples of films and acrylic for approval, and coordinate site visits with the contractor. Agreeing early who handles walls, glass and any electrical items, such as illuminated logos, avoids clashes and repeat visits on site.
