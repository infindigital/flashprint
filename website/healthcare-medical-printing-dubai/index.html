Main page in Safety & Industrial Printing

Clinics, hospitals, pharmacies, labs and dental practices rely on print every day, from the sign that guides a patient to radiology to the consent form on a clipboard. Flash Print Solution handles medical printing in Dubai for healthcare operators, facility managers and practice administrators, covering signage, patient-facing information boards and the forms and pads used in consultations.

Healthcare content belongs to your facility. Clinical wording, patient rights text, form fields and anything your licensing body expects to see are provided and checked by your team; we take care of layout, print specification and materials. That means wipe-clean laminates and non-porous signage surfaces for areas cleaned often, NCR sets and numbering for forms, and neat, consistent pads for every doctor.

Based in Business Bay, we work with single-doctor clinics and multi-site healthcare groups across Dubai.

What the healthcare range covers

Four products cover most of what a facility needs, and each solves a different problem. Ordering them together is worth considering when a clinic opens or rebrands: one set of colours, fonts and pictograms carried from the entrance directory to the prescription pad makes the facility feel coherent, and a single artwork check covers the logo, address and bilingual naming used on everything.

  • Hospital signage: fixed wayfinding and identification, from entrance directories to department and room signs
  • Patient information boards: writable or changeable boards for wards, rooms and waiting areas
  • Medical forms: registration, consent, request and discharge forms as loose sheets, pads or NCR sets
  • Prescription pads: numbered, padded sheets personalised for each doctor or clinic

Materials chosen with cleaning routines in mind

Surfaces in clinical areas are wiped frequently, so we favour non-porous, sealed materials: acrylic, ACP and laminated boards with smooth faces, minimal crevices and rounded edges. Wipe-clean laminates let patient boards and wall notices be cleaned with the products your team already uses, and we can supply samples so your infection-control team can test them before a large order.

Paper items are specified for how they are used. NCR sets transfer handwriting to copies without carbon, numbering supports record keeping, and pads with a firm board back make writing at a bedside or reception desk easier. Warning labels for oxygen points, sharps areas and equipment are produced in our safety and industrial range.

Content, sign-off and ordering

Your facility supplies and checks every word. We do not write clinical content or advise on what a form, board or sign must include. Artwork is proofed to a named contact, and for multi-doctor orders a master template plus a separate list of each doctor's details keeps personal information limited to what actually needs to print.

Timelines depend on quantity, finish and artwork readiness, and we confirm the schedule with your quote before production. For a clinic opening, it helps to start signage early, because door signs, directories and room numbers depend on the final floor plan, while forms and pads can follow once their content is signed off.

  • Send current forms or signs, even as scans, if we are updating them
  • Name one person to approve content and proofs
  • Confirm numbering ranges and quantities per doctor or department
  • Tell us which cleaning agents are used where boards and signs will hang

Not sure which option fits?

Tell us where it will be used, how long it needs to last and your quantity. We'll recommend the right healthcare & medical printing option.

FAQs

Healthcare & Medical Printing FAQs

Do you write or check medical content?

No. Clinical wording, consent text, patient rights content and anything required by your licensing body or facility policy is provided and checked by your team. We handle layout, typesetting, print specification and materials, and send proofs so the responsible person can confirm every word before printing.

Are your signs and boards suitable for infection-control areas?

We offer materials that suit frequent cleaning, such as sealed acrylic, ACP and boards with wipe-clean laminates, with smooth faces and rounded edges. Whether a product is acceptable for a specific area is for your infection-control team to decide, and we can supply samples for them to test with your cleaning agents.

Can you reprint our existing forms with updates?

Yes. Send the current form as a PDF, scan or printed copy along with the changes, and we will rebuild it as clean, editable artwork. Rebuilding also lets us improve legibility, align fields and add numbering or barcodes, while keeping the structure your staff already know.

Can everything be printed in Arabic and English?

Yes. Signage, boards and forms are often bilingual in Dubai healthcare settings. Supply Arabic content as editable text from your translator or team, and we typeset it with the English. The proof shows both languages exactly as they will print, ready for your final check.
