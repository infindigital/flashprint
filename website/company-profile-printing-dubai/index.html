About company profile printing

Company profile printing in Dubai is common for tender submissions, pre-qualification packs, investor meetings and trade delegations, where a well-made document says something about how you run projects. The profile has to read clearly, open properly and survive being handled, passed around a meeting table and filed.

Page count drives the binding. Saddle stitching suits profiles of roughly 8 to 48 pages and needs a total in multiples of four. Thicker documents move to perfect binding, which gives a flat printed spine carrying your company name, or to wire-o, which lets the profile lie completely flat. Bilingual profiles that read Arabic first are bound on the right-hand edge, so tell us the reading direction early.

Covers carry most of the first impression. A 300 or 350 gsm board with soft-touch lamination feels substantial, and spot UV or foil on the logo adds contrast without clutter. Inside pages are typically 150 to 170 gsm silk or matte for crisp project photography, or uncoated stock for a more understated, text-led profile.

Materials

Silk coated paper

A smooth, low-sheen stock that keeps photographs sharp without gloss glare. The most common choice for project-heavy profiles.

Matte coated paper

Flatter than silk and comfortable to read under office lighting, while still reproducing images well.

Uncoated paper

Natural, slightly textured and easy to annotate. Suits consultancy and professional service profiles that are mostly text.

Rigid board cover

Hardcover-style boards for small premium runs, such as investor packs or special editions for key clients.

Finishing options

Soft-touch lamination

A velvety cover finish that resists fingerprints and feels substantial in the hand.

Spot UV

Clear gloss applied only to chosen areas, such as the logo or a hero project image, for contrast on a matte cover.

Foil blocking

Gold, silver or coloured foil on the cover title or logo. Works on laminated and uncoated boards alike.

Embossing or debossing

Raises or presses the logo into the cover board for a tactile detail without extra ink.

Die-cut window

A shaped cut-out in the cover that reveals an image or title printed on the first inside page.

Artwork checklist

  • Supply a print-ready PDF with 3 mm bleed on every page
  • Keep text and page numbers at least 5 mm inside the trim, more near the spine on perfect-bound profiles
  • Project photos should be 300 dpi at the printed size
  • Set the page count in multiples of four for saddle stitching
  • Send the cover as a separate spread including the spine once we confirm its width
  • Confirm reading direction and binding edge for Arabic or bilingual profiles

Company profile printing FAQs

How many pages should a company profile have?

Most profiles land between 12 and 32 pages: enough for an introduction, services, key projects, team and contact details without padding. Tender packs sometimes run longer to include method statements and project sheets. Once you know the page count we can advise on binding, and suggest where detailed content could move to a separate insert.

Should our profile be printed digitally or offset?

Digital printing is usually the better route for short runs, such as copies for a single tender or a few meetings, and it allows small content updates between runs. Offset makes sense for larger quantities, such as stock for a trade show, where a longer press run is more efficient. We recommend the method in your quote.

Can you print a profile with Arabic and English sections?

Yes. Some clients print two separate versions; others choose a single tête-bêche profile that reads English from one cover and Arabic from the other, meeting in the middle. We need the Arabic text as live, correctly shaped text or outlined in the PDF, and we recommend a native speaker proofreads before approval.

Which cover finish gives the most premium feel?

Soft-touch lamination with spot UV or foil on the logo is a popular combination, because it contrasts texture and shine without adding colour. For a quieter look, an uncoated textured board with a blind deboss works well. We can show printed samples of each so you can compare them in the hand before deciding.

Can you supply a digital version as well?

We print from your final PDF, and the same layout can be exported as a lighter file for email, with bleed and crop marks removed and images compressed. If our team designs the profile, we can hand over both the print-ready file and the screen version together.
