About safety vest printing

Safety vest printing in Dubai turns a generic hi-vis vest into something that identifies your company and each person's role at a glance. On a busy site or at an event gate, a back print reading First Aid, Supervisor or Visitor tells people who to approach, while a front logo shows which contractor or organiser the wearer belongs to.

Vests are mostly polyester, either solid knitted fabric or open mesh that stays cooler in summer. Because the fabric is thin and synthetic, the usual decoration is heat transfer vinyl or DTF, applied at a temperature suited to polyester. Screen printing works for larger runs of a simple one-colour design. Artwork is placed around the reflective tape, never over it, so the strips stay fully exposed.

Surveyor-style vests with pockets, zip fronts and ID holders suit engineers and site managers, while basic hook-and-loop vests are practical for visitors and temporary staff. If your consultant, client or safety officer specifies a vest standard or colour, share it with the brief and we will source to that specification.

Materials

Polyester mesh

Open-weave fabric that lets air through, suited to outdoor work in summer. The surface is slightly textured, so bold lettering reads best.

Solid knitted polyester

Smooth, closed surface that gives the cleanest result for detailed logos and longer text.

Surveyor vest fabric

Heavier polyester, mesh-lined in some styles, with pockets, pen holders and a clear ID pocket.

Reflective tape

Retroreflective strips sewn onto the vest. They are left unprinted so they keep returning light to its source.

Finishing options

Black on yellow or orange

The highest-contrast combination for role titles and company names.

Reflective vinyl lettering

Silver reflective names or titles that also catch vehicle headlights at night.

Full-colour DTF logo

Keeps brand colours accurate for client-facing event, valet and parking staff.

Numbered vests

Sequential numbers for marshals, stewards and gate teams, set up from a list.

Safety vest printing FAQs

Will printing on the vest cover the reflective strips?

No. We lay out logos and text in the fabric areas between and around the reflective bands, and the tape itself is left clear. On vests with wide tape and small panels, this can limit the print size, so we check the available area on the actual vest style and show it on the proof before production.

Which print method is used for safety vests?

Heat transfer vinyl and DTF are the most common because they bond well to thin polyester and mesh at controlled temperatures. Vinyl suits one-colour logos, titles and names, including reflective vinyl. DTF handles full-colour logos. For large runs of one simple design, screen printing can also be used on solid-fabric vests.

Can we use different vest colours for different roles?

Yes, and it is a practical way to organise a site or event. For example, orange for supervisors, yellow for general crew and another colour for first aiders or visitors, each with a matching back title. Availability of less common colours varies, so we confirm stock with the quote.

Do printed vests meet safety standards?

The visibility performance depends on the vest itself, so we source vests to the standard or specification your client, consultant or safety officer provides. Our part is to brand them without covering the reflective tape or changing the vest's construction. If a standard is required, include it in your brief so we can match the vest to it.
