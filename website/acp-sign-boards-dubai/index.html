ACP Sign Boards for Shopfronts and Buildings

Aluminium composite panel, or ACP, forms the flat, rigid background behind much of Dubai's shop and building signage. We cut, route and fold it into sign trays, fascias and panels, then add printed vinyl, cut-out lettering or 3D letters, lit or non-lit.

About ACP sign boards

ACP sign boards in Dubai are popular because the material behaves well on a facade. Two thin aluminium skins bonded to a polyethylene or mineral-filled core make a panel that is light, flat and stiff, with a factory finish in solid colours, metallics, brushed or wood-grain effects. It cuts cleanly, and when the back skin is V-grooved it folds into a tray with crisp returns, so a flat sheet becomes a sign box.

Most ACP signs are one of three builds. A flat panel with printed or cut vinyl suits directories, car park signs and simple name boards. A folded tray, fixed over an aluminium or steel sub-frame, forms a clean fascia for a shopfront and often carries 3D letters. A routed panel has letters or a logo cut out of the face and backed with acrylic, so light from inside shows only through the lettering.

Outdoors in the UAE, colour and expansion need attention. Dark panels absorb heat and expand, so fixings allow movement and long fascias are split into sections. Core type matters too: where your consultant, landlord or authority specifies a fire-retardant (FR) or mineral core, we produce to that specification rather than using a standard polyethylene core.

Materials

PE-core ACP

The general-purpose panel for signs and smaller fascias, with a polyethylene core between two aluminium skins.

FR or mineral-core ACP

Panels with a mineral-filled core, used where fire performance is set by the building's consultant or authority.

Brushed and mirror ACP

Metallic-look skins that suit corporate and showroom signs without the weight and cost of solid metal sheet.

Sub-frame sections

A hidden frame of aluminium or galvanised steel box section that the tray fixes to, keeping the face flat on uneven walls.

Finishing options

Printed vinyl with UV laminate

Full-colour graphics applied to the panel face and sealed with laminate for sun exposure.

Cut vinyl lettering

Single-colour text and logos cut from coloured film, for clean, flat signs that are simple to update.

Push-through acrylic

Letters routed out of the ACP and filled with thick acrylic that projects slightly and glows when lit from inside.

3D letters on a tray

Built-up letters fixed onto a folded fascia, the most common combination on Dubai shopfronts.

Why choose us for ACP sign boards

  • Folded, not patched together

  • Core chosen to specification

  • Sub-frames where needed

Artwork checklist

  • Supply fascia dimensions and a straight-on photo; we confirm with a site measurement before fabrication
  • Specify panel colour by the manufacturer's colour code or RAL, plus Pantone references for graphics
  • Logos for routing must be vector, with stroke widths thick enough to cut and back cleanly
  • Keep text at least 50 mm from folded edges and fixing points
  • Mark letter positions and light sources if the tray will carry lit 3D letters

ACP sign boards FAQs

What thickness of ACP should I choose?

3 mm ACP is common for indoor signs, directories and smaller flat panels. 4 mm is the usual choice for exterior fascias and larger trays, where extra stiffness helps the face stay flat in heat and wind. Your landlord or consultant may specify thickness and core type, in which case we follow their specification.

What does FR ACP mean?

FR stands for fire-retardant. These panels use a mineral-filled core in place of standard polyethylene, which changes how the panel behaves in a fire. Whether FR panels are required depends on the building, the sign's position and the rules that apply. We produce to the core type your consultant, landlord or authority specifies.

Can an ACP sign be illuminated?

Yes, in several ways. Letters can be routed out and backed with acrylic so they glow from LED modules inside the tray; lit 3D letters can be fixed to the face; or slim LED floodlights can wash the panel from above. Each option needs a power point nearby and room for drivers, which we plan during the survey.

How is an ACP sign fixed to the building?

Small flat panels can be fixed with stand-offs or bonded. Larger fascias are usually folded into trays and fixed to a sub-frame of aluminium or galvanised steel, which is anchored into the wall and allows for movement in heat. The fixing method depends on the wall construction, so we assess it on site before confirming.

Can the graphics be changed later?

Printed or cut vinyl can be removed and replaced on the same panel, which makes ACP a practical base for signs that may change. Routed lettering is permanent, so a name change means a new face. If a rebrand is likely, a tray with 3D letters or vinyl graphics is easier to update than a routed design.
