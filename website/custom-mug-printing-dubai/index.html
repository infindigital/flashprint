About custom mug printing

Custom mug printing in Dubai mostly means sublimation. The design is printed onto transfer paper with sublimation inks, then heated against a polymer-coated mug so the ink bonds into the coating. The result is a smooth, full-colour image with no raised edge, which can wrap from one side of the handle to the other or sit as a single logo.

The 11 oz (about 330 ml) white ceramic mug is the most requested shape. Two-tone mugs have a coloured handle and interior, bringing in a brand colour without printing a solid background. Magic mugs stay black until a hot drink is poured in, then reveal the design, which makes them a favourite for launches and seasonal gifts. Larger 15 oz mugs suit teams who like a bigger coffee.

Stainless steel travel mugs and tumblers are decorated differently. Laser engraving removes the powder coat, or marks the bare steel, to show the logo, so there is no ink sitting on the surface. We advise which option fits your artwork and send a proof with the print area marked on a mug template.

Materials

White ceramic

Coated ceramic with a gloss surface that shows colours brightly. The go-to for photos, multi-colour logos and full wraps.

Two-tone ceramic

White outer body for the print, with a coloured interior and handle in red, blue, black, green and more.

Magic mug

Heat-reactive black coating that clears with hot drinks to reveal the print. Better as a gift than a daily office mug.

Stainless steel

Double-wall travel mugs and tumblers, often powder-coated. Engraving gives a crisp silver or tone-on-tone logo.

Frosted glass

Translucent glass with a sublimation coating. Softer colour than ceramic, suited to cafés and lifestyle brands.

Finishing options

Full wrap

Design runs around the mug from handle to handle, useful for patterns and panoramic images.

Single or double logo

Logo on one or both sides, the simplest layout for large office orders.

Personalised names

Each mug carries a different name or number from your list in the same layout.

Laser-engraved logo

A permanent mark on steel or coated tumblers with no ink layer on the surface.

Why choose us for custom mug printing

  • Right method for each mug

    Ceramic is sublimated and steel is engraved, so the decoration suits the surface instead of sitting on top of it.

  • Proofed on a mug template

    Artwork is placed on a real template so you can see where the handle falls and how the wrap meets.

Artwork checklist

  • Design to our wrap template, roughly 210 × 85 mm for an 11 oz mug
  • Keep logos and text about 10 mm clear of the handle and the top and bottom edges
  • Supply photos at 300 dpi at print size; vector logos keep edges sharp
  • Sublimation cannot print white, so white areas show the mug colour
  • For engraving, send a single-colour vector logo without gradients or shading

Custom mug printing FAQs

What is sublimation mug printing?

Sublimation uses special inks printed onto transfer paper, which is wrapped around a coated mug and heated. The heat turns the ink into a gas that bonds with the polymer coating, so the image becomes part of the surface rather than a layer on top. It gives smooth, full-colour results, including photos and gradients, on white or light coated mugs.

Are printed mugs dishwasher safe?

Care advice depends on the mug. For sublimated ceramic mugs we recommend hand washing, because strong detergents and repeated high-heat cycles can gradually dull colours. Engraved steel mugs have no ink to fade, but many double-wall travel mugs should be hand washed anyway to protect their seals and coatings. We include the care guidance for your chosen mug with the quote.

Can each mug be printed with a different name?

Yes. Personalised mugs are set up from one master design, with the name, initials or number changing on each mug. Send the list in a spreadsheet with names spelled exactly as they should print. We proof the layout and the list before production, which makes it practical for staff gifts and team events.

Can you print on black or coloured mugs?

Sublimation needs a white or light coated area, so fully coloured mugs are not suited to it. Two-tone mugs are the usual answer: white outside for the print, with a coloured handle and interior. For dark finishes, engraved steel tumblers or magic mugs give a strong result. Tell us the look you want and we will suggest the closest option.
