Corporate Printing for Dubai Offices

Business cards, stationery, company profiles, reports, certificates and event material printed to your brand guidelines, with approved files kept on record so every reorder matches the last.

Corporate printing in Dubai is rarely a single, isolated job. A typical office orders business cards for each new joiner, letterheads and envelopes, presentation folders for proposals, company profiles for tenders, certificates for training programmes and a steady flow of event material. We handle that mix of business printing as one ongoing arrangement, so marketing, HR, admin and procurement teams can all order from the same approved specifications.

The priorities differ from one-off marketing print. Brand consistency matters across hundreds of items and many reorders; approvals often involve several people; and purchasing teams need clear, itemised quotes that line up with purchase orders. Our role is to keep colours, typefaces, paper and finishes identical from batch to batch, flag anything that departs from your brand guidelines and make repeat office printing simple to request, whichever department is placing the order.

Corporate stationery and business cards

Stationery is the most frequently reordered corporate item. Business cards are typically 90 × 55 mm or 85 × 55 mm on 350–400 gsm card, with options such as soft-touch lamination, spot UV or foil for senior teams. Letterheads are usually A4 (210 × 297 mm) on 100–120 gsm uncoated paper so they run cleanly through office laser printers, with matching DL or C4 envelopes and compliment slips.

For larger organisations, we set up a template for each card design so new names, titles, phone numbers and QR codes drop into an approved layout. Bilingual English and Arabic cards are typeset with the correct text direction and balanced type weights.

Company profiles, reports and folders

A company profile or annual report is often the first detailed document a prospective client or investor sees. Common formats are A4 portrait or landscape, saddle-stitched up to around 48 pages or perfect bound for thicker documents, with a 300–350 gsm cover and 150–170 gsm silk text pages. Covers can carry soft-touch lamination, spot UV or foil.

Presentation folders hold proposals, tender documents and welcome packs. A4 folders with one or two pockets, a business card slot and an optional capacity spine for thicker documents are the usual format, printed on heavy board and laminated to resist scuffing.

Certificates, forms and internal documents

Offices also need printing that never reaches the public: training certificates, ID cards, NCR invoice and delivery books, forms, notepads and internal manuals. Certificates are commonly printed on 250–300 gsm textured or pearlescent card, sometimes with a foil seal, and can be personalised from a spreadsheet of names.

Carbonless books are produced in duplicate or triplicate sets with sequential numbering, perforation and a card back, bound in books of 50 or 100 sets as specified. Training manuals can be wire-o bound so they lie flat on a desk.

Event, exhibition and branded materials

Conferences, product launches and exhibitions bring a burst of printed material tied to a fixed date: roll-up banners, backdrops, name badges, brochures, invitations, delegate packs and gifts. Planning them as one order keeps colours and messaging aligned across items made by different processes.

Corporate gifts such as diaries, pens, mugs and USB drives, along with uniforms and T-shirts for staff, can be branded to the same guidelines. Share the event date at the brief stage and we plan proofs and production back from it, confirming the schedule in the quote.

Keeping print on brand

Brand colours drift when different suppliers use different papers, processes and colour settings. We work from your brand manual, record the Pantone and CMYK values, papers and finishes approved for each item, and check new artwork against them. When a design comes from an agency, our artwork check covers bleed, fonts, image resolution and colour mode before proofing.

If no brand guidelines exist yet, our design team can set up a simple print specification covering logo versions, colours, fonts and stationery layouts, which then becomes the reference for every future order.

Bulk and repeat ordering for offices

Most corporate customers settle into a pattern of recurring orders. To keep those simple, each approved file and specification is kept on record, so a reorder only needs the item, the quantity and any changed details. For business cards, send a list of new names and titles and we return proofs in the approved layout for sign-off.

Larger volumes can move to offset printing where it lowers the unit cost, and delivery to more than one address can be arranged in the quote. Timelines are confirmed with each order.

FAQs

Corporate Printing: FAQs

How do repeat orders for business cards work?

Once a card design is approved, we keep the template and specification on file. For each reorder, send the new names, titles, phone numbers and email addresses, ideally in a spreadsheet. We set them into the approved layout, send proofs for sign-off and print on the same card and finish as before, so new cards match the ones already in circulation.

Can you keep our colours consistent between orders?

Consistency comes from repeating the same specification: the same file, colour values, paper and finish. We record those for every item and use your previous print as a reference. Where exact colour is essential, offset with Pantone spot inks gives the tightest control. Some variation between different papers and processes is normal, and we point it out at the proof stage.

Can several departments order under one account?

Yes. Many companies nominate one or two people to approve orders while different departments request items. We can group quotes by department or cost centre and reference your purchase order numbers, which keeps invoicing straightforward for procurement. Tell us how approvals work internally and we will organise proofs and quotes to match that process.

Can you print bilingual English and Arabic stationery?

Yes. Business cards, letterheads, certificates and forms can be produced in English and Arabic. We check that Arabic text is set right to left with correctly joined characters, and that both languages are balanced in weight and size. Supply the translated Arabic copy and our design team will lay it out alongside the English for your approval.

What do you need to quote a corporate stationery package?

List the items, quantities and sizes, plus any paper or finish preferences, and send your logo and brand guidelines if you have them. Mention whether you need design work, how many names are on the business card list and where the order should go. We then return a written quote with the specification and schedule for each item.
