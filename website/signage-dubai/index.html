Business Signage Made and Installed in Dubai

Shopfront signs, reception logos, office and wayfinding signs, safety signage and site boards, designed to your brand, fabricated in acrylic, aluminium composite, metal or vinyl, and fitted at your premises in Dubai.

Signage in Dubai has to work in two very different settings: bright exteriors where signs face strong sun, dust and heat, and air-conditioned interiors where finish and detail are seen up close. We produce business signage for both, from illuminated shop signage and 3D letter fascias to reception logos, office door signs, wayfinding systems and site safety boards. Each sign is designed around viewing distance, mounting surface and how it will be lit.

A sign board is also part of a building, so it involves more than printing. Most projects start with a site visit and measurements, followed by a drawing that shows size, materials, colours, lighting and fixing method. Where a landlord, mall management or authority must approve the design, the client arranges that approval and we produce to the approved specification. Fabrication and installation are then planned together, so the sign arrives ready to fit.

Outdoor signage and shopfronts

External signs carry a business name to people passing by car or on foot, so legibility at a distance comes first. Shopfront fascias commonly combine an ACP (aluminium composite panel) backing with 3D letters, either non-illuminated, face-lit, halo-lit from behind or lit through acrylic faces. Light boxes and neon sign boards add night-time presence for restaurants, cafés and retail.

Outdoor materials are chosen for sun and heat exposure: UV-resistant vinyls and inks, aluminium or stainless steel for letter returns, and LED components suited to exterior use. We recommend options based on the sign's orientation, mounting height and how it will be cleaned and maintained.

Indoor, office and reception signage

Inside a building, signage becomes part of the interior design. Reception signs are often built from layered acrylic, brushed or mirrored metal-effect finishes and stand-off fixings that hold a panel a short distance from the wall. Office signage carries the same look through to door plates, meeting room names, directory boards and frosted glass manifestation.

Consistency matters more indoors because signs are seen together. We work from your brand colours and typefaces and can prepare a signage schedule listing every sign, its location, wording and size, so a whole floor is produced and installed as one coordinated set.

Safety, wayfinding and construction signage

Wayfinding signs guide visitors through malls, hospitals, schools, car parks and office towers using consistent symbols, arrows and colour coding. Safety signs communicate prohibitions, mandatory actions, warnings and emergency information with standard pictograms and colours, printed on rigid PVC, aluminium or photoluminescent material where specified.

On construction sites, project boards, hoardings, directional signs and safety boards need to be durable and simple to replace as the site changes. For any sign governed by regulations, such as fire exit or hazard signage, we produce to the specification your consultant, safety officer or authority provides.

Signage materials compared

Material choice sets the look, durability and budget of a sign; exact specifications are confirmed in the quote.

  • Acrylic: clear, frosted, coloured or mirrored sheet, cut to shape for panels, letters and stand-off plaques
  • ACP (aluminium composite panel): flat, rigid and light, the usual backing for shopfront fascias and large boards
  • 3D letters: acrylic, aluminium, stainless steel or PVC foam, illuminated or non-illuminated
  • Light boxes: printed or vinyl-faced panels backlit with LEDs, single or double-sided
  • Neon signs: flexible LED neon shaped into lettering or logos, usually mounted on acrylic
  • Vinyl: cut or printed graphics for glass, walls and temporary signs
  • Flex: printed PVC banner stretched over a frame for large sign boards

Planning a sign: survey, drawings and approvals

A site survey records the mounting surface, dimensions, power supply position for illuminated signs and access for installation. From that, we prepare a drawing or visual showing the sign on the elevation, with materials, colours, letter heights and fixing details, so everyone agrees the design before fabrication starts.

Many buildings in Dubai require landlord or mall consent for external signs, and some signs also need a permit from the relevant authority. The client arranges these approvals; our drawings can be supplied for the submission, and we then produce to the approved specification. Allowing time for approval in the plan avoids a finished sign waiting in storage.

Fabrication and installation

Once drawings are approved, the sign is fabricated in the workshop: panels and letters are cut, edges finished, faces printed or covered in vinyl, and lighting fitted and tested before it leaves. Installation at your premises in Dubai is scheduled around access, building rules and trading hours, with the power connection for illuminated signs coordinated with your electrician or facilities team.

We also replace existing signs during a rebrand, reprint damaged faces and carry out maintenance such as LED replacement.

FAQs

Signage: FAQs

Do I need approval before installing a shop sign in Dubai?

In most cases, yes. Malls and landlords usually have signage guidelines and ask to approve the design, and external signs on many buildings also need a permit from the relevant authority. The client or landlord arranges these approvals. We can prepare the drawings and visuals needed for the submission, then fabricate and install to the approved specification.

Which is better for a shopfront: 3D letters or a light box?

Built-up 3D letters give a more architectural look and suit ACP fascias, stone or glass. Light boxes are simpler, show a full-colour graphic and are easier to update by changing the face. Budget, landlord guidelines and whether you need night-time visibility usually decide it. We can show both options on a visual of your frontage before you choose.

How long do outdoor signs last in the Dubai climate?

Lifespan depends on materials, orientation and maintenance rather than a fixed number of years. South- and west-facing signs take the strongest sun, so we specify UV-resistant vinyls and inks, aluminium or stainless steel letters and LEDs suited to exterior use. Regular cleaning, checking fixings and replacing damaged faces early all help any sign stay in good condition for longer.

Can you install signs at our office or site?

Yes. Installation at your premises in Dubai is part of our fabrication and installation service. We confirm the fixing method from the site survey, agree access and working hours with your building management and coordinate power connections for illuminated signs. Some buildings require permits or method statements for work at height, so tell us early to allow time for them.

What information do you need for a signage quote?

Send photos of the location, approximate dimensions, your logo in vector format and whether the sign should be illuminated. Mention any landlord guidelines or brand manual you need to follow. For larger or external signs, a site survey confirms measurements, the mounting surface and access before the final quote, so the drawing reflects real conditions.
