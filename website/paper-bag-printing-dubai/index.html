About paper bag printing

Paper bag printing for retail and events is a different product from a takeaway bag. Here the bag is part of the brand experience: it carries a new abaya, a bottle of oud or a conference gift through a mall, a hotel lobby or an exhibition hall, and a well-made bag is often reused. We print on smooth coated art paper, laminate it for strength and finish, then fold and reinforce it with a board strip at the top and a base card at the bottom.

Handles set the tone. Polypropylene or cotton rope is the classic choice, knotted inside through reinforced holes or finished with metal eyelets. Satin or grosgrain ribbon feels softer and more delicate for jewellery, fragrance and bridal boutiques. Die-cut handles, cut through the bag itself, give a cleaner shape for exhibitions and promotions.

Full-bleed colour, a matte or soft-touch laminate and one well-placed foil logo is a common specification. Sizes are matched to your products, and a range of two or three bag sizes keeps stock manageable. Paper bags are produced in batches rather than on demand, so plan quantities around your stock cycle, season or event date.

Materials

157 gsm art paper

A lighter bag for event handouts, brochures and light promotional items.

200–250 gsm art card

A stiffer, more substantial bag that holds its shape for boutiques, perfume and corporate gifts.

Coloured and textured card

Uncoated card in black, white or colour for an understated luxury look, usually paired with foil.

Finishing options

Matte lamination

Protects the print from scuffing and gives a calm, contemporary surface.

Soft-touch lamination

A velvety feel that customers notice the moment they pick the bag up.

Hot foil

Metallic logos in gold, silver or rose gold, often the only decoration on a solid-colour bag.

Spot UV

Gloss highlights on a matte bag, such as a pattern or logo that catches the light as it moves.

Embossing

A raised logo on heavier card for a detail people can feel.

Artwork checklist

  • Use our dieline for your bag size, showing front, back, gussets and fold-over top
  • Keep important elements out of the 3–4 cm fold-over at the top
  • Keep logos clear of handle holes and gusset folds
  • Supply foil and spot UV elements as separate vector layers
  • Specify brand colours as Pantone references; lamination slightly shifts colour

Paper bag printing FAQs

How much weight can a laminated paper bag carry?

It depends on the paper weight, handle type and reinforcement. A 250 gsm laminated bag with rope handles and board strips is suited to clothing, boxed perfume and gift sets, while heavy bottles or books need a stronger base and handle fixing. Tell us the heaviest item the bag will carry and we specify the construction accordingly.

Should I choose a laminated paper bag or a kraft bag?

Choose a laminated paper bag when presentation matters: boutiques, gifting, events and luxury retail, where full-colour print, rope handles and foil make the bag part of the product. Choose kraft for high-volume everyday use such as takeaway, bakery and grocery, where practicality and a natural look count more than finish.

Can the inside of the bag be printed?

Yes. A solid brand colour, pattern or short message on the inside gives a surprising detail when the bag is opened, and it hides the reverse of the paper. Inside printing adds to the setup, so include it in your brief. It works particularly well with dark or black bags.

Can we order paper bags for a single event?

Yes. Event bags are usually sized for brochures and giveaways and may use lighter paper and simpler handles. Share the event date, the items going in and the rough quantity when you enquire, and we will recommend a construction and confirm the schedule with your quote, working back from your set-up date.
