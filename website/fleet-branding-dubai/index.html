About fleet branding

Fleet branding in Dubai is less about one great-looking vehicle and more about fifty that clearly belong to the same company. A livery that works on a panel van rarely fits a pick-up or a minibus without changes, so we build a design system: fixed logo sizes, colour zones, an order of information and clear rules for how each element moves between body types.

Colour is where fleets drift apart. Printed film from different media batches, laminates or print settings can shift a brand colour visibly between vehicles. We record the film, laminate and colour profile used for your fleet, print a reference swatch for sign-off and reuse those settings on every repeat order, including vehicles added months later.

Coverage can also vary by role. Management cars might carry discreet door logos while service vans take a half wrap and trucks get full-length box graphics. We plan installation in groups so only part of the fleet is off the road at any time, and keep numbered artwork for each vehicle so accident repairs can be reprinted panel by panel.

Materials

Cast printed vinyl

For vehicles where the livery crosses bumpers, arches or rear doors. It keeps the same look on cars and vans with complex bodywork.

Polymeric calendered vinyl

Suits flat box bodies and trailer sides, where large areas of film do not need to stretch.

Cut coloured vinyl

Solid-colour lettering and logos cut on a plotter. Handy for fleet numbers and details that change from vehicle to vehicle.

Reflective vinyl

Adds night-time visibility to rear doors, bumpers and fleet numbers on vehicles that run early or late shifts.

Finishing options

Gloss laminate

The usual choice for fleets, as it resembles paint and is easy for drivers to keep clean.

Satin or matte laminate

A more restrained corporate look, provided the fleet's washing routine suits a non-gloss surface.

Unlaminated cut vinyl

Solid coloured vinyl needs no laminate, which keeps simple lettering straightforward to replace or update.

Fleet branding FAQs

How do you keep colours consistent across a large fleet?

We sign off a printed colour reference on the chosen film and laminate, then record the media, profile and settings used. Repeat orders are printed to that record. We also recommend branding vehicles in batches where possible, because film from the same production run gives the closest match between vehicles parked side by side.

Our fleet has different makes and models. Can one design work on all of them?

Yes, if it is built as a system rather than a single layout. We fix the elements that must stay identical, such as logo size, colours and the order of information, then adjust positions and shapes to each body. You approve a proof for every vehicle type before anything is printed.

Can vehicles be branded without taking the whole fleet off the road?

That is the normal approach. We agree a schedule with your fleet or operations manager and bring vehicles in small groups, so deliveries and service calls continue. Graphics for each group are printed and laminated in advance, so each vehicle is only unavailable for its own installation.

What happens when a branded vehicle is damaged?

Because we keep the artwork for each vehicle, the affected panel, such as one door or a rear bumper, can be reprinted rather than rewrapping the whole side. Older vehicles may have weathered slightly, so we check the surrounding panels and advise if a wider section should be refreshed for an even look.

Does advertising on company vehicles need a permit?

Commercial advertising on vehicles in Dubai can require a permit, and arranging it is the vehicle owner's responsibility. We are happy to supply proofs and dimensions for your application, then produce every vehicle to the approved design so the finished fleet matches what was submitted.
