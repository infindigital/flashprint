About end-cap display printing

End cap display printing in Dubai supports some of the most valuable space in any supermarket or hypermarket. Aisle ends see traffic from three directions, and brands often secure them for a fixed promotional period. The graphics need to make the most of that window, with a header readable from the main walkway and shelf-level messages close to the product.

Most end-cap kits combine several parts. A header board sits above the top shelf, typically fixed into the gondola uprights or held on a bracket. Side panels frame the unit, shelf strips run along each shelf edge, and a base wrap covers the lower plinth. Some promotions add a pallet skirt or a freestanding dump bin in front for volume products.

Gondola sizes vary by retailer, commonly around 1,000 to 1,330 mm wide and up to about 2,000 mm high, so we always work from the store's own measurements. Materials follow the campaign length: printed corrugated board for short promotions, 3 or 5 mm foamex for rigid headers, and fluted polypropylene where the display may be knocked by trolleys or wiped down.

Materials

Foamex (PVC foam board)

Light, rigid and flat. Used for headers and side panels that must stay straight under store lighting.

Corrugated board

Printable and recyclable, well suited to short promotions and structural pieces such as dump bins.

Fluted polypropylene

A lightweight fluted plastic board that shrugs off moisture and knocks from trolleys and baskets.

Thin PVC sheet

Flexible strip material for shelf-edge runners and base wraps that bend around fixtures.

Finishing options

Matte lamination

Cuts glare from bright supermarket lighting, keeping headers readable from the aisle.

Contour cutting

Shaped headers that follow a product silhouette or logo rather than a plain rectangle.

Double-sided print

Headers printed on both faces for end-caps seen from two directions.

Artwork checklist

  • Get the gondola width, header height and shelf channel size from the retailer
  • Keep the main message in the top third of the header for aisle visibility
  • Supply separate files for header, sides, shelf strips and base wrap

End-cap display printing FAQs

What is an end-cap display?

An end-cap is the shelving unit at the end of a supermarket or store aisle, facing the main walkway. Because shoppers see it while moving in several directions, retailers use it for promotions and new products. Printed end-cap graphics usually include a header, side panels, shelf-edge strips and a wrap around the base.

Which sizes should we print?

Work from the retailer's own measurements, since gondola widths, heights and shelf channels differ between chains and even between store formats. If you are rolling out across several retailers, we can adapt a core header design to each size. Photos with a tape measure in shot are a practical way to avoid errors.

How are end-cap headers fixed?

Common methods include slotting into the gondola uprights, clip brackets on the top shelf, magnetic strips on steel shelving or double-sided tape where it is allowed. Retailers often have rules about what can be attached to their fixtures, so confirm the method before production and we design the header with the right slots or margins.

Can the same kit be reused for another promotion?

Rigid foamex headers and side panels can be reused if the core branding stays the same, with promotional messages changed through printed overlays or new shelf strips. Board kits are usually single-campaign items and are recycled afterwards. We can plan the design so permanent and changeable parts are separated from the start.
