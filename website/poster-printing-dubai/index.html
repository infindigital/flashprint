About poster printing

Poster printing in Dubai is mostly about indoor visibility: window promotions, lift lobby notices in residential towers, clinic awareness campaigns, school displays, event announcements and wall art in offices. A poster has to communicate from a distance, so size, colour and type scale matter more than fine detail.

Smaller posters in A3 (297 × 420 mm) and A2 (420 × 594 mm) are often printed on a digital press on 170–250 gsm silk or gloss. Larger A1 (594 × 841 mm), A0 (841 × 1189 mm) and custom sizes are printed wide-format on satin or matt photo papers, which hold smooth gradients and deep blacks. Synthetic polypropylene papers resist tearing and moisture for busy corridors and semi-covered areas.

Unlike flyers, posters are one-sided and made to be displayed rather than carried. Outdoor exposure to sun and rain calls for PVC or fabric banners instead, and illuminated displays need backlit film. Mounting onto 3–5 mm foam board gives a rigid, ready-to-display poster for easels and events.

Materials

Silk 170–250 gsm

A smooth, low-sheen paper that suits A3 and A2 posters for notice boards, schools and offices.

Satin photo paper

Rich colour and smooth tones for large-format photography, retail windows and décor posters.

Synthetic polypropylene

Tear-resistant and water-resistant, used for posters that are handled, moved or displayed in humid areas.

Backlit film

A translucent film printed for light boxes and illuminated frames in malls and restaurants.

Finishing options

Matt lamination

Reduces glare under shop lighting and protects against fingerprints and scuffs.

Gloss lamination

Deepens colour and adds a wipe-clean surface for posters in food and retail environments.

Encapsulation

Seals the poster between two layers of film with a clear border, suited to posters that are pinned or used in kitchens.

Foam board mounting

Mounting onto 3–5 mm foam board creates a rigid poster for easels, counters and event signage.

Artwork checklist

  • Set the document at final size, or to scale with the ratio clearly noted
  • Add 3 mm bleed, or 5 mm if the poster will be mounted on board
  • Aim for 150–300 dpi at full size; the lower end is fine for A1 and A0 viewed from a distance
  • Keep key text at least 20 mm from the edge if the poster goes into a frame or rail
  • Make headlines readable from where people will stand; print a scaled-down copy to test
  • Convert spot colours to CMYK unless a specific colour match has been agreed

Poster printing FAQs

What resolution do I need for a large poster?

For A3 and A2 posters, aim for 300 dpi at final size. For A1 and A0, 150–200 dpi at full size is usually sufficient because posters are viewed from further away. Phone photos may be enough for smaller sizes but can look soft when enlarged. We check resolution at print size and flag anything that could look pixelated.

Can posters be used outdoors?

Paper posters are designed for indoor use or sheltered spots behind glass. For outdoor display in Dubai's sun and humidity, PVC flex, mesh or fabric banners and rigid boards are more suitable. Synthetic paper with lamination is a middle ground for covered areas such as car park lobbies or shaded walkways.

What is the difference between a poster and a banner?

Posters are printed on paper or synthetic paper, typically up to A0, and are hung on walls, in frames or behind glass. Banners are printed on flexible PVC or fabric, often much larger, with hems and eyelets for outdoor fixing. If your display needs to be moved, rolled or used outside, a banner is the better choice.

Can you mount posters on foam board?

Yes. Posters can be mounted on 3 mm or 5 mm foam board for a rigid, lightweight finish, suited to easels at events, reception counters and temporary displays. Laminating before mounting adds protection for boards that will be handled or wiped. Tell us how the board will be displayed so we can suggest the right thickness.

Can you print a single poster?

Yes. Wide-format and digital poster printing make single posters and small sets practical, which suits event signage, framed artwork and one-off displays. For campaigns with many identical posters across branches, larger quantities can be produced in one run and packed by location for your team.
