Range

Government & Compliance Printing products

3 options in this category. Open any item for specifications, materials and FAQs.

Facility Identification Boards

Building ID boards, directories and room plates for offices, depots, utilities and public facilities.

Mandatory Signage

Blue mandatory-action signs and required notices, produced line by line from your sign schedule.

Regulatory Stickers

Notice, inspection, asset, door and vehicle stickers, including numbered and tamper-evident options.

Government signage printing in Dubai is mostly about accuracy. Government departments, facility management companies, utilities, schools and regulated businesses need signs and notices whose wording, symbols, colours and sizes are set by someone else: an authority, a consultant, a landlord or an internal compliance team. Our job is to reproduce that specification faithfully, on materials that suit the building.

This category brings together three groups: facility identification boards for buildings, rooms and plant areas; mandatory signs and statutory notices, including the blue mandatory-action class; and regulatory stickers for doors, equipment, vehicles and inspection records. We do not issue approvals or advise on legal requirements. We work from your schedule, proof every item for the responsible person to check, and install at your premises in Dubai where needed.

Three product groups, three different jobs

Keeping the groups separate makes schedules easier to manage and procurement easier to track, because each is usually owned by a different person in your organisation.

  • Facility identification boards: building names and numbers, department directories, room and plant-room plates, and entrance boards for offices, depots and utilities
  • Mandatory signage: blue mandatory-action signs and the notices your authority, landlord or consultant asks you to display, ordered from a sign schedule
  • Regulatory stickers: small adhesive notices, inspection and asset stickers, door and vehicle stickers, tamper-evident seals and numbered labels

Working from a specification

The most reliable orders arrive as a schedule: each item with a reference, exact wording in English and Arabic, symbol, colour reference, size, material, quantity and location. We quote line by line, prepare artwork per item and return proofs in the same order, so the person responsible can check them against the source document without hunting.

Where a colour must match a stated RAL or Pantone reference, or a font is named in a guideline, we set up artwork to that reference and flag anything we cannot match on the chosen material. Arabic text is typeset from supplied wording and proofed; final checking of the translation stays with your team or translator.

General workplace safety signs and equipment warning labels sit in our safety and industrial range and follow the same proofing process, so a single schedule can cover both.

Materials for public buildings and facilities

Identification boards and door plates are often acrylic, brushed aluminium, stainless steel or ACP, sometimes with 3D letters or engraved detail. Mandatory signs go on foamed PVC, ACP or self-adhesive vinyl, while regulatory stickers use vinyl, polyester or tamper-evident films. Outdoor items get UV-resistant laminates or direct UV print.

Large estates change constantly, with departments moving, rooms repurposed and notices reissued. Keeping one master file per sign type and a current schedule lets replacements match the originals across every building. We produce new items against the same references, and our team can remove old signs and fit new ones during a single visit to your premises in Dubai.

  • Acrylic and ACP for entrance and directory boards
  • Brushed aluminium or stainless steel for plates
  • Foamed PVC and vinyl for mandatory signs and notices
  • Polyester and destructible vinyl for stickers and seals

FAQs

Government & Compliance Printing FAQs

Can you get our signs signed off by the authority?

No. We are a print and fabrication company. Any inspection, approval or sign-off of signage is between you, your consultant and the relevant authority. What we do is reproduce the specification, symbols and wording you supply accurately, and send proofs so your responsible person can check every item against the source document before production.

Can you work to a government entity's brand guidelines?

Yes. Send the guideline document with fonts, colour references, logo files and layout rules, and we will set up artwork to match. If the guidelines call for a font we do not hold a licence for, we will ask you to supply it or agree an alternative before proofing.

Do you print in Arabic and English?

Yes, bilingual layouts are standard for this category. Supply Arabic wording as editable text from your translator or internal team, and we typeset it with the English. The proof shows both languages exactly as they will print, so your team can check spelling, word order and alignment.

Can you install signs in occupied buildings?

Yes. Our installation team can fit boards, plates and signs in occupied offices, schools and facilities, working around your access rules and hours. Tell us about security clearance, escort requirements or permit-to-work procedures when you request a quote, so the visit can be planned properly.
