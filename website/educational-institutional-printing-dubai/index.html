School and institutional printing in Dubai follows the academic calendar. Term starts bring ID cards and lanyards, assessment windows bring exam papers, and the end of each term brings report cards and certificates. We help schools, nurseries, universities, training academies and HR departments across Dubai plan these recurring jobs so each one arrives in the right format for the people using it.

Every item in this range is produced from your approved artwork or template, proofed before production and checked before it leaves us. Many institutions reuse the same designs every year, so we record the agreed stock, size and finish on each quote to make repeat orders easier to match. Before the next run, we ask you to confirm anything that has changed, such as a new logo, a revised grading scale or an updated signatory.

Choosing stocks and formats for school documents

The right paper depends on how long a document needs to last and how it will be handled. Certificates are kept for years and often framed, so heavier board with a smooth or textured surface makes sense. Exam papers are written on in pen and pencil, which calls for uncoated paper that takes ink without smudging. Report cards travel home in school bags and come back signed, so a folded card of moderate weight balances durability with easy filing.

ID cards are a separate material family. PVC cards at the standard CR80 size (85.6 × 54 mm) cope with daily use in a holder or on a lanyard, while laminated paper cards can suit short-term visitor or event passes.

  • Certificates: 250–350 gsm board, textured or pearlescent stocks, optional gold or silver foil
  • Exam papers: 80–100 gsm uncoated paper, stapled or loose-leaf, A4 or A3 folded to A4
  • Report cards: 250–300 gsm card, folded to A4 or A5, blank shells or personalised
  • ID cards: 0.76 mm PVC, CR80 size, printed one or both sides with photos and names
  • Training manuals: 80–120 gsm text pages with 250–350 gsm covers, wire-o, perfect or saddle-stitch bound

Personalised data, confidentiality and version control

Much educational printing is personalised. Student names, photos, grades, roll numbers and certificate serials can be merged from a spreadsheet supplied by your registrar or HR team. We check a sample of merged records with you at proof stage, because a misspelt name on a certificate or the wrong photo on an ID card is difficult to put right once the whole batch is printed.

Assessment papers need a different kind of care. When you request it, exam papers can be packed and sealed by class, subject or session, labelled to your instructions and handed over only to the contact you nominate. These handling steps are agreed before production so everyone involved knows how the material moves from file to classroom.

Version control matters too. Training manuals and staff handbooks are revised often, so we print the edition number and revision date you specify on the cover or footer. Trainers can then confirm at a glance that everyone in the room is working from the current version.

Planning around the academic year

The busiest periods for schools are predictable: the weeks before a new term, mid-year and end-of-year assessments, and graduation season. Sending artwork and data early leaves room for a proper proof round, which is where most errors are caught. Timelines depend on quantity, finish and artwork readiness, and we confirm the schedule with your quote before production.

If your team is short of design time, our design service can build certificate templates, ID card layouts or report card shells that staff can reuse, with fields set up for data merging.

  • Share the event or term date when you request a quote
  • Send final data as a spreadsheet with one row per person
  • Nominate one person to approve proofs and receive sealed items

Tell us where it will be used, how long it needs to last and your quantity. We'll recommend the right educational & institutional printing option.

FAQs

Educational & Institutional Printing FAQs

Can you print certificates with each student's name already on them?

Yes. Send a spreadsheet with one row per recipient and the columns you need, such as name, course, date and certificate number. We merge the data into your approved template and send a proof showing a sample of records. Arabic and English names can both be set, provided the spreadsheet holds the correct spelling for each language.

How do you handle exam papers confidentially?

Confidential handling is a process we agree with you before the job starts. Typical requests include limiting file access to the staff working on the order, packing papers in sealed envelopes or boxes by class or session, labelling each pack, and releasing them only to a named contact. Tell us your requirements when you request a quote.

Do you print ID cards on PVC?

Yes. Student and staff ID cards are commonly printed on 0.76 mm PVC at the standard 85.6 × 54 mm credit-card size, one or both sides, with photos, names and barcodes merged from your data. Lanyards, clips and card holders can be added, and chip or RFID cards can be discussed on request.

Can we reorder the same items next term?

Yes. Repeat orders are straightforward when the artwork and specification stay the same. Send the updated data or any changed details, such as new dates, signatories or logos, and we will proof the changes before printing. Keeping the same stock and finish helps each new batch sit comfortably alongside earlier ones.
