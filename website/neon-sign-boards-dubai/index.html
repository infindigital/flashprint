About neon sign boards

Neon sign boards in Dubai are now usually made with LED neon flex rather than hand-bent glass tube. The look is similar, a continuous line of soft light, but LED neon runs at low voltage, weighs far less, travels without the fragility of glass and uses less power. That makes it practical for café feature walls, salon mirrors, retail windows, wedding stages and branded photo walls.

The design is traced into a path that the flex follows, then fixed into channels or held with clips on an acrylic backing board. Clear acrylic keeps the backing almost invisible on a wall; black or coloured acrylic lets the sign stand on its own as an object. The backing can be cut tight to the outline or kept as a simple rectangle.

Details decide how neat the result looks. Flex comes in several widths, commonly 6 mm, 8 mm and 12 mm, and small lettering is hard to form cleanly in thicker flex, so we may suggest a larger sign or a simpler font. Script lettering looks neatest as one connected line with few joins. For terraces and exterior walls, we specify sealed, outdoor-rated flex and connections, with the power supply kept out of the weather.

Materials

Silicone LED neon flex

Silicone gives an even, dot-free glow and tolerates heat and UV better than PVC-jacketed flex.

Clear acrylic backing

5 to 8 mm clear acrylic makes the lettering appear to float on the wall, with cable routed discreetly at the back.

Coloured and mirror acrylic

Black, pastel or mirror backings for signs that should read as a finished object even when switched off.

Outdoor-rated components

Sealed end caps, waterproof connectors and a protected adapter for terraces, pool areas and exterior walls.

Finishing options

Cut-to-shape backing

The acrylic follows the outline of the lettering with a small margin, for a lighter, more graphic look.

Printed backing

A logo or pattern printed or applied in vinyl on the acrylic behind the neon, combining artwork with glowing text.

Dimmer and remote

Brightness adjusts for daytime, evening or photography; RGB versions can change colour or run simple effects.

Artwork checklist

  • Send the design as vector artwork; single-line and script fonts convert most cleanly
  • Keep lowercase letters generously tall, as small text in thick flex fills in and loses shape
  • Mark which elements are neon and which are printed or cut on the backing
  • Choose the white: warm white suits cafés and hospitality, cool white suits modern retail
  • Tell us where the socket is, so we place the cable exit on the right side

Neon sign boards FAQs

Is LED neon the same as traditional neon?

It looks similar but works differently. Traditional neon is gas-filled glass tube running at high voltage. LED neon is a flexible silicone strip with LEDs inside, running on a low-voltage adapter. It is lighter, less fragile and simpler to install, which is why most neon-style signs in cafés, shops and events today use it.

Can neon signs be used outdoors?

Yes, if they are built for it. Outdoor signs need outdoor-rated flex, sealed joints and end caps, a protected power supply and UV-stable backing. Direct sun reduces how bright the neon appears during the day, so outdoor neon is most effective in shade, under canopies or for evening trade. Tell us the location when you brief us.

How big does my neon sign need to be?

Size depends on the wording and where it will be seen. Short phrases and simple logos work well from around 50 to 60 cm wide; longer wording or detailed logos need more width so each letter can be formed cleanly. Share the wall size and viewing distance, and we will propose a size and flex width.

Does LED neon get hot?

LED neon runs at low voltage and gets warm rather than hot, which is one reason it suits cafés, children's spaces and events. The adapter also warms up, so it needs ventilation and should not be buried under fabric or sealed in a box. In direct UAE sun, the sign heats up far more from sunlight than from the LEDs.

Can I get a neon sign for a single event?

Yes, neon signs are often made for weddings, launches and brand activations. As each sign is custom-made, timelines depend on size, design and quantity, and we confirm the schedule with your quote. A neutral design can be reused afterwards, for example in an office.
