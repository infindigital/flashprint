About sticker removal

Sticker removal in Dubai is harder than it looks. Vinyl that has spent years in the sun becomes brittle and breaks into small flakes instead of peeling in one piece, and the adhesive underneath hardens onto the surface. Forcing it off with the wrong tool can scratch glass, lift paint or leave a sticky film that stops new graphics from bonding.

The method depends on the surface. On glass, graphics are softened with gentle heat, lifted with plastic scrapers or a blade held flat where the glass allows, and residue is dissolved with an adhesive remover before a final clean. On vehicles, heat and slow, low-angle peeling protect the paint, and plastic tools are used around trims. Painted walls need the most care, because weakly bonded paint can come away with the vinyl.

Before full removal starts, we test a small area and tell you about any risk we find, such as poorly bonded paint, resprayed vehicle panels or coated glass. Removal is often booked as part of a rebrand or just before new graphics are fitted, so the surface is left clean and prepared for the next installation.

Materials

Glass and shopfront glazing

Usually the most forgiving surface. Old vinyl and residue come off with heat, scrapers and solvent, leaving clear glass for new graphics. Coated glass needs gentler methods.

Vehicle paintwork

Decals and wraps are warmed and peeled slowly at a low angle. Original paint generally releases cleanly, while resprayed or sun-damaged panels carry more risk and are checked first.

Painted walls

The most sensitive surface. Removal is tested first, and where paint lifts, we advise on touch-up or repainting before new graphics go on.

Metal, ACP and laminate

Sign panels, doors and counters generally tolerate heat and adhesive remover well, though solvents are tested on printed or laminated finishes first.

Sticker removal FAQs

Will removing stickers damage the glass?

Plain glass tolerates careful removal well. The vinyl is softened with heat, lifted with plastic or flat-held blades, and the residue is dissolved with a suitable remover. Coated or tinted glass needs gentler methods because blades and some solvents can mark the coating, so tell us about any coatings or films and we adjust the approach.

Can old decals come off our vehicles without harming the paint?

On original factory paint in good condition, decals and wraps can usually be removed cleanly using heat and slow, low-angle peeling. Paint that has been resprayed, repaired or weakened by sun is less predictable and may lift. We inspect each vehicle, test a small area first and tell you about any concerns before continuing.

What about stickers on painted walls?

Painted walls carry the most risk because the vinyl adhesive can be stronger than the bond between the paint and the wall. We test a small area first. If the paint starts to lift, we stop and discuss options, which may include slower removal or planning a touch-up or repaint before new graphics are applied.

Why is old vinyl so hard to remove?

Sun and heat make older vinyl brittle, so it cracks into small pieces instead of peeling off in one sheet, and the adhesive hardens onto the surface. Graphics that have been up for years on sun-facing glass usually take the longest, which is why we ask how long they have been in place when quoting.

Do you fit new graphics after removal?

Yes, and it is often the most practical order of work. Once old graphics and residue are gone, the surface is cleaned and degreased, and new vinyl, frosted film or vehicle graphics can be fitted on the same scheduled visit or soon after. Combining both steps avoids leaving a shopfront or vehicle unbranded between contractors.
