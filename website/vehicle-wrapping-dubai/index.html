Full Vehicle Wrapping in Dubai for Cars, Vans and Trucks

A full wrap covers every painted panel of the vehicle in printed or colour-change film, turning a plain car, van or pick-up into a moving billboard. It suits businesses that want maximum visibility from one vehicle, and it uses conformable cast film laminated for outdoor use.

About vehicle wrapping

Full vehicle wrapping in Dubai means covering the entire painted body, including bumpers, mirror caps and usually the pillars, with a single printed design or a solid colour film. Because the film has to wrap compound curves, recessed number-plate areas and bumper vents, we specify cast vinyl. It is thin and dimensionally stable enough to stretch into those shapes without shrinking back the way thicker calendered film can.

The design is built on an accurate outline of your exact model, then checked against photographs of the real vehicle so logos and phone numbers do not land on door handles, fuel flaps or panel gaps. Printed wraps are overlaminated with a matching cast laminate that protects the ink from UV and abrasion and sets the final sheen.

Windows are handled separately. Rear and rear-side glass can take perforated one-way film so the design continues while the driver still sees out; the windscreen and front side windows are normally left clear. Door shuts are not usually wrapped unless you ask, which keeps later removal cleaner.

Materials

Cast wrap vinyl

A thin, conformable film that stretches into recesses and around bumpers without pulling back. It is the standard choice for full wraps on curved modern bodywork.

Colour-change film

Unprinted film in solid gloss, satin, matte or metallic colours. Popular when you want a new base colour and will add printed or cut logos on top.

Cast overlaminate

A clear protective layer bonded over the printed film. It shields the ink from sun and brushes and decides the final gloss, satin or matte look.

Perforated window film

Printed vinyl with a pattern of small holes that shows the graphic from outside while keeping a view out from inside. Used on rear and rear-side glass.

Finishing options

Gloss

Deep colour and a paint-like shine that is easy to wipe clean. It does show minor surface imperfections more than satin.

Satin

A soft sheen that reads as premium on dark colours and hides light swirl marks from washing.

Matte

Flat and glare-free, striking on dark liveries and performance cars, but it needs gentler cleaning to keep an even appearance.

Metallic and textured films

Metallic, brushed or carbon-effect colour-change films, used as the base colour or for accents such as roofs and mirrors.

Where it’s used

  • Delivery vans carrying product imagery
  • Company cars for sales and service teams
  • Food trucks and mobile coffee vans
  • Launch vehicles for events and promotions
  • Show cars for real estate projects
  • Colour change on executive or showroom cars

Why choose us for vehicle wrapping

  • Coverage planned panel by panel

    We map each panel, edge and recess before printing, so film is produced with enough bleed and no panel comes up short.

  • Graphics placed around hardware

    Text and logos are positioned clear of handles, badges, sensors and door gaps, then proofed over the outline of your actual model.

  • Film and laminate matched

    Cast film is paired with a compatible cast laminate, so both layers behave the same way on curves and in heat.

  • Files kept for repairs

    We keep the final print files, so a single damaged door or bumper can be reprinted to match the rest of the wrap.

Artwork checklist

  • Tell us the exact make, model, year and body style, including roof height and wheelbase for vans
  • Send clear, straight-on photos of both sides, the front, the rear and the roof
  • Supply logos as vector files (AI, EPS or PDF with fonts outlined)
  • Photographs need to be around 100–150 dpi at full wrap size; small web images will not enlarge cleanly
  • Keep phone numbers, websites and small text away from door gaps, handles and body creases
  • Give Pantone references for brand colours so we can print a swatch on the chosen film

Vehicle wrapping FAQs

Does a full wrap damage the paint underneath?

On sound, factory-cured paint, correctly installed and removed wrap film sits on top of the clear coat rather than bonding into it. Problems usually come from resprayed panels, poor earlier repairs or chipped lacquer, which can lift when film is peeled away. We inspect the vehicle before starting and point out any areas where adhesion or removal could be a concern.

How should a wrapped vehicle be washed?

Hand washing with a mild, pH-neutral shampoo is the safest approach. Keep pressure-washer nozzles well away from film edges, avoid solvents and abrasive polishes, and never wax matte or satin films. Parking in shade whenever possible also helps, since constant direct sun is the biggest stress on any vehicle film in the UAE.

How long does the vehicle need to be off the road?

That depends on the vehicle size, how much trim has to come off and whether the design is printed or a colour change. Printing, drying and laminating the film all happen before the vehicle arrives, so it is only unavailable for the installation itself. We confirm the schedule with your quote before production starts.

Do I need a permit for advertising on my vehicle?

Commercial advertising on vehicles in Dubai may require a permit, and arranging it is the vehicle owner's responsibility. Requirements can depend on the vehicle type and the content shown. Once you have the approved design, we produce and install the wrap to match it exactly, so what appears on the road is what was submitted.
