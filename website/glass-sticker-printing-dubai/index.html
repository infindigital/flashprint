About glass sticker printing

Glass sticker printing in Dubai covers the everyday decals that every shop, café, clinic and office door needs. On their own they are small, but together they decide whether an entrance looks cared for. Faded opening hours and peeling logos send a signal before a customer has even pushed the door.

The main technical choice is which side of the glass the sticker goes on. A sticker applied outside is printed normally and is easy to replace, but it faces sun, dust and daily cleaning. A sticker applied inside and read from outside is printed in reverse on clear film, with a white layer behind the colours so they stay bright against the darker interior. Inside application keeps the print protected and much harder to peel off.

In glass sticker printing, film choice follows the look you want. Clear film gives a no-background finish where only the logo and text show. White vinyl suits solid decals and makes small text easier to read. Frosted and cut vinyl give a discreet finish for offices and hotels, and static cling film suits short promotions because it holds without adhesive and can be repositioned.

Materials

Clear vinyl with white underprint

Colours sit directly on the glass with no visible background, while the white layer keeps them opaque.

White vinyl

The most legible option for text-heavy decals such as opening hours and terms.

Frosted vinyl

A discreet etched look for office door logos and room names on glass.

Static cling film

No adhesive, so it can be moved or reused; handy for seasonal offers and short promotions.

Finishing options

Reverse print

Printed as a mirror image for inside application, reading correctly through the glass.

Double-sided print

A second image printed behind a blocking layer so the sticker reads from both sides.

Cut lettering

Individual letters with no background for a signwritten look.

Gloss or matt laminate

Protects outward-facing stickers from cleaning and sun.

Glass sticker printing FAQs

Should glass stickers go inside or outside?

Inside is usually better for anything long-term. The sticker is protected from weather, cleaning and tampering, and a reverse print with white backing reads clearly from the street. Outside mounting suits stickers that change often, such as promotions, and glass that is tinted or reflective enough to make an inside sticker hard to see.

Will stickers leave marks on the glass when removed?

Removable vinyl and static cling leave little or no residue on glass. Permanent vinyl can be removed too, with a scraper and a little heat, and any adhesive left behind cleans off with a suitable remover. Glass is one of the easiest surfaces to restore, which is why stickers suit leased shops and offices.

Can a glass sticker be read from both sides?

Yes. A double-sided sticker is printed with one image, a white or black blocking layer, and a second image on top, so it reads correctly from inside and outside. It is popular on glass doors and for 'Open' and 'Closed' messages. For a see-through effect from inside, perforated window film is the alternative.

How do I reduce sun fading on window stickers?

Laminate outward-facing stickers, choose good-quality polymeric or cast vinyl for long-term use, and apply them inside the glass where possible. Direct sun on a south- or west-facing shopfront in Dubai is demanding for any print, and bright reds and yellows show wear before darker colours. We recommend materials based on the orientation of your frontage.

Can you apply stickers to a shop inside a mall?

Yes. Malls usually set rules on what can be applied to shopfront glass and when installation can take place. Share the mall's retail design guidelines and we produce and install to them, normally outside trading hours. If the mall asks you to submit window graphics for review, we can supply mock-ups and specifications to go with your request.
