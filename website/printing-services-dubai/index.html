Flash Print Solution provides printing services in Dubai for organisations that want their printed material handled properly from the first brief to the final fitting. As a printing company in Dubai working with paper, card, vinyl, fabric, acrylic, board and metal, we produce everything from business cards and brochures to shopfront signs, vehicle graphics and product packaging. Most of our orders come from marketing teams, procurement managers, retail and hospitality operators, clinics, schools and contractors who would rather brief one supplier than visit a different print shop for every item.

Anyone searching for a printing press in Dubai usually needs more than ink on paper. Short runs and personalised items suit digital printing; long runs with strict colour requirements suit offset; banners, wall graphics and hoardings go through wide-format production; signs are fabricated and then installed. We recommend the process that fits your quantity, material and deadline, and explain the trade-offs so you can decide. Every order then follows the same route: brief, quote, artwork check, proof approval, production, finishing, quality check, and delivery or installation.

Digital and offset printing for paper products

Paper-based print is the backbone of most orders: stationery, flyers, brochures, catalogues, menus, certificates and forms. Digital printing handles short runs, variable data and jobs printed in stages, with no printing plates to make. Offset printing uses plates and a press setup that pays off on larger quantities, holding colour steady across thousands of identical sheets and accepting a wider choice of paper stocks.

The deciding factors are usually quantity, the number of versions and how exact the colour must be. When a job sits between the two methods, we quote both so you can compare before committing. Professional printing on paper is then completed with finishes such as matte or gloss lamination, soft-touch, spot UV, foiling, embossing, folding, perforating, numbering and binding.

Large format printing and signage

Anything bigger than a poster moves to wide-format production: roll-up and flex banners, event backdrops, window graphics, wall murals, floor graphics and construction hoardings. Graphics are printed onto roll or rigid media, then laminated, cut, hemmed or eyeleted according to where they will be displayed and for how long.

Signage adds fabrication to the process. Acrylic panels, aluminium composite (ACP) boards, 3D letters, light boxes and neon signs are cut and assembled in the workshop, then fitted at your premises, to the specification your landlord, mall management or the relevant authority has signed off.

Stickers, labels and packaging

Sticker and label work ranges from die-cut logo stickers and transparent window decals to barcode labels, product labels and regulatory stickers. The material matters more than most buyers expect: paper labels suit dry, indoor use; vinyl and PVC cope with moisture and handling; reflective and magnetic films serve vehicles and safety applications. Labels can be supplied on sheets or rolls, depending on how they will be applied.

Packaging printing covers custom boxes, product cartons, paper and kraft bags, sleeves, tags and inserts. Before a full run, a sample or plain structural mock-up can be requested to check fit, folds and how the artwork wraps across panels.

Corporate printing, branded items and vehicle graphics

Companies tend to reorder the same items: business cards for new joiners, letterheads, envelopes, presentation folders, company profiles and ID cards. Approved files and specifications are kept on record, so a repeat order starts from the last signed-off version rather than from scratch.

For branded items, the decoration method follows the product: printing or engraving on pens, USB drives and mugs; embroidery or heat transfer on caps and polo shirts; screen or transfer printing on T-shirts. Fleet branding and vehicle wraps are printed on vehicle-grade vinyl film and applied by installers.

Materials and finishes we work with

The substrate often matters more than the print itself. A banner for a weekend exhibition and a hoarding facing the Dubai summer need very different materials. These are the options buyers ask about most; we confirm stock and exact choices in your quote.

  • Papers and boards: uncoated, silk and gloss papers from 90 to 170 gsm, card from 250 to 400 gsm, plus kraft, textured and speciality stocks
  • Self-adhesive films: paper, white and clear vinyl, PVC, reflective, frosted, one-way vision and magnetic sheet
  • Large format media: PVC flex, fabric, backlit film, canvas, wallpaper and floor-grade laminates
  • Rigid boards: foam board, PVC foam board, ACP, acrylic, MDF and corrugated board
  • Metals and wood: aluminium, stainless steel and brass plates, and wood for engraving and cut lettering
  • Finishes: lamination, spot UV, foil, embossing, die-cutting, eyelets, hemming, mounting and stand-offs

How turnaround is planned

Timelines depend on quantity, material, finishing and how ready the artwork is, so the schedule is set out in your quote before production rather than taken from a generic lead-time table. A reprint of an approved business card and a fabricated illuminated sign are very different jobs, and the plan reflects that.

The two stages that most affect timing are the artwork check and proof approval. Complete, print-ready files and a clear sign-off keep a job moving. If you are working to a fixed event or opening date, share it at the brief stage so the plan can be built backwards from it. Our design team can prepare or correct files where needed.

Printed orders can be collected from our office in Business Bay or delivered to your address, with the arrangement confirmed per quote. Once installed, we can reprint damaged panels, remove old vinyl ahead of a refit and handle rebranding when a name or logo changes.

FAQs

Printing Services in Dubai: FAQs

Which printing services in Dubai do you cover?

We cover commercial printing such as business cards, letterheads, flyers, brochures and catalogues; large format including banners, backdrops, wall and window graphics; signage; stickers and labels; packaging; branded merchandise; apparel; and vehicle graphics. Design support, installation at your premises and maintenance of existing signs are also available. The category index on this page links to every product.

How long will my order take?

It depends on the product, quantity, finishing and whether your artwork is print-ready. A reprint of an approved file involves far fewer steps than a new fabricated sign. We confirm a production schedule with your quote and work to that plan once the proof is approved. If you have a fixed event or launch date, include it in the brief so we can tell you what is realistic.

Do you deliver and install?

Printed items can be collected from our Business Bay office or delivered, with the arrangement confirmed in your quote. Signage, wall graphics, window film and vehicle branding can be installed at your premises in Dubai. For building and shopfront signs, the client or landlord arranges any approvals, and we produce and fit to the approved specification.

What file format should I send?

A print-ready PDF is ideal: CMYK colour, fonts embedded or outlined, images at around 300 dpi at final size and 3 mm bleed on each edge of trimmed items. Signage and large format usually need vector artwork. If you only have a Word document, a JPG or a logo taken from your website, send it anyway; our design team can rebuild or correct files before proofing.

Can I meet you in person to discuss a print job?

Yes. Our office is in Silver Tower on Marasi Drive, Business Bay, open Monday to Saturday from 9:30 AM to 7:00 PM. Bringing a previous print job, paper samples or a colour reference helps us understand what you expect. Call or message on WhatsApp beforehand so the right person is available to go through materials and options with you.
