About name badge printing

Name badge printing in Dubai is ordered by hotels, clinics, retailers, banks and schools that want every staff member identifiable to a customer, and by event teams who need hundreds of badges printed from a delegate list. The fitting and the finish depend on how long the badge will be worn.

Staff badges for daily wear are usually metal or rigid plastic with a name, job title and logo. Magnetic fittings are the common choice now, because a two-part magnet holds through a shirt or blouse without pinholes. Pins suit heavier jackets and uniforms, and clips work on lanyards and pockets. Reusable holders take a printed paper insert behind a clear window, so a badge can be updated when a role changes without replacing the holder.

Button badges are a separate product used for campaigns, launches and events. A printed paper disc is crimped under a clear film onto a metal shell with a pin or magnet back. They are inexpensive, colourful and made in large quantities for staff to wear on launch days or for visitors to collect.

Name badge printing FAQs

Are magnetic name badges safe for everyone?

Magnetic badges use strong magnets, so people with pacemakers or other implanted medical devices are generally advised not to wear them. For those staff we supply the same badge with a pin or clip fitting. If you are ordering for a clinical or healthcare team, tell us and we can split the order by fitting.

Can badges show names in Arabic and English?

Yes. Names and titles can be printed in both languages on one badge. Please send Arabic names typed by your team rather than asking us to transliterate them, because spellings of names vary. We set both languages with appropriate typography and proof the full list before production.

What is the difference between a name badge and a button badge?

A name badge is a flat metal or plastic badge with a person's name, worn daily as part of a uniform. A button badge is a round printed badge crimped onto a metal shell, used for campaigns, events and giveaways. Name badges are personalised per person; button badges usually carry the same design in large quantities.

Can we reorder badges for new staff later?

Yes. Once a layout is approved we keep it on record, so new names can be added to the same design at any time. Send the new names in the same spreadsheet format. Small top-up orders are common for hotels, clinics and retailers with regular new joiners.
