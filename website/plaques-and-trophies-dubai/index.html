About plaques and trophies

Plaques and trophies in Dubai are ordered for moments people remember: a sales kick-off, a long-service dinner, a school sports day or a partner appreciation evening. The piece needs to look considered on stage and still read clearly afterwards on a shelf or an office wall.

Acrylic is the most versatile material. Thick clear or colour-backed acrylic can be laser cut to a custom shape, engraved, printed in full colour on the reverse, or layered with mirror gold and silver details. Wooden plaques in walnut, oak or bamboo carry either direct engraving or an engraved metal plate for a warm, traditional feel. Brass and stainless plates on wood or acrylic mounts suit formal recognition and dedication pieces.

Most orders combine one design with many names. We set up a master layout, then populate names, titles and dates from your list and proof the full set, so spelling can be checked before anything is engraved. Presentation boxes, pouches or printed sleeves can be added for the handover on the day.

Materials

Thick clear acrylic

Heavy, glass-like blocks and plaques with polished edges. Engraving shows as a clean frosted white mark.

Coloured and mirror acrylic

Layered onto clear acrylic to add brand colours or a gold or silver accent without paint.

Wood and bamboo

Warm, natural surfaces for traditional plaques, engraved directly or used as a mount for a metal plate.

Brass and stainless plates

Engraved plates fixed onto wood or acrylic mounts for formal recognition and dedication pieces.

Finishing options

Frosted engraving

Crisp white mark on clear acrylic, the classic look for recognition pieces.

Reverse colour print

Full-colour logo printed on the back of clear acrylic and seen through the front.

Gold or silver layering

Mirror acrylic shapes bonded to the face for a metallic highlight.

Paint-filled metal plates

Black or colour infill on engraved brass or steel so text reads clearly.

Plaques and trophies FAQs

Can each plaque have a different name?

Yes, and most orders work this way. We design one master layout with your logo and wording, then change the name, title or date on each piece from your spreadsheet. The full list is proofed before engraving so it can be checked by whoever is organising the event, which matters because an engraved name cannot be edited later.

What is the difference between acrylic and crystal trophies?

Crystal is optical glass: heavy, very clear and cold to the touch, and usually supplied in set shapes that are then engraved. Crystal-look acrylic is lighter, far less likely to break in transit, and can be laser cut into custom shapes and layered with colour. Both engrave to a frosted white finish. We confirm available crystal shapes with the quote.

Can you add our logo in colour?

Yes. On clear acrylic, a full-colour logo can be printed on the reverse so it shows through the front with depth. Alternatively, logo parts can be cut from coloured or mirror acrylic and layered on. On wood, colour usually comes from a printed or engraved metal plate, as direct engraving gives a single tone.

How should we plan plaques for an event?

Fix the design first and finalise names as late as the schedule allows, since late changes in winners or titles are common. Send the logo, wording and quantity early so the layout can be approved, then supply the name list. Timelines depend on quantity and finish, and we confirm the schedule with your quote.
