About branded pens

Branded pens in Dubai are one of the few promotional items people keep using long after an event. A pen left on a hotel desk, handed out at an exhibition or included in a welcome pack ends up in bags, cars and meeting rooms, carrying your name with it. The pen should reflect where it goes: a light plastic click pen for large events, a weightier metal pen for client gifts.

Branding method depends on the pen body. Pad printing transfers one or two solid colours onto curved plastic or metal barrels and clips, and suits simple logos. UV digital printing reproduces full-colour logos and gradients on flat or gently curved areas. Laser engraving removes the coating of anodised aluminium or lacquered metal pens, leaving a crisp mark that is part of the pen surface rather than a layer of ink.

Branding areas are small, typically around 40–50 mm long by 6–8 mm high on the barrel, so logos with thin lines or long taglines need simplifying. We show your logo on the chosen pen in a digital mock-up before production, and can pack pens in gift boxes or combine them with branded diaries and notebooks.

Branded pens FAQs

Which branding method should I choose for pens?

Pad printing is the usual choice for one or two-colour logos on plastic pens. UV printing suits full-colour logos, gradients or photos. Laser engraving works on metal, anodised aluminium and bamboo pens and gives a refined, understated mark. We recommend a method based on the pen you choose and the detail in your logo.

How much space is there for a logo on a pen?

Most pens offer an area of roughly 40–50 mm long by 6–8 mm high on the barrel, and less on the clip. That space is small, so a wide horizontal logo usually works best. Long taglines, small website text or detailed emblems may need simplifying. We show the exact area on a digital mock-up before production.

Can each pen be engraved with a different name?

Yes, on metal and bamboo pens suited to laser engraving. Personalised names are popular for executive gifts, graduation presents and staff recognition. Send the names in a spreadsheet and we proof them before engraving. Plastic pens branded by pad printing are generally produced with the same design on every pen.

Can pens be packed with diaries or other gifts?

Yes. Branded pens can be supplied bulk packed for events, in individual sleeves, in branded gift boxes, or combined with diaries, notebooks and other items as a gift set. Tell us how they will be handed out and we will suggest packaging that suits the occasion and keeps the pens protected in transit.
