About standee printing

Standee printing in Dubai covers the life-size cut-outs used for product launches, cinema promotions, brand ambassadors and photo opportunities at events, as well as freestanding display boards with a shaped top. A standee works because it occupies floor space at eye level, where a poster on the wall would be overlooked.

In the UAE the word is also used for roll-up banners, which are a different product: printed media on a retractable stand. If you need something that packs into a carry bag, a roll-up banner will suit better. A rigid standee is printed directly onto board and contour-cut to the shape of a person, product or logo, so the outline itself does part of the talking.

Stability is the practical issue. A tall, light board catches air conditioning and passing shoppers, so we size the easel strut, add a weighted base or design slot-together feet depending on the height and location. For outdoor or longer placements we move from foam board to foamex, which is heavier and handles knocks better.

Materials

Foam board

Very light with a smooth print surface. Good for indoor, short-term standees and event photo cut-outs.

Foamex (PVC foam board)

Denser and tougher, for standees that will be moved between locations, handled or reused.

Corrugated board

Recyclable and easy to ship flat, often chosen for multi-store campaigns produced in volume.

Aluminium composite panel

A rigid, weather-resistant board for outdoor standees or premium tabletop pieces.

Finishing options

Matte lamination

Protects the print and removes glare for photos taken beside the standee.

Contour cutting

Follows the outline of a person, product or logo, leaving no background board visible.

Face cut-outs

Openings where visitors put their face through for photos at events and store activations.

Where it’s used

  • Product launch cut-outs at store entrances
  • Brand ambassador and mascot figures
  • Event and wedding photo standees
  • Cinema and entertainment promotions
  • Mall atrium activations
  • Welcome boards at receptions and venues

Industries we support with standee printing: Retail, Hotels & Hospitality, Restaurants & Cafés, Real Estate.

Why choose us for standee printing

  • Stability planned for the site

    Supports are designed around the standee's height, weight and where it will stand.

  • Cut to the real outline

    Contour cutting follows the figure or product, so the standee reads as the object itself.

Artwork checklist

  • Supply a high-resolution image with a clean edge or transparent background for cutting
  • Images need around 150 dpi at full size, so a 1.8 m figure needs a very large original
  • Add a cut-line path, or tell us the outline you want and we will create it
  • Decide whether the cut follows the figure tightly or leaves a small border around it
  • For face cut-outs, mark the opening position and the typical height of your visitors

Standee printing FAQs

What is the difference between a standee and a roll-up banner?

A standee is a rigid board, often cut to the shape of a person or product, that stands on an easel back or base. A roll-up banner is flexible printed media on a retractable aluminium stand that packs into a bag. Standees make more visual impact at an entrance; roll-ups are easier to carry between events.

Will a tall standee stay upright in a busy store?

Stability depends on the support. Tall standees get extra easel struts, a weighted base or wider feet, especially near doors and air conditioning vents. For very busy spaces we can fix the figure to a flat plinth. Tell us where the standee will stand and we design the support to match.

Can standees be used outdoors?

Foam board is for indoor use only. For outdoor or semi-outdoor spots, foamex or aluminium composite panel is more suitable, with a heavy base or ground fixing, since wind is the main risk. Strong direct sun also fades printed colours over time, so shaded placement is better for longer displays.

Can you make a standee from a photo of a person?

Yes, if the photo is sharp and large enough to print at the intended height. Full-length shots against a plain background give the cleanest cut. We check resolution before printing and can retouch edges or extend backgrounds. Make sure you have permission to use the person's image for promotional purposes.
