About corporate gift sets

Corporate gift sets in Dubai carry a season. Year-end client gifting, Ramadan and Eid hampers, National Day packs, new-joiner welcome kits and conference giveaways each want a different mix of items and a different level of presentation. The set is built backwards from that occasion rather than from a fixed product list.

Most sets pair a writing item with something practical: an A5 diary and a metal pen, a vacuum flask and a ceramic mug, or a power bank, a cable set and a card holder. Bamboo and cork ranges give a sustainability story, while black and gunmetal ranges read as executive. Every item in a set is branded with the method that suits its surface, so a diary is debossed, a flask is engraved and a cork power bank is printed.

Presentation is half the gift. A fitted foam or card insert holds each item in place, the lid can be foiled or debossed, and a ribbon pull, belly band or printed card finishes it. We quote the box alongside the items so the whole set has one specification rather than being assembled from parts later.

Materials

Rigid board box

Greyboard wrapped in printed or textured paper, with a magnetic or lift-off lid. The usual choice when the box itself is part of the gift.

Kraft board box

Uncoated brown board with a natural surface. Pairs well with bamboo, cork and recycled items, and takes a one-colour print cleanly.

Foam insert

Die-cut EVA foam that holds each item in a recess. Keeps a set looking arranged after a courier run.

Moulded pulp insert

Recycled fibre tray moulded to the item shapes, used where the whole set needs to stay paper-based.

Finishing options

Hot foil on the lid

Gold, silver, rose gold or a colour foil pressed into the box wrap for a crisp metallic logo.

Blind deboss

Logo pressed into a textured wrap with no ink or foil, for a quiet, tactile finish.

Spot UV

Gloss varnish over a matt laminated lid, so the logo shows as a change of sheen rather than colour.

Ribbon and band

Satin ribbon pull inside the box or a printed belly band around it, adding colour without reprinting the box.

Artwork checklist

  • Send one vector logo (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) plus a one-colour version for engraving and debossing
  • Give Pantone references so foil, print and thread choices can be matched across different items
  • Confirm the item list and quantity per item before artwork, because branding areas differ by product
  • Supply any insert card or greeting text as editable copy, not as a flattened image
  • Tell us the delivery format: assembled sets, bulk cartons or split across several addresses

Corporate gift sets FAQs

How many items should a corporate gift set contain?

Most sets hold two to four items. Two works for a large staff run, usually a notebook and a pen. Three or four suits client gifting, often adding a flask, power bank or card holder. Beyond that the box grows and the set becomes harder to courier. We normally shape the set around the budget per recipient and the occasion.

Can every item in the set carry our logo?

Yes, and each one takes the method that suits its surface. A PU diary is debossed or foiled, a metal flask and pen are laser engraved, a cork power bank is printed and a ceramic mug is sublimated. We proof all of them together on one sheet so you can see the logo at its actual size on each item before anything is decorated.

Do you supply the gift boxes as well?

Yes. Boxes are printed and made alongside the items, with a die-cut foam or card insert shaped to hold each piece. Lids can be foiled, debossed or printed in full colour, and we can add ribbon pulls, belly bands and printed cards. Quoting the box with the contents keeps the fit correct and avoids a second supplier.

Can sets be delivered to more than one address?

Yes. Tell us at the brief stage how the sets are being distributed, because it changes how they are packed. Sets going to individual recipients are usually assembled, wrapped and boxed for courier, while sets handed out at an event are often packed in bulk cartons by item. Delivery details are confirmed with your quote.
