About shelf talkers

Shelf talkers in Dubai supermarkets, pharmacies and electronics stores do their work in the last metre of the shopper's journey. The shopper is already standing at the shelf; the talker points to one product among dozens and gives a reason to pick it up. Short copy, a strong colour and a clear product image matter more than detail.

Formats vary by fixture. Flat talkers slot into the price channel on the shelf edge or hang just below it, while blades and flaggers project out at 90 degrees so they can be seen from further down the aisle. Shelf strips run the full width of the shelf in the data channel, turning a row of products into a brand block. Channel heights differ between retailers, so we size strips and tabs to the actual fixture.

For short promotions, 300 to 350 gsm card with lamination is sufficient and easy to replace. In chillers, freezers and fresh sections, condensation makes card curl, so we switch to 0.3 to 0.5 mm PVC or polypropylene that stays flat and can be wiped clean.

Materials

Laminated card

Rigid and sharply printed for dry shelves, with lamination to resist handling. Best for campaigns of a few weeks.

PVC sheet

Stays flat and moisture resistant in chillers and freezers, where card would soften and curl.

Polypropylene

Flexible and tough, good for blades and flaggers that get knocked by trolleys and baskets.

Magnetic sheet

For steel shelving where no adhesive is allowed, so talkers can be moved between shelves.

Finishing options

Gloss lamination

Makes colours punchy under store lights and helps card resist fingerprints.

Matte lamination

Reduces glare where lights sit directly above the shelf.

Shaped die-cut

Starbursts, arrows or product silhouettes that break the straight line of the shelf edge.

Artwork checklist

  • Measure the shelf channel height and length, or ask the retailer for the specification
  • Keep copy to a few words that can be read at arm's length
  • Use our die-line for shaped talkers and keep text 3 mm from cut edges
  • Include the fixing tab area in the template if the talker slots into a channel
  • Avoid placing important text where price tickets will cover it

Shelf talkers FAQs

What is the difference between a shelf talker and a shelf strip?

A shelf talker is a single sign for one product or offer, either flat on the shelf edge or projecting from it. A shelf strip runs the full length of the shelf in the data channel, creating a continuous band of branding across a block of products. Many campaigns use both together.

What material should we use in a chiller?

Choose PVC or polypropylene for chillers and freezers. Condensation forms every time a door opens, and card absorbs it, softens and curls. Plastic materials stay flat, wipe clean and do not stain. For freezers, ask us about suitable adhesives, because standard tapes can lose grip at low temperatures.

How do shelf talkers attach to the shelf?

Most slot into the clear price channel on the shelf edge using a tab, while others use a small adhesive strip, a clip or a magnet. Retailers often have rules about adhesives on their fixtures, so confirm the method before we cut the tabs. Blades and flaggers usually use a clip that grips the shelf lip.

Can you print different versions for different offers?

Yes. Talkers can be printed in several versions in the same run, for different products, prices or languages, with each version counted and packed separately. Digital printing makes short quantities of many versions practical, which suits promotions that change by store or by week.
