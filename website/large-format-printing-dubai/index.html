Large format printing in Dubai covers anything too big for a sheet-fed press: a 6 m building banner, a roll-up for a clinic reception, printed vinyl for a glass partition or a translucent graphic for a mall light box. The work is printed on wide-format roll printers onto flexible media, then hemmed, welded, laminated, cut or mounted depending on where it will live.

Choosing the right substrate matters more at this scale than it does on paper. A banner that suits an air-conditioned exhibition hall will struggle on a windy rooftop in August, and a vinyl that peels cleanly from glass is not the one you want on a textured wall. This hub explains the main options so you can brief us with confidence; we confirm stock, sizes and finishing in your quote.

Choosing media for indoor and outdoor conditions

Outdoors, the UAE climate is the deciding factor. Strong UV fades weak inks, a sun-facing wall gets far hotter than the air around it, and coastal humidity and dust settle on everything. For exterior work we lean towards PVC flex or mesh printed with solvent or UV-curable inks, reinforced hems and fixings sized for the wind at that height.

Indoors, the priorities shift to colour, flatness and finish. Roll-up films, polyester fabric and laminated vinyl give sharper detail at close viewing distances, without the slight sheen and texture of outdoor flex. Anything lit from behind is printed on backlit film or fabric with a denser ink layer, so colours hold up when the lamps are on.

  • PVC flex in frontlit, backlit and blockout grades for banners and sign faces
  • Mesh banner for fences, scaffolds and exposed high-level positions
  • Self-adhesive vinyl, from monomeric to cast, with matt or gloss laminate
  • Backlit film and fabric for light boxes and illuminated frames
  • Foam board and rigid boards for short-term indoor signs

Banners, stands or vinyl: matching the format to the job

A fixed banner is the right call when you need a large message in one place for a set period: a sale, a launch, a site announcement. Portable stands suit teams who move between venues. A roll-up retracts into its own base and packs into a carry bag, while an X-banner is lighter still and its graphic is easier to swap between campaigns.

Printed vinyl is for surfaces you already have, such as glass, walls, doors, boards and counters. It can be cut to shape, laminated against scuffs and chosen with removable adhesive for short campaigns. Backlit displays sit at the other end of the range, turning an illuminated frame into a sign that reads by day and after dark.

Artwork and planning at large sizes

Resolution at large sizes is governed by viewing distance rather than a fixed number. A roll-up read at arm's length needs around 150 ppi at full size, while a building banner seen from the road looks sharp at 30 to 50 ppi. Logos and text should stay as vector artwork at any size.

Allow bleed and safe areas for hems, eyelets, pole pockets and the hidden base of a roll-up, and state the final size on every file. Very large banners are often supplied at 1:10 scale. If you only have a logo and a rough idea, our design team can set up or correct the files for you.

  • Brief: size, location, indoor or outdoor, fixing method and quantity
  • Quote with confirmed media, finishing and schedule
  • Artwork check and digital proof for your sign-off
  • Printing, finishing and a quality check before dispatch
  • Delivery, or installation at your premises in Dubai

FAQs

Large Format & Signage FAQs

What is the difference between a banner and a flex banner?

Banner is the general term for any flexible printed sign that hangs or is stretched in place. A flex banner is one made from PVC flex, the coated polyester material used for most outdoor banners in the UAE. Mesh, fabric and vinyl banners are alternatives with different weight, finish and wind behaviour, and each suits different locations.

Which banner material works outdoors in Dubai?

Frontlit PVC flex is the usual choice for walls and shopfronts because it handles sun, dust and humidity and takes eyelets well. Where a banner is large and exposed, such as on a fence, scaffold or rooftop, PVC mesh lets wind pass through and reduces the load on fixings. We check the position and height before recommending one.

Do you install banners and large graphics?

Yes. Installation is available at your premises in Dubai, from shopfront banners and window vinyl to high-level banners where safe access can be arranged. Tell us the fixing surface and height when you brief us, and include photos if you can, so the quote covers the right fixings and access for the job.

How long does large format printing take?

Timelines depend on quantity, size, finishing and how ready the artwork is. A single roll-up and a set of welded building banners are very different jobs. We confirm the schedule with your quote before production, so you can plan around an opening, event or site handover date.
