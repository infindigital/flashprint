About branded caps

Branded caps in Dubai are worn in strong sun, so fabric and fit matter as much as the logo. A six-panel cotton twill baseball cap is the everyday choice for staff and promotions. Mesh-back trucker caps let heat escape and suit outdoor crews, while lightweight polyester sports caps with vented panels work for runs, golf days and beach activations.

Embroidery is the most common decoration. Your logo is digitised into a stitch file and sewn directly into the front panel. Flat embroidery suits most logos; 3D puff embroidery raises bold lettering over a foam underlay for a structured, dimensional look. Gradients, hairlines and very small text do not translate well into thread, so we may suggest a simplified version of the logo for the cap, or a woven patch that holds finer detail.

Printed options include DTF and heat transfer for full-colour logos or campaign artwork, often chosen for event caps where exact colour matters more than texture. Side and back positions are useful for a website address, a slogan, a sponsor mark or an individual name.

Materials

Cotton twill

Structured, matte fabric that holds embroidery neatly and keeps its shape. The default for staff caps and corporate gifting.

Brushed cotton

Softer, slightly peached surface with a relaxed look. Suits unstructured dad-style caps for lifestyle and café brands.

Polyester sports fabric

Lightweight and moisture-wicking, often with laser-cut vents. A comfortable pick for running events and outdoor teams.

Mesh-back trucker

Foam or twill front panel with open mesh behind. Popular for promotional handouts and staff working outdoors.

Finishing options

Flat embroidery

Stitched logo lying flush with the panel. Handles most logos with a handful of thread colours.

3D puff embroidery

Foam underlay lifts the stitching for a bold, raised logo. Best for thick lettering and simple shapes.

Woven or PVC patch

Logo made separately and sewn on, keeping detail that direct stitching cannot reproduce.

DTF or heat transfer

Full-colour print pressed onto the panel for gradients and photographic artwork on smooth fronts.

Artwork checklist

  • Send a vector logo (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF); we digitise it into a stitch file for embroidery
  • Keep lettering at least 5–6 mm tall for embroidery, or expect us to suggest a simplified version
  • Give thread colours as Pantone references so we can match the nearest thread shade
  • Avoid gradients and fine hairlines for stitching; choose a patch or print if they are essential
  • Tell us the cap colour early so we can check contrast, such as white thread on navy

Branded caps FAQs

Is embroidery or printing better for caps?

Embroidery suits most corporate caps because it looks structured, sits well on twill and has no ink layer to crack. Printing is better when the logo has gradients, many colours or photographic detail, or when a lightweight sports cap would pucker under dense stitching. We look at your artwork and the cap style together, then recommend one method and explain why.

What is 3D puff embroidery?

It is embroidery stitched over a layer of foam, so the finished logo stands raised from the cap front. It works best on bold, simple shapes and thick letters at least a few millimetres wide. Thin strokes and small text do not puff well, so logos often combine raised main lettering with flat-stitched detail around it.

Can each cap have a different name?

Yes. Names, initials or numbers can be embroidered or printed on the side or back of each cap while the main logo stays on the front. Send a spreadsheet listing each name against a cap size and colour if those vary. We set one layout for the name position and proof the list before production.

Which cap colours are available?

Common stock colours include black, navy, white, grey, khaki, red and royal blue, with two-tone and trucker combinations in many styles. Availability varies by style and quantity, so we confirm exact colours and stock in your quote. If you need a close brand match, send the Pantone reference and we will show the nearest options.
