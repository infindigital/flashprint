About branded water bottles

Branded water bottles in Dubai have moved from a giveaway to a staple. Offices supplying them instead of single-use plastic, site teams needing something that keeps water cold through a shift, and event organisers filling delegate bags all order the same category for different reasons, and the wall construction is what separates them.

Double-wall vacuum stainless steel is the workhorse. The gap between the two walls slows heat transfer, so a drink stays cold or hot for hours and the outside stays dry with no condensation ring on a desk. Single-wall stainless is lighter and cheaper to fill a delegate bag with. Tritan and RPET sports bottles suit gyms and outdoor activations, and borosilicate glass with a silicone sleeve suits meeting rooms and hospitality.

Branding follows the surface. A laser engraves through the powder coat on stainless and leaves a bright metal mark that will never wash off in a dishwasher. UV and 360-degree rotary printing wrap full-colour artwork around the body, which is what you want for a campaign graphic. Pad printing covers small one-colour marks on lids, sleeves and plastic bodies.

Materials

Double-wall stainless steel

Two steel walls with a vacuum between them. Keeps drinks cold in summer heat and leaves no condensation on a desk or a meeting table.

Single-wall stainless steel

Lighter and slimmer, without insulation. A practical choice for large delegate runs where weight and volume matter.

Tritan and RPET

Clear, impact-resistant plastics. RPET bodies are made from recycled bottles, which gives a sustainability line to the gift.

Borosilicate glass

Clear glass body with a silicone sleeve or bamboo collar. Reads well in meeting rooms and hotel settings where plastic looks out of place.

Finishing options

Laser engraving

Burns through the powder coat to the steel beneath, leaving a permanent silver mark that survives dishwashing.

360-degree rotary UV

Prints full-colour artwork around the whole body, so a pattern or photo can wrap without a visible join.

UV print

Flat full-colour panel printed onto one face. Good for logos with gradients that engraving would reduce to one tone.

Pad printing

One or two spot colours applied to lids, sleeves and small curved areas where a flat print head cannot reach.

  • Delegate bags at conferences and exhibitions
  • Gym, running and cycling event giveaways
  • Hotel meeting rooms and guest welcome sets

Why choose us for branded water bottles

  • Right wall for the use

    We ask where the bottle will be used before recommending one, because an insulated flask and a delegate-bag bottle are different products.

  • Engraving proofed on your colour

    Powder coats engrave to slightly different tones, so you see the mark proofed on the actual bottle colour you have chosen.

  • Packed as a set if needed

    Bottles can be boxed on their own or built into a gift set with a mug, notebook or lunch box in one printed box.

Artwork checklist

  • Send a vector logo (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF); engraving needs a one-colour version
  • Keep engraved text above about 2 mm tall so the laser holds the counters in small letters
  • For a full wrap, supply artwork as a flat rectangle with 3 mm bleed on all four edges
  • Give Pantone references if the print has to match a brand colour on a coloured body
  • Tell us the bottle colour early; a light engraving mark on a pale coat has less contrast

Branded water bottles FAQs

Should we choose engraving or printing on a metal bottle?

Engraving is the better pick for anything that will be washed often, because the mark is cut into the steel rather than sitting on top of the coating. It gives one tone, usually silver against the body colour. Choose printing when the logo has several colours or a gradient, or when the artwork needs to wrap around the whole bottle.

Are branded bottles dishwasher safe?

Most stainless bodies are hand-wash recommended and most lids should not go through a machine, because heat can affect seals and printed areas. A laser-engraved logo is unaffected either way, since it is a mark in the metal. Exact care advice varies by model, so we confirm it for the specific bottle you choose with your quote.

What capacity works best for staff bottles?

For desk use, 500 ml suits most people and fits in a bag without being heavy. Site and outdoor teams usually prefer 750 ml or 1 litre so they refill less often. For conference delegate bags, 500 ml single-wall bottles keep the bag weight down. We can quote two sizes together if you want to compare.

Can each bottle carry a staff member's name?

Yes. Names can be engraved below the logo on stainless bodies, which is common for office refill schemes where bottles otherwise get mixed up. Send the names in a spreadsheet, checked for spelling and in the order you want them packed. We proof the list once, then engrave and pack each bottle to that sequence.
