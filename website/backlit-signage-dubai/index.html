Backlit Signage in Dubai: Displays and Light Box Graphics

Backlit graphics are printed on translucent media so they glow evenly when light passes through them. We produce faces for menu boards, mall light boxes, snap frames and fabric light walls, and can supply slim illuminated frames for new displays or print replacements for units you already have.

About backlit signage

Backlit signage in Dubai is everywhere once you notice it: menu boards behind café counters, product visuals in mall corridors, lit posters in gyms and clinic waiting areas. Each display has two parts, an illuminated frame and the printed graphic in front of it. This page is about that graphic and the display units it sits in; for fabricated storefront boxes built for the facade, see our LED light box signage page.

Printing for light is different from printing for daylight. A normal print that looks right on a wall will wash out when lit from behind, so backlit media carries more ink. For displays that switch off after hours or sit near windows, a day/night print adds a second, registered image on the reverse so the graphic reads correctly both lit and unlit.

The media choice follows the frame. Snap frames and slim light panels take backlit film, a thin polyester sheet with a diffusing coating. Larger boxes often use backlit flex. Silicone-edge graphics (SEG) are printed fabric with a thin silicone bead sewn around the edge, pressed into a channel in an aluminium frame for a smooth, frameless look that is simple to change.

Materials

Backlit film

A translucent polyester film with a diffusing coating, used in snap frames and slim lit panels. It holds very sharp detail for close viewing.

Backlit flex

Translucent PVC flex for larger boxes and exterior faces, where a rigid film would be impractical to fit.

SEG backlit fabric

Dye-printed polyester with a silicone edge that lights evenly across large areas, with no visible clips or frame profile.

Opal acrylic

For permanent faces, print or translucent vinyl can go on 3 to 5 mm opal acrylic, which spreads LED light smoothly.

Finishing options

Day/night print

A second registered image on the reverse adds density, so colours stay rich when lit and still look right in daylight.

Silicone edge (SEG)

A flexible silicone bead sewn around the fabric edge, so the graphic presses into the frame channel and pulls taut.

Protective overlaminate

A clear film over backlit prints in food outlets and public areas, so the face can be wiped without scratching the image.

Artwork checklist

  • Give us both the visible (sight) size and the full graphic size; frames often hide 10 to 20 mm at each edge
  • Avoid large flat areas of pale tint, which reveal uneven lighting inside older boxes
  • Use 150 ppi or more at full size, as backlit displays are often viewed at close range
  • For day/night prints, keep fine text away from the edges so both layers register cleanly
  • Send brand colour references; colours shift when lit, so we may suggest density adjustments

Backlit signage FAQs

Why does my backlit poster look faded when the light is on?

Usually it was printed as a standard print, with the ink density meant for reflected light. When lamps shine through it, the colour washes out. Backlit media printed at higher density, or as a day/night print, keeps colours strong. Very bright or unevenly spaced lamps can also flatten colour, which we can check from photos of the box.

What is the difference between backlit displays and LED light box signs?

Backlit displays, on this page, are the printed translucent graphics and the frames that hold them, mostly indoors: menu boards, poster frames and fabric light walls. LED light box signs are fabricated, weather-sealed boxes built as permanent signage on a facade or projecting from a wall. The printing principles overlap, but construction and installation differ.

Can you print new faces for our existing menu light boxes?

Yes. We need the visible area, the full size of the insert and how it fits, whether in a snap frame, behind a clear cover or slid into a channel. Photos help. Replacing only the graphics is a common way to update menus and promotions without changing the fixtures, and we can print one display or a full set across branches.

Do backlit prints work for outdoor signs?

Yes, with suitable media and a weather-sealed box. Backlit flex, or translucent vinyl on opal acrylic, is used for exterior faces, with UV-resistant inks. Backlit film is mostly for indoor or sheltered frames. For a complete outdoor lit sign including fabrication, electrics and installation, our LED light box signage page is the better starting point.
