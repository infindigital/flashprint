About exam paper printing

Exam paper printing in Dubai is about accuracy and handling far more than decoration. A question paper with a missing page, a duplicated page or a misprinted diagram can disrupt an entire exam session, so collation and page checks are the core of the job. We print from your locked PDF, collate booklets in page order, staple or fold them, and check sample sets from across the run before packing.

Papers are usually printed in black on 80 gsm uncoated paper, which takes pen and pencil well and scans cleanly if you mark digitally. Colour pages can be included for maps, charts or science diagrams where colour carries meaning. Bubble answer sheets and lined answer booklets can be produced to your layout; if a scanner will read them, we match the paper and tolerances to the scanner supplier's specification.

Confidential handling is arranged as a set of steps agreed with you before the job starts. Typical requests include accepting files only from named staff, keeping the job together from printing to packing, sealing papers in envelopes or boxes labelled by class, subject and session, and handing them over only to the contact you nominate.

Materials

80 gsm uncoated paper

The standard exam sheet. Light enough for thick booklets to staple neatly, and it takes ballpoint, gel pen and pencil without smudging.

100 gsm uncoated paper

A slightly heavier sheet with less show-through, useful for double-sided papers with dense diagrams or when students press hard.

Pale coloured paper

Yellow, blue, green or pink sheets help invigilators tell versions, sets or subjects apart without reading the cover.

Lined and grid answer paper

Printed with lines, margins or graph grids to your layout for extended answers, calculations and sketches.

Finishing options

Corner stapling

A single staple in the top-left corner for short papers of up to around 20 sheets. Easy to remove if pages are scanned for marking.

Saddle stitching

A3 sheets folded and stapled through the spine to form A4 booklets. Pages lie flat on the desk and stay in order.

Hole punching

Two or four holes for papers that will be filed in ring binders once marking is complete.

Sealed packing

Sets counted into envelopes or boxes, sealed and labelled with class, subject, session and count, when you request it.

Why choose us for exam paper printing

  • Collation checked across the run

    Sample sets from the start, middle and end of each run are checked for page order, print quality and staple position.

  • Handling steps agreed upfront

    Sealed packing, labelling and named hand-over are set out with you before production, so there are no surprises on exam day.

  • Version coding options

    Coloured paper, printed version codes or different cover colours help separate parallel papers sat in the same hall.

Artwork checklist

  • Send a locked PDF with fonts embedded so equations and symbols print exactly as written
  • Number every page and show the total on each, for example Page 3 of 12
  • Keep a 10 mm margin free of text on the stapled edge
  • Make booklet page counts a multiple of four for saddle stitching
  • Supply diagrams and graphs at 300 dpi or as vectors so thin lines stay sharp
  • Include a packing list with the number of copies per class or room

Exam paper printing FAQs

Can papers be packed and labelled by class?

Yes. Send a packing list showing each class or room, the subject, session and number of students, plus any spares you want. Papers are counted into envelopes or boxes, sealed and labelled with those details. Packs can be handed over only to the person you name, and we confirm the arrangement with your quote.

What paper weight should we use?

80 gsm uncoated paper is the usual choice for question papers and answer booklets. It is easy to write on, staples neatly in thicker booklets and suits scanning. Choose 100 gsm when papers are double-sided with dense diagrams or photographs, as the heavier sheet reduces show-through from the reverse.

Can you print answer sheets for machine marking?

We can print bubble answer sheets and marking grids to your template. If the sheets will be read by a scanner or marking software, send us the system's layout specification, including paper weight and position tolerances, so we can match it. We recommend running a test batch through your scanner before the full print run.

How many pages can a stapled booklet have?

Corner stapling suits short papers, typically up to about 20 sheets. Saddle-stitched booklets are made from folded A3 sheets, so the page count must be a multiple of four, and around 48 pages is a practical upper limit at 80 gsm. Longer papers can be split into sections or wire-bound instead.

Do you keep copies of our exam files?

Tell us your file-handling requirements before the job starts. We can agree who is allowed to send and receive files, limit access to the staff working on your order, and delete working files once you have confirmed receipt. Specific requests, such as returning misprinted sheets to you rather than recycling them, can be added.
