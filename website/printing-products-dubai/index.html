Foam Board Printing

Foam board prints are lightweight rigid boards, commonly 3 mm, 5 mm or 10 mm thick, with the graphic printed directly or mounted as laminated vinyl. They suit easel signs, seating plans, photo displays, event welcome boards and short-term retail signs. Foam board dents and warps in heat and damp, so keep it indoors.

EnquireWhatsApp

One-Way Vision

One-way vision is a perforated window film printed on its white face with a black adhesive side. From outside, people see the full graphic; from inside, the glass stays largely see-through. Common perforation ratios are 50/50 and 70/30. It is used on shopfronts, office glazing and vehicle rear windows, usually with an optically clear laminate outdoors.

Construction Boards

Construction boards are rigid printed panels for yards, laydown areas and work zones, carrying site rules, hazard notices, contact numbers and area names. Common builds are 3 mm ACP, 5 mm foamed PVC or corrugated plastic with eyelets or drilled holes for fence and post fixing. Entrance PPE boards and project signs sit in our Construction & Site Printing range.

Floor Stickers

Floor stickers mark pedestrian walkways, forklift crossings, keep-clear zones in front of fire equipment and stand-here points at counters. They are printed on floor-grade vinyl and sealed under an anti-slip textured laminate, as arrows, footprints, circles or strips typically 200 to 600 mm across, for smooth, sealed indoor floors.
