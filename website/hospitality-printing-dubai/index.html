Hospitality printing in Dubai works harder than most print. A menu is handled by hundreds of guests, wiped between services and read in low light; a door hanger is hung, removed and dropped; a table mat meets coffee, sauce and cleaning spray. The right material decides whether a piece still looks good at the end of the month, not just on the first day.

The range suits restaurants, cafés, hotels, beach clubs, serviced apartments and catering companies, from a single wine list to a menu refresh across several outlets. This hub covers menus, table mats and room signage, and links to door hangers and tent cards, which sit in our marketing print range. Stock, sizes and finishes are confirmed in your quote.

Materials that cope with service

Front-of-house print faces moisture, grease and repeated cleaning. Laminated card is the default for menus and table talkers because a matt or gloss film seals the surface. Synthetic papers, made from polypropylene or polyester, go further: they do not absorb water and resist tearing, which suits pool bars, terraces and children's menus.

Disposable items such as placemats, tray liners and takeaway menus are a different calculation. Uncoated or lightly coated paper prints well, suits high volumes and can be replaced after every guest, so there is no need for heavy stock or lamination. Tray liners and colouring mats are the obvious examples, and both double as advertising space for offers, apps and loyalty schemes.

  • Laminated 300–400 gsm card for menus, tent cards and door hangers
  • Synthetic paper for outdoor dining and high-handling pieces
  • 80–120 gsm paper for disposable table mats and tray liners
  • Acrylic, aluminium and engraved plates for room and facility signs

Keeping outlets and languages consistent

Hotels and restaurant groups often run several outlets with different menus but one identity. We set up templates for menus and table cards so dishes and prices can change without the layout drifting, and keep colour references on file between reprints so a reorder matches the last batch. One set of brand guidelines can then cover every outlet's menus, mats and table cards.

Bilingual layouts are routine in Dubai. English and Arabic need different line lengths and reading directions, so we plan layouts with both languages from the start rather than squeezing Arabic in at the end. Calorie information, allergen icons and QR codes also need space designed in, not added as an afterthought.

Guest-room and back-of-house print

Beyond the restaurant, hotels and serviced apartments need door hangers, in-room directories, amenity cards, laundry lists and room signage. Many of these are small runs refreshed often, which suits digital printing; larger standard items can move to offset printing once quantities justify it.

Room number plates, suite names and corridor signs are longer-term pieces, usually made in acrylic, brushed aluminium or engraved materials chosen to match the interior. We can produce them to the hotel's wayfinding plan, alongside the printed items, so fonts and colours stay aligned from the lobby to the room.

FAQs

Hospitality Printing FAQs

Which menu material works for outdoor seating?

Synthetic paper is the usual choice for terraces, pool bars and beach clubs. It is printed like card but made from polypropylene or polyester, so it does not absorb water, resists tearing and wipes clean. Laminated card also works outdoors, but water can eventually get in at trimmed edges or around drilled holes if the menu stays wet.

Can you print menus and signage in English and Arabic?

Yes. Bilingual layouts can be set side by side, on facing pages or as separate versions. Arabic reads right to left and often runs a different length from the English, so we plan both from the start. Send us the Arabic text as editable copy, and our designers can help check the layout.

Do you print small quantities for menu updates?

Yes. Short runs are common in hospitality, because dishes, prices and promotions change often. Digital printing suits small quantities and frequent changes of content, while offset printing suits larger standard runs. Timelines depend on quantity, finish and artwork readiness, and we confirm the schedule with your quote.

Where can I find door hangers and tent cards?

Both have their own pages in our marketing and promotional printing range, because they are also used outside hospitality. Hotel door hangers cover do-not-disturb, make-up-room and breakfast order formats, and tent cards cover table talkers for promotions, Wi-Fi details and reserved signs. They can be ordered together with menus and mats.
