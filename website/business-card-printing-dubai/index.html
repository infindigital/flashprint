About business card printing

Business card printing in Dubai is still one of the most practical orders a company places. Cards change hands at site visits, property viewings, exhibitions and meetings every day, and a clean, legible card is often the only record a contact keeps of who you are. This page covers our standard full-colour range, without specialist foil or embossing.

Most UAE businesses use 90 × 50 mm, which fits local cardholders and wallets. The European 85 × 55 mm size and square 55 × 55 mm cards are also popular with creative and hospitality brands. Cards are printed digitally for short runs and staggered reorders, and offset for large corporate volumes where one design is shared across many names.

For teams, we set up one master design and add each person's details as a separate version, so titles, phone numbers and emails are proofed individually. If you want metallic foil, raised effects or extra-thick board, see our gold foil business cards, which follow a different production route.

Materials

350 gsm silk

A smooth, lightly coated board that holds sharp detail and rich colour. A dependable all-rounder for most corporate cards.

400 gsm matt coated

Noticeably stiffer, with a low-glare surface that suits dark backgrounds and minimal designs. Pairs well with soft-touch lamination.

350 gsm uncoated

A natural, slightly toothy paper you can write on with any pen, useful for appointment cards. Colours print a little softer than on coated board.

Kraft board

Brown, recycled-look board for cafés, bakeries and eco-minded brands. Works best with bold, dark artwork, since light colours sink into the fibre.

Finishing options

Matt lamination

A thin film that protects the card from scuffing and fingerprints while keeping a calm, non-reflective look.

Gloss lamination

Makes photography and bright colours look more saturated, a common choice for property and product imagery.

Soft-touch lamination

A velvety film that gives a standard card a richer handle, and the natural base for spot UV highlights.

Rounded corners

Corners trimmed to a 3–6 mm radius, which resists dog-earing in wallets and gives a friendlier shape.

Spot UV

Clear gloss varnish applied only to chosen areas such as a logo or pattern, creating contrast against a matt surface.

Why choose us for business card printing

  • Every name proofed separately

    For multi-person orders we proof each version, so a wrong digit in one phone number is caught before the batch is printed.

  • Right process for the run

    We recommend digital or offset printing based on your quantity and reorder pattern, and explain the reasoning in your quote.

  • Bilingual layouts handled properly

    Arabic text is set right-to-left with correctly joined characters, and we check that both sides sit correctly after trimming.

Artwork checklist

  • Supply a PDF at final size plus 3 mm bleed on every edge
  • Keep text and logos at least 4 mm inside the trim line
  • Use text no smaller than 7 pt; fine serif fonts are safer at 8 pt and above
  • Outline or embed all fonts, especially Arabic typefaces
  • Build QR codes at 18 mm or larger with a clear white margin
  • For multiple names, send details in a spreadsheet with one row per person

Business card printing FAQs

What is the standard business card size in the UAE?

Most companies in Dubai use 90 × 50 mm, which fits standard cardholders and wallets. Some international brands keep the European 85 × 55 mm or the US 89 × 51 mm size for consistency with overseas offices. Square and slim formats are available too, but check they suit how your contacts store cards.

Should I choose 350 gsm or 400 gsm?

350 gsm is sturdy enough for everyday cards and suits large team orders. 400 gsm feels firmer and more substantial, which many clients prefer for senior staff or client-facing roles. Lamination adds a little stiffness to either weight. If you are undecided, ask for samples and compare them side by side.

Can each employee have different details on the same design?

Yes. We set up one template and create a version for each person from the names, titles and contact details you send, ideally in a spreadsheet. Each version appears on the proof, so every staff member can check their own card. Reorders for new joiners then use the same template, which keeps the team's cards consistent over time.

Can you print Arabic on one side and English on the other?

Yes, bilingual cards are very common in Dubai. We typeset the Arabic side right-to-left, using an Arabic font that matches the tone of your English typeface, and mirror the layout so logos and contact blocks sit logically on each face. If you already have an approved Arabic translation, send it as editable text rather than an image.

Can you design my business card?

Yes. Our design team can create a card from your logo and brand colours, adapt an existing layout, or tidy up a file that is not print-ready. You will see a digital proof of the design and each name before production, and design is quoted separately from printing.
