About die-cut sticker printing

Die-cut sticker printing turns a flat design into an object people keep. The cutter follows a path drawn around your artwork, usually with a slim white or coloured border, so the finished sticker takes the shape of the logo, character or icon itself. That border does real work: it hides tiny shifts between print and cut, and it gives the sticker a clean edge that is easier to peel.

Two cut styles are common. A true die-cut goes through both the vinyl and its backing paper, producing individual stickers ready to hand out at events or drop into online orders. A kiss-cut goes through the top layer only, so one or several shapes sit on a larger backing card that can carry your branding, instructions or a product code. Sticker sheets with multiple designs are always a kiss-cut job.

Most die-cut stickers for Dubai brands are printed on white vinyl with a laminate, which copes with water bottles, laptops and cool boxes. Paper suits packaging seals and short-term giveaways, and clear vinyl works where the background should show through around the design.

Materials

White vinyl

The default for durable stickers: flexible, water-resistant and bright under full-colour print. Suits bottles, laptops, notebooks and cool boxes.

Clear vinyl

Lets the surface show through around the design, giving a printed-on look on glass, clear packaging and light-coloured products.

Paper

Semi-gloss or kraft paper for indoor seals and giveaways that will never meet water, with a surface that is easy to write on.

Holographic or metallic film

A rainbow or chrome face for merchandise and limited editions, available on request, with white ink controlling which areas shine.

Finishing options

Gloss laminate

Deepens colours and wipes clean, the usual choice for merchandise and giveaway stickers.

Matte laminate

A flat, glare-free surface that looks more understated on laptops, notebooks and packaging.

Soft-touch laminate

A velvety feel that suits premium gift seals and stickers packed with luxury orders.

Where it’s used

  • Event and exhibition giveaways
  • Laptop, water bottle and notebook stickers
  • Box and bag seals for boutiques and bakeries
  • Kiss-cut sticker sheets for schools and children's activities
  • Merchandise and fan packs

Why choose us for die-cut sticker printing

  • Cut lines set up for you

    Send a logo and we draw the contour and border on the proof, so you see exactly where the blade will run before anything is cut.

  • Fine detail checked

    We flag thin strokes, sharp inside corners and loose elements that would tear or fall away, and suggest simple fixes before production.

  • Singles or branded sheets

    The same artwork can be supplied as loose die-cut pieces or as kiss-cut sheets on a printed backing card.

Artwork checklist

  • Supply vector artwork (AI, EPS or PDF) so the cut path can follow clean curves
  • Put the cut line on its own named layer or spot colour, set to overprint
  • Extend the background 2 mm beyond the cut line so no white edge shows
  • Keep text and fine details at least 2 mm inside the cut
  • Avoid gaps and spikes narrower than about 1 mm, which the blade cannot follow cleanly
  • For clear or holographic stickers, supply the white ink as a separate layer

Die-cut sticker printing FAQs

What is the difference between die-cut and kiss-cut stickers?

A die-cut sticker is cut through the sticker and its backing, so each one is a separate piece shaped like your design. A kiss-cut sticker is cut through the sticker layer only, leaving it on a larger backing sheet or card. Die-cut suits handouts and order inserts; kiss-cut suits sticker sheets, multi-design sets and pieces with printed instructions on the backing.

Can you make die-cut stickers from a JPEG or photo of our logo?

Often, yes. A clean, high-resolution image can be traced to create a cut path, and our design team can redraw simple logos as vector artwork if needed. Busy photographs are usually given a smooth outline or a rounded border rather than following every edge. We show the proposed cut line on the proof so you can approve the shape first.

Are die-cut vinyl stickers water-resistant?

Laminated vinyl stickers resist rain, spills and regular wiping, and they handle hand-washing on bottles and lunch boxes. Repeated dishwasher cycles will eventually lift the edges of most stickers, so we would not recommend them for that. Paper die-cut stickers are for dry, indoor use only, even with a laminate on top.

Can I order several sticker designs in one run?

Yes. Multiple designs can be printed together, either as separate die-cut pieces or grouped on kiss-cut sheets. Let us know how many of each design you need and whether they should be packed separately. We confirm the split, materials and finish in your quote, and each design appears on the proof for approval.
