About reflective signage

Ordinary printed signs rely on ambient light. Retro-reflective sheeting contains glass beads or micro-prisms that return light towards its source, so a sign lit by headlights or a torch appears bright to the person behind that light. That is why reflective signage in Dubai's basement car parks, service roads, loading docks and night-shift yards is far easier to read than a standard print.

We produce reflective speed-limit, height-clearance, no-parking, stop, pedestrian-crossing, direction and hazard chevron signs for private premises, plus reflective panels for bollards, barriers and racking ends. Graphics are either printed onto white reflective sheeting with compatible inks and a clear overlaminate, or cut from coloured reflective film and layered, which suits bold lettering, arrows and chevrons.

Signs on public roads follow the specification set by the road authority and the project's traffic consultant, and for those jobs we work strictly from the drawings and material schedule supplied. Inside private compounds your facility manager or HSE lead sets the layout, and we help choose a sheeting grade that suits the viewing distance.

Materials

Engineering-grade sheeting

Glass-bead sheeting suited to low-speed areas such as car parks, private driveways and warehouse yards, where signs are read from shorter distances.

High-intensity prismatic sheeting

Micro-prismatic sheeting returns more light than bead types and is widely specified for site roads, gate lanes and longer approaches.

Aluminium sheet (2–3 mm)

A rigid, rust-free backing that takes post clamps and holds its shape in outdoor heat. The usual base for pole-mounted signs.

Aluminium composite panel

Lighter than solid aluminium for large wall-mounted signs, and a flat base for reflective films in covered car parks.

Finishing options

Clear protective overlaminate

Shields printed reflective graphics from abrasion and UV exposure; the laminate is chosen to suit the sheeting so reflectivity is kept.

Edge sealing

Sealing the cut edges of sheeting helps limit moisture and dust creeping under the film in exposed spots.

Rear channel

Aluminium channel bonded to the back so signs clamp to posts without drilling through the face.

Reflective signage FAQs

What is the difference between reflective and glow-in-the-dark signs?

Reflective sheeting only shines when a light, such as a headlight or torch, is pointed at it, and it bounces that light back to the viewer. Photoluminescent material stores light and glows on its own in darkness. Reflective suits vehicle routes and car parks; photoluminescent is typically chosen for exit and fire-equipment signs inside buildings.

Which reflective grade should we choose?

Engineering-grade sheeting is common for car parks and low-speed private areas. High-intensity prismatic sheeting returns more light and is often chosen for site roads, entrances and longer viewing distances. If a consultant or authority has specified a grade, that specification takes priority. Otherwise, tell us where the sign will be and we will suggest options in the quote.

Can you print full-colour logos on reflective signs?

Yes. Full-colour artwork can be printed onto white reflective sheeting and protected with a compatible clear laminate. Colours look slightly different on reflective film than on paper or plain vinyl, so for brand-critical colours we recommend checking a physical sample before a large order goes ahead.

Do you make reflective signs for public roads?

Signs for public roads are specified by the road authority and the project's traffic consultant, including sizes, symbols, fonts and sheeting. We can produce to that specification when it is supplied with the order. We do not design road signage schemes or obtain permissions for them; that stays with the consultant and contractor responsible.

Can reflective signs be fixed to existing posts?

Usually, yes. Signs can be supplied with an aluminium channel on the back for post clamps, pre-drilled for through-bolts, or as self-adhesive faces that cover an old sign. Tell us the post diameter and whether it is round or square, and we will match the clamps to suit.
